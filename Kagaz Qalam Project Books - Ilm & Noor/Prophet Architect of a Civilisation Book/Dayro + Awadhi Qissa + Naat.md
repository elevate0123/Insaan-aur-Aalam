**Naat-e-A‘la: Khutba-e-Wida‘ (ba-beher-e-ramal, ba-radif: “sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam”, ba-takhallus: Hamza)**

**Matla‘**

**“Ayyuhan-naas, isma‘u qawli”** — nida-e-sirr-e-do ‘aalam,  
Dasht-e-Arafat mein jhalka noor-e-azam, faiz-e-mujassam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“Inna dimaa’akum wa amwaalakum haraamun ‘alaykum”** — hukm-e-haq-e-muhkam,  
Khoon-o-maal ki hurmat se qaim hua ‘adl ka haram, qanoon-e-mukarram, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“La fadla li ‘arabiyyin ‘ala ‘ajamiyyin illa bit-taqwa”** — fasl-e-noor ka parcham,  
Nasb-o-rang ke but shekast, taqwa bana sirr-e-a‘zam, darja-e-mu‘azzam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“Fa-ittaqullaha fin-nisaa”** — lehja-e-lutf-e-paiham,  
Haqq-e-niswaan ko mila rutba-e-‘izzat-e-da’im, karam bar karam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“Wa istausoo bin-nisaa’i khayran”** — husn-e-sulook ka aalam,  
Rehmat ki saba se mehka har rishta-e-jaan-o-janan, gulshan ba-gulshan, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“Taraktu feekum ma in tamassaktum bihi lan tadillu”** — rehnuma-e-har dam,  
Qur’an ka noor-e-mubeen bana rasta-e-subh-o-shaam, zulmat shikan dam-ba-dam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“Ala hal ballaghtu?”** — nida-e-shafqat ba-andaz-e-ikram,  
“Na‘am, ballaghta!” ki sada se hua itmam-e-deen ka nizaam, faiz-e-mudam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**“Allahumma fashhad”** — du‘a ba-janib-e-‘arsh-e-a‘zam,  
Lauh-e-qadar par naqsh hua yeh paighaam-e-kamil, noor ka marqam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

Noor se noor tak is khutba ka silsila, sirr se zahir ka aalam,  
Zulmat ki shab mein bhi chamka tera paighaam-e-rahmat, subh-e-mujassam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

**Maqta‘**

Hamza ke qalam ko ata ho woh zauq-e-sana, woh ishq-e-mukarram,  
Ya Muhammad, nazar-e-lutf se kar de use bhi ahl-e-haram, sahib-e-karam, sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam.

# 🌙 **Indo-Islamic Mehfil System (Full Performance Design)**

### Theme: Khutbat al-Wida‘ (Arafat Sermon Reconstruction)

---

# 🧭 1. PERFORMANCE ROLES

You need **three voice types**:

## 🎙️ 1. Narrator (Awadhi Kissa Voice)

- calm, storytelling tone
    
- slightly rhythmic speech
    
- carries the “story world”
    

## 🕊️ 2. Malfuzat Voice (Sufi Teacher Tone)

- slower
    
- reflective
    
- philosophical explanation
    

## 🌿 3. Naat Voice (Devotional Peak Voice)

- emotionally elevated
    
- short bursts only
    
- used sparingly for impact
    

---

# ⏱️ 2. TIMING STRUCTURE (60-minute mehfil model)

---

## 🕯️ SEGMENT 1 (0–10 min): INVOCATION + SETTING

### Flow:

- soft naat opening
    
- malfuzat framing
    
- transition into kissa world
    

### Sample:

**Naat Voice (soft):**  
Ya Muhammad…  
sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam…

**Malfuzat Voice:**  
“Yeh sirf khutba nahi tha… yeh insaaniyat ka aakhri ailan tha…”

**Narrator (Awadhi tone):**  
“Tab ka zamana aisa tha… jab log ek doosre se door ho chuke the…”

---

## 📜 SEGMENT 2 (10–25 min): KISSA BUILD-UP (PRE-KHUTBA WORLD)

### Purpose:

Establish moral darkness → need for revelation

Narrator:

- describes jahili context
    
- social breakdown
    
- human conflict
    

Malfuzat inserts:

- ethical reflection every 2–3 minutes
    

No naat yet (build tension)

---

## 🌿 SEGMENT 3 (25–35 min): KHUTBA ENTRY

### Transition moment:

Narrator slows down:  
“Phir Arafat ka din aaya…”

**(pause 5–7 seconds)**

Malfuzat:  
“Wahan jo kaha gaya… woh waqt se bahar tha…”

---

## 🌙 SEGMENT 4 (35–45 min): NAAT PEAK ZONES

### Only short bursts allowed:

**Naat Voice:**  
Ya Nabi…  
insaaniyat ko aap ne jaga diya…  
sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam…

Narrator resumes immediately after (no applause break)

---

## 📜 SEGMENT 5 (45–52 min): KHUTBA CORE (ETHICAL TRANSMISSION)

Narrator delivers key Khutba ideas:

- equality of mankind
    
- prohibition of injustice
    
- rights of women
    
- trust and morality
    

Malfuzat reinforces meaning:

“Yeh hukum sirf sunane ke liye nahi tha…”

---

## 🔔 SEGMENT 6 (52–57 min): CHORUS MOMENT (ONLY FIXED PARTICIPATION)

All voices pause narration.

### Fixed chorus ONLY:

**All participants:**  
“Hum gawah hain…”  
“Hum gawah hain…”

“Paighaam pohcha diya gaya…”

(No improvisation beyond this)

---

## 🌿 SEGMENT 7 (57–60 min): CLOSURE + DISSOLUTION

Malfuzat tone:

“Jab haq bol diya jaata hai…”

“to waqt ruk jaata hai…”

Narrator soft:

“Arafat ki woh baat… aaj bhi zinda hai…”

---

## 🌙 Final Naat echo (fade-out)

Ya Muhammad…  
sallallahu ‘alayhi wa sallam…

- audience only speaks in CHORUS SEGMENT
    
- prevents dilution of narrative control
    
- maintains dramatic authority

They never overlap simultaneously.
