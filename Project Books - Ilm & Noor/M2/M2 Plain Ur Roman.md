# Meesaq-e-Sakinah
**(The Covenant of Tranquility)**

## Aaghaz se pehle Qariyeen ke liye ek Lafz
**(A Word to the Reader Before You Begin)**

Shuru Allah ke naam se, jo bada Meherbaan, nihayat Reham wala hai.

Is safar ke aaghaz se pehle, mujhe aapse, yani qari se, ek aitraaf karna hai. Aapke haathon mein jo kitab hai, woh ek **"Ilahi Nasb-ul-Ain"** ki tafseel hai. Yeh us roshan choti ka naqsha hai, woh Qibla hai jis ki taraf rukh karne aur jad-o-jehad karne ka humein hukum diya gaya hai. Taham, yeh meri apni shadi shuda zindagi ki mukammal tasveer nahi hai, aur na hi is roo-e-zameen par kisi aur ki shadi ki, siwaye Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ki mubarak zindagi ke. Main bhi aap ki tarah is raaste ka ek pur-khata aur koshish karne wala musafir hoon, in usoolon ka ek talib-ilm jo kuch din kamiyab hota hai aur kuch din nakaam. Yeh kitab maharat ya kamaal ke maqam se nahi, balki ek gehri aur aksar takleef-deh tamanna ke maqam se wajood mein aayi hai.

Is liye, aap in safhaat ko padhenge aur, Allah ke fazl se, aap ko tehreek (inspiration) milegi. Lekin yeh jaan lein: yeh jazbati kaifiyat madham pad jayegi. Aap in usoolon ko nafiz karne ki koshish karenge aur, baaz auqaat, aap nakaam bhi honge. Yeh munafiqat ki alamat nahi hai; yeh insaan hone ki alamat hai jo **"Jihad-e-Nafs"** (roohani jad-o-jehad ki aala tareen shakal) mein masroof hai. Is kitab ka maqsad farishton jaisi mustaqil pakeezgi hasil karna nahi hai. Iska maqsad is raaste par baar baar, khuloos aur umeed ke saath palatne ka muqaddas fun seekhna hai. Is zindagi mein kamiyabi kabhi na girne mein nahi, balki Allah ki khatir, ek doosre ke saath mil kar dobara uth khade hone mein hai.

Lehaza, main is kitab ka ilm aap ko bataur **"Al-Amanah"** — ek muqaddas amanat — sonp raha hoon. Aur main aapse, us haq ke wasty jo ek momin ka doosre par hai, darkhwast karta hoon ke iski do sharaa'it qabool karein.

**Pehli Shart:** Aap ko is kitab ko naqshay ke taur par istemaal karne se pehle **Aainay** ke taur par istemaal karna hoga. Apne shareek-e-hayat ki tashkhees (diagnose) karne se pehle, in safhaat mein apna aks, apni khamiyan, aur apni islah ke mawaqay talash karein. Ek behtar Ahad (Covenant) ka safar hamesha, bila-istisna, apni zaat se shuru hota hai.

**Doosri Shart:** Aap is kitab ki sachaiyon ko kabhi bhi apne shareek-e-hayat ko zakhmi karne ke liye **Talwar** ke taur par istemaal nahi karenge. Yeh usool aapki apni rooh ke liye marham hain, behes jeetne ke liye hathiyar nahi. Agar aapne is muqaddas ilm ko apne shareek-e-hayat ko ghalat saabit karne, jhagday mein bartari hasil karne, ya apni kotahiyon ka jawaz pesh karne ke liye istemaal kiya, to aap is kitab ke sabse bunyadi imtehaan mein nakaam ho jayenge aur hamare darmiyan is aitemaad ko thes pohanchayenge.

Agar aap in sharaa'it ko qabool kar sakte hain, to aage badhein. Aur meri dua hai ke Allah, jo **"Al-Fattah"** (Kholne Wala) hai, aapke dil ko apne **Khudayi Manhaj** ki khubsurti ke liye khol de aur is kaam ko aapke ghar mein gehri aur der-pa khair ka zariya banaye.

### **Tamheed: Do Samundaron ka Milaap**
**(Prologue: The Meeting of the Seas)**

Meesaq (Nikkah) se pehle, rooh ek tanha samundar ki manind hoti hai.

Yeh bazat-e-khud ek kainaat hai, ek gehra aur athaa samundar jiski gehraiyon mein kayi duniyayein abad hain. Iski apni tareekh hai, jo yaadon ki badalti rait par likhi gayi hai. Iski umeed ki apni lehrein hain aur khauf ke apne bhanwar. Iske sukoon ke apne mausam hain aur Nafs ke achanak, shadeed toofan. Yeh ek mukammal aur pecheeda haqeeqat hai, jo dil ki posheeda zindagi se bhari hui hai aur Fitrah ki door-daraz, aasmani roshni se munawwar hai.

Aur phir, Rehman ke hukum se, ek zabardast waqia ronuma hota hai. Ek aur samundar, jo utna hi wasee, utna hi gehra, aur apni tareekh aur toofano se utna hi bhara hua hai, iske sahil par laya jata hai. Yeh Meesaq ka lamha hai, woh muqaddas milaap jis ka zikr Rabb-e-Zuljalal ne yun farmaya:

> *Marajal bahrayni yaltaqiyaan* (19)
> **"Usne do darya baha diye ke woh baham mil jayen."**
> (Al-Quran, 55:19)

Kyunke do azeem samundaron ka milaap kabhi bhi narm-o-nazuk nahi hota. Yeh shadeed dabao ka ilaqah hai, mitti aur yaadon ka imtizaj, lehron aur iradon ka takrao. Yeh toofan nakami ki alamat nahi hai. Yeh ek taqatwar, tabdeeli lane wale waqiye ki alamat hai jo jari hai.

Kyunke Khaliq-e-Kainaat ne, apni kamil hikmat mein, is milaap ko bahami tabahi ke liye design nahi kiya. Usne iske andar ek muqaddas, control karne wala usool rakha hai:

> *Baynahuma barzakhun la yabghiyaan* (20)
> **"Un dono ke darmiyan ek aad (Barzakh) hai, woh apni hadd se nahi badhte."**
> (Al-Quran, 55:20)

Yeh **"Al-Barzakh"** hai. Yeh koi deewar nahi jo taqseem karti ho, balki ek bareek, mojizati parda hai. Yeh muqaddas hudood ka qanoon hai, ek doosre ki alag shanakht ka bahami ehtram, jo do roohon ko unke milaap ke ibtedayi toofan mein ek doosre ko mita dene se rokta hai. Yeh woh Khudayi usool hai jo ibtedayi, shadeed takrao ko ek takhleeqi, zindagi-bakhsh raqs mein badal deta hai.

Sirf isi raqs se — milaap ke shadeed dabao se, jo Barzakh ke muqaddas ehtram se mehfooz ho — is rishtey ka asal maqsad hasil kiya ja sakta hai. Kyunke Rabb-e-Zuljalal farmata hai:

> *Yakhruju minhumal lu'lu'u wal marjaan* (22)
> **"Un dono se moti aur mongay nikalte hain."**
> (Al-Quran, 55:22)

Yeh qeemti jawahiraat tanha samundar mein nahi milte. Yeh sirf is azeem, pur-shor, aur Khudayi intezam ke tehat hone wale milaap se janam lete hain. Yeh Meesaq ke samraat (fruits) hain: Woh **"Sakinah"** jo dil ko qaraar bakhshti hai, woh nek aulad jo aankhon ki thandak banti hai, woh mushtarqa aamaal jo tarazu mein bhari hote hain, woh sharakat jo rooh ko wapas uske Khaliq ki taraf le jati hai. Yeh jawahiraat shadeed dabao aur muqaddas hadd-bandi ke *bawajood* nahi, balki unki *wajah se* tashkeel paate hain.

Yeh kitab samundaron ko pur-sukoon karne ke bare mein nahi hai; yeh kaam sirf Allah ka hai. Yeh kitab is muqaddas **"Bahr ki Shinawari"** ke fun ko samajhne ke bare mein hai. Yeh hangama-khezi mein safar karne, "Barzakh" ka ehtram karne, aur un motiyon ko talash karne ka naqsha aur qutub-numa hai jo gehraayi mein muntazir hain.

## Pehla Darwaza: Ilahi Meesaq (Al-Mithaq) - Bunyad
**(Gate I: The Divine Covenant - The Foundation)**

### **Baab 1: Muahiday se Meesaq tak**
**(Chapter 1: From Contract to Covenant)**

#### **[Juz] Bunyadi Usool (Al-Asl)**
**([Component] The Anchor)**

**Maakhuz:** Quran, Surah An-Nisa, 4:19

**Arabi Matn:**
> ...وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ ۚ فَإِن كَرِهْتُمُوهُنَّ فَعَسَىٰ أَن تَكْرَهُوا شَيْئًا وَيَجْعَلَ اللَّهُ فِيهِ خَيْرًا كَثِيرًا

**Tarjuma:**

#### **[Juz] Wazahat (Al-Bayan)**
**([Component] The Illumination)**

Jadeed Muslim shadiyon ke dil mein ek khamosh bimari ghar kar gayi hai, ek dheema zeher jo is Meesaq ki roohani zindagi ko nichod raha hai. Yeh **"Kat-hairay ki Zehniyat" (Courtroom Mindset)** ka apnana hai.

> Is zehreeli "Shikayaat ki Adalat" aur zaroori o sehat-mand **"Ma'roof Mushawarat"** mein farq karna lazmi hai. Shadi duniya ki pecheedgiyon mein ek mushtarqa safar hai, aur iske liye musalsal mushawarat ki zaroorat hoti hai. Zarooriyat par baat karna, zindagi ke bojh ko baantna, aur apne saathi se madad mangna — *"Main thak gaya hoon, kya aap aaj raat bacchon ko sambhal sakte hain?"* — yeh kisi bimar Meesaq ki alamat nahi hai. Yeh do roohon ka mamool aur zaroori kaam hai jo insaaf aur reham ke saath apne faraiz poora karne ki koshish kar rahi hain. Bimari tab shuru hoti hai jab mushawarat mustaqbil ki hum-aahangi (harmony) ke bare mein nahi, balki maazi ki shikayaat ke bare mein ho; jab sikka bahami ta'awun ka nahi, balki puranay scores ke kadwahat bharay hisaab kitaab ka ho.

Is Manhaj (Methodology) mein, ghar ek panah-gaah nahi, balki shikayaat ke muqadmaat ka stage ban jata hai. Shareek-e-hayat ek saathi nahi, balki **"Khasm Yawm al-Qiyamah"** (Qiyamat ke din ka muddai/hareef) ban jata hai. Har kotahi gunahon ke ek kadwahat bharay register (*Diwan-uz-Zunoob*) mein darj hoti hai, aur har ikhtilaf ek **Jirah** (cross-examination) ban jata hai. Yeh haqooq aur faraiz ka sard hisaab kitaab hai, ek aisi shadi jiski dhadkan mohabbat nahi, balki ek talkh aur thaka dene wali accounting hai.

**Khudayi Manhaj** iska ilaaj pesh karta hai: **"Baaghbaan ki Zehniyat" (Orchard Cultivator Mindset)**. Ek baagh muqadma-baazi ki jagah nahi, balki kaashtkari ki jagah hai. Yeh phal ka mutalba karne ke bare mein nahi, balki mitti ko zarkhez banane, Nafs ki jari-bootiyon ko ukhadne, aur yaqeen rakhne ke bare mein hai ke ek khubsurat fasal tayyar hogi.

Woh kya cheez hai jo insaan ko is talkh adalat se nikal kar zindagi-bakhsh baagh mein dakhil hone ke qabil banati hai? Yeh halaat ki tabdeeli nahi, balki dil ki tabdeeli hai. Yeh Islami riwayat ke ek intehayi khubsurat aur taqatwar tasawwur ko apnana hai: **"Al-Fadl"**.

**"Fadl"** mehez khush-akhlaqi nahi hai. Yeh razakarana, be-los sakhawat ki ek fa'aal (active) halat hai. Yeh woh jazba hai jo mehez insaaf ke mayaar se upar uth kar kaam karta hai. Insaaf (**Adl**) kehta hai, *"Yeh tumhara haq hai."* Fadl kehta hai, *"Main tumhein tumhare haq se zyada doonga, aur iske badle mein Allah ki raza ke siwa kuch nahi chahoonga."* Insaaf farsh hai; Fadl woh buland chatt hai. Woh shadi jo sirf insaaf ke sard farsh par zinda rehti hai, woh roohani gurbat ki halat mein hai.

Yeh Fadl ka usool mehez achay akhlaq ka mashwara nahi; yeh us dil ka amli saboot hai jo haqeeqi, Khuda-khaufi par mabni mohabbat ki taraf mail hai. Dil ke mahir tabib, Imam Ibn al-Qayyim ne rooh ki do azeem mohabbaton mein farq kiya hai: Nichli satah ki mohabbat yani **"Ishq"**, jo lena chahta hai, aur Allah ki khatir aala mohabbat yani **"Mahabbah"**, jo dena chahti hai. "Kat-hairay ki Zehniyat" us shadi ka nateeja hai jo "Ishq" par mabni ho; jab jazba madham padta hai to sirf haqooq ka hisaab baqi reh jata hai. Lekin jo shadi "Mahabbah" par ustawar ho, woh qudrati taur par "Fadl" mein tabdeel ho jati hai, kyunke iska maqsad shareek-e-hayat se lena nahi, balki asal Mehboob, Allah ﷻ ki khatir dena hai. Fadl ke saath jeena is baat ka saboot hai ke yeh Meesaq ek duniyawi len-den nahi balki ibadat hai.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Meesaq ko andar se mazboot karne ke farz ko samajhne ke liye, humein pehle un sakht hudood ko dekhna hoga jo Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne isay bahar se mehfooz rakhne ke liye qaim ki. Imam Abu Dawud apni *Sunan* mein, Kitab al-Talaq mein, ek riwayat naqal karte hain jo har Muslim shadi ke taqaddus ki muhafiz hai.

**Maakhuz:** Sunan Abi Dawud. (Hasan darja)

**Arabi Matn:**
> لَيْسَ مِنَّا مَنْ خَبَّبَ امْرَأَةً عَلَى زَوْجِهَا

**Tarjuma:**
> "Woh hum mein se nahi jo kisi aurat ko uske shohar ke khilaf wargalaye (ya kharab kare)."

**Tashreeh o Sayaq:**
Lafz **"Khabbaba"** bad-niyati par mabni dhoka-dahi aur fasaad ki nishandahi karta hai. **"Takhbib"** kisi teesre fareeq ka woh amal hai jo miyan biwi ke kaan mein zeher gholta hai — unke saathi ki khamiyon ko ujagar karna, shikayaat ko barha charha kar pesh karna, aur ta'aluqaat ko kharab karne ki niyat se fitna phelana. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ka yeh elaan ke *"Woh hum mein se nahi"* (Laysa minna), Hadees ke zakheere mein sakht tareen muzammaton mein se ek hai, jo un gunahon ke liye makhsoos hai jo Muslim mashray ke taanay-baanay ko tod dete hain.

**Tajziya:**
Yeh Hadees mehez aadab ka koi mamooli nukta nahi; yeh izdawaji bandhan ki noiyat ke bare mein ek gehra Ilahiati bayan hai. Aakhir "Takhbib" ka jurm kya hai? Yeh koi jismani hamla ya maal ki chori nahi hai. Yeh dil par hamla hai. Yeh us **"Mawaddah"** (Mohabbat) aur **"Sakinah"** (Sukoon) ko tabah karne ki danista koshish hai jo Allah ﷻ ne khud miyan biwi ke *"darmiyan rakhi"* hai. Takhbib ka murtakib Allah ki ek Nishani (Ayat) ko mitane ki koshish kar raha hai. Isi liye yeh gunah itna sangeen hai.

Yeh Nabvi Namuna "Al-Fadl" ke usool ke liye hatmi jawaz faraham karta hai. Agar shadi ke andar mohabbat aur wafadari par *beruni* hamla itna sangeen gunah hai ke yeh insaan ko Nabi ﷺ ki Ummat se kharij karne ka khatra paida kar sakta hai, to phir *androoni* hamlay ki sangeeni ka kya aalam hoga?

Kadwahat ka har amal, sard-mehri se bola gaya har lafz, dil mein paali gayi har shikayat, score-keeping ka har lamha — yeh sab **"Androoni Takhbib"** ki shaklein hain. Yeh woh amal hai jis mein shareek-e-hayat khud is bandhan ke khilaf fasaad ka agent ban jata hai jiski hifazat ki usne qasam khayi thi; yeh andar se baagh ko ahista ahista zeher dene ke mutradif hai.

Lehaza, Fadl ki mashq — insaaf ke bajaye sakhawat ka intekhab, sakhti ke bajaye narmi, badla lene ke bajaye maaf karna — koi jazbati neki ya ikhtiari **roohani aasaish** nahi hai. Yeh Meesaq ke androoni khatmay ke khilaf bunyadi difaa hai.

#### **[Juz] Uboori Pul (The Transitional Bridge)**

Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne na sirf Meesaq qaim kiya, balki iska muqaddas dhancha bhi tameer kiya. Woh azeem Aimma (Imams) jinhone is dhanchay ko warsay mein paya, woh mehez makeen nahi thay. Woh iske pehle aur sabse mukhlis muhafiz thay, jinka kaam iski bunyadon ki paimaish karna aur is bhari bojh ko bayan karna tha jis ke liye isay tameer kiya gaya tha. Lehaza, unke alfaaz mehez tasalli ke liye nahi hain; woh woh paimanay hain jo is Khudayi idaray ki haqeeqi, atal amoodiyat (verticality) aur is aitemaad ki chaunka dene wali sangeeni ko zahir karte hain.

#### **[Juz] Wirsa (Al-Turath)**
**([Component] The Heritage)**

Misaal ke taur par, azeem Damishqi aalim, **Ibn al-Qayyim al-Jawziyya**, ne Meesaq ko qanoon ki nazar se nahi, balki rooh ke maqsad ki nazar se bayan kiya. Unke nazdeek, shadi ka hatmi maqsad ibadat mein sharakat-dari thi, Allah ki taraf ek mushtarqa safar jo dono sathiyon ke imaan ko mukammal aur khubsurat banata hai.

Agar Ibn al-Qayyim ne Meesaq ke aala maqsad ko bayan kiya, to Hujjat-ul-Islam, **Imam Abu Hamid al-Ghazali**, ne iski sangeeni ko aisi fikri quwwat ke saath bayan kiya jo rooh ko jhanjhod deti hai. Unke alfaaz ka intekhab jaan boojh kar aisa hai jo jadeed kaanon ke liye chaunka dene wala ho.

> **(Muhafiz ka Note:** Azeem asatiza, rooh ko ghaflat ki neend se bedaar karne ki khwahish mein, kabhi kabhi aisi zaban istemaal karte thay jo jaan boojh kar sakht hoti thi. Isay qanooni tafseel ke taur par nahi, balki **Roohani Bedari ka Jhatka** (Shock treatment) ke taur par samajhna chahiye.)

**Namuna:**

**Maakhuz:** Al-Ghazali, Abu Hamid. *Ihya' 'Ulum al-Din*, Jild 2, *Kitab Adab al-Nikah*.

**Arabi Matn:**
> النِّكَاحُ نَوْعٌ مِنَ الرِّقِّ، فَلْتَنْظُرْ أَحَدُكُمْ عِنْدَ مَنْ يُرِقُّ كَرِيمَتَهُ

**Tarjuma:**
> "Nikah ghulami (Riqq) ki ek qism hai. Lehaza, tum mein se har ek ko gaur karna chahiye ke woh apni ba-izzat beti ko kis ke paas **ghulami** mein de raha hai."

**Tajziya:**
Yahan lafz "Riqq" ka istemaal jadeed azadi ke tanazur mein nahi, balki **"Al-Amanah"** (Muqaddas Amanat) ke Qurani tanazur mein samajhna chahiye. Yeh woh amanat hai jo itni badi thi ke aasmano, zameen aur paharon ne isay uthane se inkaar kar diya, lekin insaan ne isay qabool kar liya.

Jab Imam Ghazali khud-mukhtari ke khatmay ke liye intehayi sakht duniyawi istiaara (metaphor) istemaal karte hain, to woh us intehayi sakht roohani zimmedari ko bayan kar rahe hote hain jo ek insaan utha sakta hai: doosri rooh ke liye "Amanat". Yeh shohar ke liye Aaqa banne ka license nahi, balki Allah ke samne bataur Trustee (Ameen) uske ehtisaab ki ek khaufnaak tanbeeh hai. Is bayan ka markaz biwi ki haisiyat nahi, balki shohar ki achanak aur khaufnaak zimmedari hai. Usne biwi ki roohani, jazbati aur jismani falah-o-behbood ki amanat qabool ki hai. Ghazali ka maqsad shadi ke bare mein sathi, romanvi nazriye ko todna aur isay intehayi sangeen nataij ke hamil Ahad ke taur par dobara tameer karna hai, ek aisa muqaddas bojh jis ka hisaab Roz-e-Qiyamat dena hoga.

Agar Imam Ghazali ne Meesaq ki amoodi sangeeni (Allah ke samne iska wazan) ki tareef ki, to Barr-e-Sagheer ke azeem Mujaddid, **Shah Waliullah Dehlawi**, ne iski ufuqi (horizontal) ahmiyat bayan ki: Insani tehzeeb ki bunyad ke taur par iska kirdaar.

**Maakhuz:** Shah Waliullah Dehlawi. *Hujjatullah al-Baligha*, Jild 2.

**Arabi Matn:**
> وَاعْلَمْ أَنَّ النِّكَاحَ أَيْضًا مِنْ جُمْلَةِ الْأُسُسِ الَّتِي لَا يَنْتَظِمُ التَّمَدُّنُ الْإِنْسَانِيُّ إِلَّا بِهَا...

**Tarjuma:**
> "Aur jaan lo ke Nikah bhi un bunyadon (Usus) mein se hai jin ke baghair Insani Tehzeeb (Tamaddun-e-Insani) ka nizam durust nahi ho sakta..."

**Tajziya:**
Shah Waliullah ke nazdeek, Meesaq mehez ek niji roohani raasta nahi hai; yeh aala tareen awami bhalayi hai, ek bunyadi sutoon (Asaas) jis ke baghair mashra khud afra-tafri ka shikaar ho jata hai. Lehaza Classici Asatiza ka vision mukammal hai: Meesaq ba-yak-waqt Aakhirat mein nataij ke saath ek gehri zaati "Amanat" hai, aur woh bunyadi sutoon hai jis par ek saleh mashra tameer hota hai. Isay mehez ek contract samajhna na sirf ek ghalti hai; yeh gehri roohani aur tehzeebi andhay-pan ka amal hai.

#### **[Juz] Mohar (Al-Khatm)**
**([Component] The Seal)**

**Manhaj ki Tabdeeli (The Paradigm Shift):**

*   **Se:** Poora kiye jane wala duniyawi Muahida (Contract).
*   **Tak:** Izzat kiye jane wala muqaddas Meesaq (Covenant).

*   **Se:** Haqooq ka mutalba karne wali "Kat-hairay ki Zehniyat".
*   **Tak:** Reham ke saath kaashtkari karne wali "Baaghbaan ki Zehniyat".

*   **Se:** Insaaf (**Adl**) ka talkh hisaab kitaab.
*   **Tak:** Fazl (**Al-Fadl**) ki be-los sakhawat.

*   **Se:** Ek niji maamla.
*   **Tak:** Ek muqaddas Amanat aur Tehzeeb ka sutoon.

**Do Duayein:**

Muahiday se Meesaq ki taraf yeh tabdeeli mehez hikmat-e-amli mein tabdeeli nahi hai; yeh us "Nafs" ke khilaf elaan-e-jung hai jo humein khud-garz banne ka hukum deta hai. Yeh roohani tazkiya ka amal hai. Lehaza, is baab par mohar lagane ke liye sabse mauzoon dua Nabi Kareem ﷺ ki apni dua hai jo Aap ﷺ apne Nafs ke tazkiya ke liye manga karte thay:

*   **Nabvi Dua (Pakeeza Dil ke liye):**
    > *Allahumma aati nafsi taqwaha wa zakkiha anta khayru man zakkaha anta waliyyuha wa mawlaha.*
    > **"Aye Allah, mere nafs ko uska taqwa ata farma aur isay paak kar de, Tu hi isay behtareen paak karne wala hai. Tu hi iska Wali aur iska Maula hai."** (Sahih Muslim)

*   **Sada Zaban mein Dua (Meesaq ke liye):**
    > "Aye Allah, **Al-Fattah** (Kholne Wale), hamare dilon ko apne Khudayi Manhaj ki khubsurti ke liye khol de. Humein adalat ki qaid se azad kar aur baagh ki kaashtkari ki khushi ata farma. Humein hamare Nafs ke waswason se bacha jo humein score rakhne ka hukum dete hain, aur humein sirf Teri khatir 'Fadl' ke saath jeene ki taufiq ata farma. Hamari shadi ko is duniya ke muahiday se badal kar ek muqaddas Meesaq bana de jo humein Teri taraf le jaye. Ameen."

### **Baab 2: Meesaq ki Hudood**
**(Chapter 2: The Boundaries of the Covenant)**

#### **[Juz] Bunyadi Usool (Al-Asl)**
**([Component] The Anchor)**

**Maakhuz:** Quran, Surah An-Nisa, 4:135

**Arabi Matn:**
> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُونُوا قَوَّامِينَ بِالْقِسْطِ شُهَدَاءَ لِلَّهِ وَلَوْ عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَوِ الْوَالِدَيْنِ وَالْأَقْرَبِينَ...

**Tarjuma:**
> "Aye Emaan walo! Insaaf par mazbooti se qaim rehne wale (**Qawwameena bil-Qist**) aur Allah ke liye gawahi dene wale ban jao, khwah woh tumhare apne khilaf ho ya walidain aur qareebi rishtadaron ke khilaf..."

#### **[Juz] Wazahat (Al-Bayan)**
**([Component] The Illumination)**

Hum is baab ka aaghaz shadi ke bare mein kisi ayat se nahi, balki pooray Quran mein insaaf ke sabse gehre aur mutlaq (absolute) ehkamaat mein se ek se kar rahe hain. Yeh ek shaoori intekhab hai. **Khudayi Manhaj** is baat par israar karta hai ke izdawaji Meesaq koi niji, la-qanooniyat wali jagah nahi hai jahan Islam ke aafaqi akhlaqi usool muattal (suspended) ho jayen. Yeh ek muqaddas ilaqah hai jo barah-e-raast is Khudayi hukum ke ikhtiyar ke tehat aata hai. Is se pehle ke hum shadi ki makhsoos hudood ki baat karein, humein pehle yeh qaim karna hoga ke yeh usi **Insaaf** ke aala usool ke tehat chalti hai jo aasmano aur zameen ko chalata hai. Ghar ke liye koi istisnayee shaq (exemption clause) nahi hai.

Yeh baab ke bunyadi thesis ko qaim karta hai: **Sakhawat se pehle Insaaf.** "Al-Fadl" (Fazl) ka khubsurat Nasb-ul-Ain, jo pichle baab mein bayan kiya gaya, **"Al-Adl" (Insaaf)** ka mutabadil nahi hai; yeh ek aala halat hai jo sirf isi bunyad par tameer ki ja sakti hai.

Isay samajhne ke liye, humein Meesaq ke manzar-namay ko ek **Jughrafiyai Naqshay** ke taur par dekhna hoga jis mein teen alag alag khittay hain:

1.  **Roshan Chotiyan: Ihsan (Roohani Fazilat).** Yeh oonchi zameen hai, woh khubsurat pahari chotiyan jahan hawa saaf aur nazara wazeh hai. Yeh "Fadl" ka daira hai, jahan miyan biwi Allah ki khatir be-los sakhawat ke saath kaam karte hain. Ek sehat-mand shadi in chotiyon ki taraf charhne ki koshish karti hai.
2.  **Zarkhez Maidan: Ma'roof (Acha Bartao).** Yeh woh wasee, payedaar ilaqah hai jahan roz-marra ki zyada tar zindagi guzarti hai. Yeh bahami ehtram, shaistagi, aur kadwahat ke baghair zimmedariyan poori karne ki sar-zameen hai.
3.  **Khaayi ka Kinara: Fiqh (Muqaddas Qanoon).** Yeh khaayi ki tez, wazeh hadd hai. Yeh Muqaddas Hudood (*Hudood Allah*) hain. Koi khaayi ke kinare par nahi rehta; insaan zarkhez maidano mein rehta hai jabke usay hamesha yeh maloom hota hai ke kinara kahan hai. Qanoon ka maqsad woh wazeh, hifazati hadd banna hai jo paataal mein girne se rokti hai.

**"Az-Zulm" (Zulm)** woh na-insaafi ka amal hai jo insaan ko roshan chotiyon se door, zarkhez maidano se bahar, aur khatarnaak hadd tak khaayi ke kinare par dhakel deta hai. Isi kinare par "Hudood ka Muhafiz" apni awaaz buland karta hai. Kyunke Muqaddas Qanoon, apni kamil hikmat aur rehmat mein, kabhi bhi Zulm ke samne khamosh rehne ka hukum nahi deta. Yeh rukne ka hukum deta hai, aur yeh ilaaj faraham karta hai.

#### **[Juz] Wirsa (Al-Turath)**
**([Component] The Heritage)**

Khudayi Manhaj tajreedi (abstract) nazriyat ka majmua nahi jo afraad ki khwahishat par chhod diya gaya ho. Yeh ek qila-band dhancha hai, jiski hifazat ek mazboot Fiqh karti hai jo sarkashi ka wazeh ilaaj faraham karti hai. Classici Asatiza romanvi idealist nahi thay; woh mahir Fuqaha aur Mahireen-e-Ilahiyat thay jo samajhte thay ke Fadl ke phalnay phoolnay ke liye Insaaf ka nifaaz zaroori hai.

Hanafi maktaba-e-fikr ke azeem muta-akhireen mein se, **Imam Ibn Abidin** is baat ka khaka pesh karte hain ke jab biwi nuqsaan (*Darar*) ki ba-qaida shikayat Qazi ke paas lati hai to insaaf kaise idara-jati shakal ikhtiyar karta hai:

**Maakhuz:** Ibn Abidin, Muhammad Amin. *Radd al-Muhtar*.

**Arabi Matn:**
> وَإِنْ قَالَتْ الزَّوْجَةُ هُوَ يَضْرِبُنِي وَيُؤْذِينِي... وَادَّعَى هُوَ الْإِصْلَاحَ... وَطَلَبَتْ التَّفْرِيقَ، فَعَلَى الْحَاكِمِ أَنْ يَبْعَثَ حَكَمَيْنِ.

**Tarjuma:**
> "Aur agar biwi kahe, 'Woh mujhe maarta hai aur takleef deta hai,' aur shohar iska inkaar kare aur neki ka dawa kare... aur biwi alaihadgi (*Tafreeq*) ka mutalba kare, to Haakim (Judge) par lazim hai ke woh do Hakam (Saalis/Arbitrators) muqarrar kare."

**Tajziya:**
Yeh mehez nasihat nahi; yeh insaaf ka idara-jati nifaaz hai. Qanoon "usne kaha, usne kaha" ki soorat-e-haal ka andaza lagata hai aur muddai ko ta'attul (stalemate) ka shikaar hone ke liye tanha chhodne se inkaar karta hai. Yeh saboot ka bojh tanha mazloom se hata kar tafteesh ka bojh community ki qayadat (Judge aur Saalis) par dalta hai. Yeh "Insaaf par mazbooti se qaim rehne" ke hukum ka qanooni mazhar hai, aur us saqafati mayaar ki barah-e-raast tardeed hai jo mubeena bad-sulooki ke samne aurat ki khamosh bardasht ka mutalba karta hai.

Agar Ibn Abidin nuqsaan ki soorat mein mudakhilat ka qanooni tareeqa ("Kaise") faraham karte hain, to Barr-e-Sagheer ke azeem Mujaddid, **Shah Waliullah Dehlawi** iske liye gehra roohani jawaz faraham karte hain. Woh talaq ke wajood ke peeche hatmi hikmat ki wazahat karte hain:

**Maakhuz:** Shah Waliullah Dehlawi. *Hujjatullah al-Baligha*.

**Arabi Matn:**
> ...فَإِذَا فَسَدَ هَذَا الْأَمْرُ، وَصَارَ الِاجْتِمَاعُ سَبَبًا لِتَعْذِيبِ الرُّوحِ... كَانَ لَا بُدَّ مِنْ طَرِيقٍ إِلَى الْخَلَاصِ مِنْهُ.

**Tarjuma:**
> "...Pas jab yeh maamla (Nikah) bigad jaye, aur unka ikattha rehna **'Rooh ke Azaab'** (*Ta'dhib al-Ruh*) ka sabab ban jaye... to is se rihayi (*Khalas*) ka koi raasta hona zaroori tha."

**Tajziya:**
Shah Waliullah talaq ko nakami ke taur par nahi, balki ek danishmand Khaliq ki taraf se nizam mein rakhay gaye zaroori aur reham-dilana **"Nikaas"** (Release/Outlet) ke taur par pesh karte hain. Tootay hue Meesaq ki unki tareef — jab yeh "Rooh ka Azaab" ban jaye — ek gehra Ilahiati bayan hai. Yeh woh halat hai jahan ghar, jisay Allah ne "Sakan" (Sukoon) ke liye banaya tha, roohani bimari ka zariya ban jata hai. Yeh Khudayi Manhaj ka mukammal ulat hai. Qanoon logon ko is roohani aziyat mein phansane ke liye nahi, balki ek ba-waqar raasta faraham karne ke liye maujood hai.

#### **[Juz] Ishtiaal (Al-Istifzaz)**
**([Component] The Provocation)**

Insaaf ke usool tajreedi nazriyat nahi hain. Unka imtehaan izdawaji Meesaq ke takleef-deh, mubham, aur intehayi zaati tanazaat mein hota hai. Hudood ko samajhne ke liye, humein inhirafaat ko be-laag wazahat ke saath dekhne ke liye tayyar rehna hoga. Darj zail manzar-namon par gaur karein.

> **(Muhafiz ka Note:** In manzar-namon mein khud ko ya apni shadi ko dekhna ek takleef-deh tajurba hai. Yeh dard kamzori nahi; yeh rooh ki talwar ko tez karna hai, isay na-insaafi ki dhund ko kaatne ke liye tayyar karna hai. Himmat ke saath parhein, mayusi ke saath nahi, kyunke insaaf ka mutalba karne ki taraf pehla qadam zakhm ko pehchanna hai.)

**Manzar-nama 1: Zaban ka Azaab**
Ek biwi khamosh iztiraab ki halat mein rehti hai. Uska shohar usay maali sahulat faraham karta hai aur usne kabhi us par haath nahi uthaya. Woh samajhta hai ke woh ek aadil aadmi hai. Taham, uska nuqsaan zaban ke zariye pohanchaya jata hai. Woh niji taur par tanziya jumlon se uski zehanat ki tazleel karta hai, khandan ke samne uske wazan ya khanay ke bare mein tauheen-aamez "mazaq" karta hai, aur sanjeeda jazbati guftagu ki koshishon ka jawab sard, saza dene wali khamoshi se deta hai jo kayi din tak jari reh sakti hai. Jab woh bil-aakhir ro padti hai, to woh us par "bahut zyada hassas" aur is aaram-deh zindagi ke liye "na-shukri" ka ilzaam lagata hai jo woh faraham karta hai.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Quran wazeh taur par biwi ko "nuqsaan pohanchane" (*Diraran*) ke liye rokay rakhne se mana karta hai. Kya Allah ki nazar mein "Dirar" ka itlaq sirf jism par hota hai, ya is mein rooh ko musalsal, jaan boojh kar kuchalna bhi shamil hai? Aur kis mod par shohar ka apni farahmi ke liye "shukr-guzari" ka mutalba woh aala ban jata hai jisay woh nuqsaan pohanchane ke liye istemaal karta hai, aur yun woh usi "Zulm" ka irtikaab karta hai jis se woh khud ko paak samajhta hai?

**Manzar-nama 2: Parhezgar Zalim**
Ek shohar maqami masjid ka sutoon (namazi) hai, jo apne sadqa aur namaz ke liye pehli saf mein maujoodgi ke liye jana jata hai. Ghar mein, woh ek zalim hai. Uski parhezgari ek hathiyar hai jisay woh apni marzi musallat karne ke liye istemaal karta hai. Woh hamesha ghussay mein rehta hai, uske alfaaz sakht hote hain, aur woh "Deen ki hifazat" ki aad mein apni biwi ki zindagi ke har pehlu ko sakht usoolon se control karta hai jo Shariat se bahut aage hain. Woh apni awami saakh ko dhaal ke taur par istemaal karta hai. Jab uski biwi kisi community buzurg se mashwara lene ki koshish karti hai, to uske khadshaat ko narmi se mustarad kar diya jata hai: *"Lekin woh to bahut nek bhai hain. Shayad aap ko bas mazeed 'Sabr' ki zaroorat hai."*

*   **Ishtiaal:** Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne sikhaya ke tum mein se behtareen woh hai jo apne ghar walon ke liye behtareen hai. Agar kisi aadmi ka kirdaar awam mein khubsurat hai lekin niji taur par zalimana hai, to Allah ki nazar mein uska haqeeqi paimana kya hai? Kya uski rasmi ibadat usay "Zulm" ke gunah se istisna (immunity) deti hai, ya ghar mein uska zulm masjid mein uski ibadat ko zaya karne ka khatra ban sakta hai?

**Manzar-nama 3: Dast-bardar Sarparast**
Ek joda shohar ke khandan ke saath rehta hai. Shohar ki maa musalsal biwi ki tazleel karti hai, tanqeed karti hai, aur zaban se takleef deti hai. Shohar yeh dekhta hai. Jab uski biwi niji taur par us se iltija karti hai ke woh uska difaa kare, to woh inkaar kar deta hai. *"Woh meri maa hain,"* woh kehta hai, *"aur Jannat unke qadmon talay hai. Main unki be-adabi nahi kar sakta. Tumhein meri khatir aur Allah ki khatir unke saath 'Sabr-e-Jameel' karna hoga. Tumhara ajr Allah ke paas hai."* Woh apni be-amli (passivity) ko neki ke taur par pesh karta hai.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Quran shohar ko biwi ke saath achay tareeqay se (*bil-ma'roof*) rehne aur insaaf par khade hone ka hukum deta hai *"khwah woh walidain ke khilaf hi kyun na ho"* (4:135). Khamosh reh kar, woh in mein se kis Khudayi hukum ko poora kar raha hai? Kya uska apni biwi se sabr ka mutalba neki ka amal hai, ya yeh apne Meesaq ki muqaddas hudood ko qaim rakhne mein uski apni nakami se **buzdilana farar** hai?

#### **[Juz] Fiqh (The Jurisprudence)**

Zulm ki takleef-deh haqeeqat ka samna karne ke baad, ab hum Muqaddas Qanoon ki gehri rehmat ki taraf ruju karte hain. Shariat tajreedi nazriyat ka aisa nizam nahi hai jo mazloom ko baghair kisi saharay ke chhod de. Iske bar-aks, yeh ek amli aur hifazati dhancha hai jo wazeh, qabil-e-nifaaz ilaaj faraham karta hai.

**1. Bunyadi Qanooni Usool (Al-Qaidah al-Fiqhiyyah): Nuqsaan ki Mumaniyat.**
*   **Usool:** *La darara wa la dirar* — "Na nuqsaan pohanchana hai aur na nuqsaan ka badla nuqsaan se dena hai."
*   **Itlaq:** Yeh hatmi qanooni dhaal hai. Shadi ke andar koi bhi amal, chahe woh alag-thalag jaiz hi kyun na ho, agar uska bunyadi maqsad ya nateeja doosre shareek-e-hayat ko qabil-e-saboot nuqsaan (*Darar*) pohanchana ho, to woh **Haram** ho jata hai.

**2. Biwi ki taraf se Rihayi (Al-Khul'): Khula**
*   **Usool:** Khula woh qanooni tareeqa-e-kaar hai jis ke zariye biwi izdawaji muahiday ko khatam karne ki pehal kar sakti hai.
*   **Itlaq:** Biwi apni shadi mein qaidi nahi hai. Muqaddas Qanoon usay ek wazeh, ba-waqar raasta faraham karta hai agar woh isay na-qabil-e-bardasht paye.

**3. Adalati Alaihadgi (At-Tafreeq lid-Darar): Nuqsaan ki wajah se alaihadgi.**
*   **Usool:** Agar biwi Qazi (Judge) ko saabit kar de ke uska shohar usay ahem, jari nuqsaan (*Darar*) pohancha raha hai — jaise jismani tashaddud, naan-o-nafqa dene mein nakami, ya la-waris chhod dena — aur shohar talaq dene se inkaar kare, to Judge ko ikhtiyar hai ke woh uski taraf se Adalati Talaq (*Tafreeq*) jari kare.

**4. Saalis Muqarrar Karna (Ba'th al-Hakamayn): Saalisi.**
*   **Usool:** Shadeed ikhtilaf (*Shiqaq*) ki soorat mein, Quran (4:35) hukum deta hai ke Judge do saalis (Hakam) muqarrar kare, ek shohar ke khandan se aur ek biwi ke khandan se, taake maamlay ki tehqeeq ki ja sake.

**Jadeed Tanazur mein Itlaq:**
Jin mumalik mein Sharai adalatein mukammal taur par fa'aal nahi hain ya jahan **Mulki Qanoon** (Civil Law) laagu hai, wahan Ulama aur Muftiyan-e-Kiram ki councils (**Darul Qaza**) yeh kirdaar ada karti hain. Khandani tanazaat mein, maqami ulama ya qabil-e-aitemaad saalison se ruju karna pehla qadam hai. Agar Zulm (jaise gharelu tashaddud) shamil ho, to mulki qanoon ka sahara lena "Shariat ke khilaf" nahi, balki apni jaan aur izzat ki hifazat ke Islami haq ka istemaal hai.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Insaaf ka Khudayi Manhaj koi nazriyati ideal nahi hai; yeh ek zinda haqeeqat hai, jisay Qanoon-saaz ﷺ ne khud mukammal wazahat ke saath dikhaya. Jab ek Meesaq apne tootne ke maqam par pohancha, to unhone aziyat ke saath la-mehdood sabr ka mashwara nahi diya. Unhone bataur Hatmi Saalis (Ultimate Arbiter) kaam kiya.

Pehle, unhone **Androoni Hadd** ki jaiziyat (legitimacy) qaim ki. Jab Sabit bin Qais (RA) ki biwi unke paas aayi, to unhone bad-sulooki ka nahi, balki rooh ki aziyat ka muqadma pesh kiya. *"Main un mein koi aib nahi pati,"* unhone elaan kiya, *"lekin main Islam mein kufr (na-shukri) ko napasand karti hoon."* Woh ek gehra bayan de rahi thin: unki shohar se shadeed zaati na-pasandidegi, bawajood iske ke woh ek achay aadmi thay, unhein bataur biwi apne faraiz mein nakaam kar degi. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne unke jazbaat par sawal nahi uthaya. Unhone unki rooh ki aziyat ko ek qanooni haqeeqat ke taur par pehchana aur fauran **Khula** ka adilana ilaaj tajweez kiya.

Doosra, unhone **Bairooni Hadd** ki hurmat qaim ki. Barirah (RA) ka waqia jazbaat par qanoon ki bartari ka masterclass hai. Jab woh ghulami se azad huin, to qanoon ne unhein apne shohar, Mughith, se nikah khatam karne ka haq diya. Unhone aisa karne ka intekhab kiya. Mughith dil-shikasta thay, Madina ki galiyon mein unke peeche rotay hue chalte thay. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ka apna dil hamdardi se bhar gaya. Unhone Barirah se narmi se sifarish ki, *"Kaash tum uske paas wapas chali jao."* Unhone phir woh faisla-kun sawal poocha jo Fiqh ke adwaar mein goonjta rahega: *"Ya Rasool Allah, kya aap mujhe hukum de rahe hain?"* Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne jawab diya: *"Main sirf sifarish kar raha hoon."* Apne hukum ke hatne ke saath, Barirah ne apna qanooni haq bayan kiya: *"Mujhe uski koi zaroorat nahi."* Maamla tay ho gaya. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ki zaati hamdardi us afaqi, qanooni haq ko nahi daba saki jo Shariat ne unhein diya tha.

#### **[Juz] Mohar (Al-Khatm)**
**([Component] The Seal)**

**Manhaj ki Tabdeeli:**

*   **Se:** Ek la-qanooniyat wali niji jagah.
*   **Tak:** Khudayi Qanoon ke zariye mehfooz ek muqaddas ilaqah.

*   **Se:** Zulm ke saath sabr ka mutalba.
*   **Tak:** "Rooh ke Azaab" se rihayi ke haq ki pasdari.

*   **Se:** Qanoon bataur qaid-khanay ki salakhein.
*   **Tak:** Qanoon bataur qilay ki deewarein.

*   **Se:** Khoon (Khandan) se wafadari.
*   **Tak:** Sachayi (Haq) se wafadari.

**Do Duayein:**

Insaaf mehez bairooni qawaneen ka nizam nahi; yeh us "Nafs" ke khilaf androoni jung hai jo apni khwahishat (Hawa) ki taraf mail hota hai. Lehaza, hum is baab ka ikhtitam is gehri dua ke saath karte hain:

*   **Nabvi Dua (Ghalti ke aitraaf ke liye):**
    > *Allahumma inni zalamtu nafsi zulman kaseera, wa la yaghfiruz-zunooba illa anta, faghfir li maghfiratan min 'indika, warhamni, innaka antal Ghafoor-ur-Raheem.*
    > **"Aye Allah, maine apni jaan par bahut zulm kiya hai, aur Tere siwa koi gunahon ko maaf nahi karta. Pas apni taraf se mujhe maaf farma aur mujh par reham farma. Beshak Tu hi Bakhshne wala, Reham karne wala hai."** (Sahih Bukhari)

*   **Sada Zaban mein Dua (Hudood ke Muhafiz ke liye):**
    > "Aye Allah, **Al-Hakam** (Faisla karne wale), **Al-Adl** (Insaaf karne wale), hum Tujh se woh baseerat mangte hain ke hum un muqaddas hudood ko dekh sakein jo Tu ne qaim ki hain. Humein insaaf par khade hone ki himmat ata farma, chahe woh hamare apne khilaf, hamare khandano, ya hamari khwahishat ke khilaf ho. Humein zalim banne aur mazloom banne se bacha. Jab hamare saath ziyadati ho, to humein woh insaaf talab karne ki hikmat ata farma jo Tu ne faraham kiya hai. Jab hum ziyadati karein, to humein tauba ki aajizi ata farma. Ameen."

## Doosra Darwaza: Insani Safeer (Al-Wukala') - Sutoon
**(Gate II: The Human Ambassadors - The Pillars)**

### **Baab 3: Qiwamiyat ka Muqaddas Bojh**
**(Chapter 3: The Sacred Burden of Qiwamah)**

#### **[Juz] Bunyadi Usool (Al-Asl)**
**([Component] The Anchor)**

**Maakhuz:** Quran, Surah An-Nisa, 4:34

**Arabi Matn:**
> الرِّجَالُ قَوَّامُونَ عَلَى النِّسَاءِ بِمَا فَضَّلَ اللَّهُ بَعْضَهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ وَبِمَا أَنفَقُوا مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ

**Tarjuma:**
> "Mard aurton par **Qawwam** (Nigehban/Muntazim) hain, is wajah se ke Allah ne un mein se baaz ko baaz par fazilat di hai aur is wajah se ke unhone apne maal kharch kiye hain."

#### **[Juz] Wazahat (Al-Bayan)**
**([Component] The Illumination)**

Yeh ayat jadeed daur mein Muslim shadi ke tanazaat ka markaz (epicenter) hai. Isay **Yargamaal** bana liya gaya hai, aur mutazad nazriyat ki tehon talay iske asal ma'ani ko daba diya gaya hai. Ek taraf, saqafati **Pidar-shahi** (Patriarchy) ke alambardaron ne isay ek danday ki tarah istemaal kiya hai, bila-choon-o-chara itaat ka mutalba karte hue aur apne tootne wale ikhtiyar ko Khuda ki marzi samajh kar. Doosri taraf, jadeed **Secular** tabqay ne isay tazheek-aamez reham ke saath pesh kiya hai, isay zulm ki ek na-qabil-e-islah nishani ke taur par pesh karte hue.

Dono shadeed ghalti par hain. Dono Quran ko apne duniyawi tasawwuraat ke dhundlay lens se dekhne ke mujrim hain — ek saqafati takabbur ka, doosra nazriyati adam-tahaffuz (insecurity) ka.

Is istilaah ko dobara hasil karne ke liye, humein wahi karna hoga jo hamari riwayat ke azeem asatiza ne hamesha kiya hai: humein Wahi ki zaban ki jaron ki taraf lautna hoga. "Qawwamoon" ke ma'ani ka safar mehez ek ilmi mashq nahi; yeh **"Roohani Aasar-e-Qadima"** (Spiritual Archaeology) ka amal hai. Aayiye hum apni riwayat ke auzaar uthayen — lughat aur grammar — aur khudayi ka kaam shuru karein.

#### **[Juz] Ishtiqaq (The Etymology)**

"Qawwamoon" ka lafz teen harfi Arabi madday **Q-W-M (Qaf, Waw, Meem)** se maakhuz hai. Is madday ka bunyadi mafhoom khada hona, uthna, qaim karna, barqarar rakhna, aur kisi cheez ko seedha ya durust karna hai. Bunyadi fail (verb) *"Qama"* (woh khada hua) hai, lekin zyada ahem shakal *"Aqama"* (usne kisi cheez ko mazbooti se qaim kiya) hai. Yeh wahi fail hai jo Allah ﷻ Namaz ke liye istemaal karta hai: *Aqimus-Salah* — "Namaz qaim karo."

Ayat mein istemaal hone wala lafz, **"Qawwam"**, grammar ke lihaaz se **Mubalaghah** ka seegha (Intensive form) hai. Yeh us shakhs ki nishandahi karta hai jo bunyadi amal ko sirf ek baar nahi, balki **musalsal, behtareen tareeqay se, aur gehri zimmedari ke saath** saranjam deta hai. Lehaza, ek "Qawwam" woh nahi jo mehez "khada" ho ya ghair-fa'aal (passive) taur par "in-charge" ho. Woh ghar ka **Sutoon** (Pillar) hai, woh jis ka kaam musalsal, fa'aal, aur seedha khada rehna hai taake woh sahara de sake. Woh ek muhafiz hai, ek nigran hai, ek manager hai, woh jo mamlaat ko durust karne aur barqarar rakhne ke liye anthak mehnat karta hai.

Lekin Allah ﷻ khud humein Qawwam hone ka hatmi matlab kahan dikhata hai? Woh iski tareef karta hai us ayat mein jo hamari riwayat mein Insaaf ka sang-e-bunyad hai:

> ...كُونُوا **قَوَّامِينَ** بِالْقِسْطِ...
> *...Kunu **Qawwameena** bil-qist...*
> "...Insaaf par **mazbooti se qaim rehne wale (Qawwameen)** ban jao..." (Quran 4:135)

Quran apni lughat khud hai. Ek Qawwam, Khudayi tareef ki roo se, woh hai jis ka bunyadi kaam **Insaaf (Al-Qist)** ko musalsal qaim karna aur barqarar rakhna hai.

Lehaza, apne khandan par mard ki "Qiwamiyat" aamirana control ka license nahi hai; yeh us khandan ke andar **Insaaf** ka bunyadi alambardar aur anthak muhafiz banne ka Khudayi hukum hai. Yeh biwi ke haqooq ki hifazat, bacchon ki zarooriyat poori karne, aur ghar ko masawat aur neki ke saath chalane ka muqaddas farz hai. Iska ikhtiyar koi **Istehqaq** nahi; yeh ek gehra aur bhari **Bojh** hai.

#### **Hatmi Faislay ka Bojh (The Burden of the Final Word)**

Khudayi Manhaj ka Nasb-ul-Ain (Ideal) woh faisla saazi hai jo mukhlasana aur mohabbat bhari **Mushawarat** (Shura) par mabni ho. Lekin insani halat ki haqeeqat baaz auqaat **Ta'attul** hoti hai. Jab do safeer, mukhlasana gaur-o-fikr ke baad, kisi ahem, jaiz maamlay par kisi nateejay par na pohanch sakein, to Meesaq ko falij-zada (paralyzed) hone ki ijazat nahi di ja sakti.

Yahan Qiwamiyat ka hatmi, bhari pehlu zahir hota hai. Tamam mashwaray lene ke baad, agar khandan ki falah ke liye faisla karna zaroori ho, to us hatmi faislay ki zimmedari shohar par aati hai. Yeh taqat ka nasha nahi; yeh **Jawabdehi** ka khaufnaak bojh hai. Woh koi badshah nahi jis ki marzi qanoon ho. Woh woh shakhs hai jisay, us lamhay mein, nateejay ka poora wazan uthana hoga aur yeh janna hoga ke woh akela Allah ke samne iske bare mein jawabdeh hoga. Zalim hatmi faisla karne mein lutf uthata hai; haqeeqi **Qawwam** iska bojh mehsoos karta hai, hidayat ke liye dua karta hai, aur khauf aur kampkapi mein faisla karta hai, yeh jante hue ke uski hatmi wafadari apne Rab ke Insaaf ke saath hai, na ke apne Nafs ki khwahishat ke saath.

#### **[Juz] Ishtiaal (Al-Istifzaz)**
**([Component] The Provocation)**

Quran ki apni zaban se Qiwamiyat ke asal ma'ani ko dobara hasil karne ke baad, ab humein is tareef ko haqeeqat ki bhatti mein parakhna hoga. Khudayi Manhaj aur iske bigday hue saqafati izhaar ke darmiyan faasla aksar bahut wasee hota hai. Is faaslay ko paatne ke liye, insaan ko pehle isay dekhne ki himmat karni hogi. Darj zail manzar-namon par gaur karein.

**Manzar-nama 1: Dast-bardar Sarparast (The Abdicated Guardian)**
Ek shohar apne khandan ko achi rahaish aur kharcha faraham karta hai. Woh samajhta hai ke uski maali farahmi uski Qiwamiyat ka aaghaz aur ikhtitam hai. Taham, woh jazbati aur roohani taur par ghair-hazir hai. Woh namaz mein apne khandan ki imamat nahi karta aur na hi seekhne ka culture qaim karta hai. Jab uske apne rishtedar aise jumlay kehte hain jo uski biwi ko zakhmi karte hain, to woh tanaza se bachne ke liye khamosh rehta hai. Usne apne ghar ki akhlaqi aur roohani **Tarbiyat** apni biwi aur beruni idaron ke supurd kar di hai. Woh ek **"Mehez Maali Kafil"** hai, lekin "Sutoon" nahi.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Quran Qawwam ki tareef is tarah karta hai jo "mazbooti se khada" ho. Kya yeh khada hona sirf check sign karne ke amal ki taraf ishara karta hai? Ya yeh ek insaaf pasand, mehfooz, aur roohani taur par sehat-mand gharanay ke fa'aal, musalsal qayam ki taraf ishara karta hai? Kya woh aadmi jo apne khandan ki roohani aur jazbati falah-o-behbood ke bunyadi muhafiz ke taur par apne kirdaar se dast-bardar ho chuka hai, waqayi unka "Qawwam" kehlaya ja sakta hai?

**Manzar-nama 2: Maali Ulat-pher (The Financial Inversion)**
Maashi buhraan ki wajah se, ek shohar ki naukri chali jati hai. Uski biwi, jo ek mahir professional hai, ghar ki bunyadi kafil ban jati hai. Shohar, jis ka poora ehsas-e-zat (sense of self) ayat ke *"Bima anfaqu"* ("jo unhone kharch kiya") hissay se juda tha, mehsoos karta hai ke uski Qiwamiyat khatam ho gayi hai. Woh sharam aur ehsas-e-kamtarri mein ghira hua hai, chirchira aur difayi (defensive) ho gaya hai. Woh apni biwi ki kamiyabi ko khandan ke liye Allah ki rehmat ke taur par nahi, balki ek mard ke taur par apni shanakht ke liye barah-e-raast khatra samajhta hai.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Qiwamiyat ki ayat do wajuhaat deti hai: ek Khudayi fitrat (*Bima faddalallahu*) aur maali kharch (*Bima anfaqu*). Agar doosri wajah halaat ki wajah se kam ho jaye, to kya pehli wajah khatam ho jati hai? Kya Qiwamiyat ek title hai jo tankhwah par munhasir hai, ya yeh shohar ki bunyadi zimmedari hai ke woh apne khandan ke liye taqat, hifazat aur insaaf ka hatmi sutoon bane, chahe us taqat ki shakal kuch bhi ho? Kya is shohar ka buhraan Khudayi Manhaj mein kisi khami ka nateeja hai, ya mardangi ke bare mein uski apni saqafati, maadiyat-parast (materialistic) samajh ki nakami hai?

> **(Muhafiz ka Note:** Yahan humein waise hi farq karna chahiye jaise Fuqaha ne kiya: Ek **"Adalat ki Qiwamiyat"** hai aur ek **"Rooh ki Qiwamiyat"** hai. Adalat ki Qiwamiyat ek qanooni haisiyat (*Hukum*) hai jo naan-o-nafqa jaise faraiz ki takmeel se judi hai. Agar koi mard is mein nakaam hota hai, to Qazi us se kuch ikhtiyarat wapas le sakta hai. Lekin Rooh ki Qiwamiyat ek roohani maqam (*Maqam*) hai, insaaf aur rehmat ka sutoon banne ki bunyadi zimmedari. Yeh kirdaar ka maamla hai, aamdani ka nahi. Woh aadmi jo naukri kho deta hai lekin apne khandan ki khidmat mein apni koshish dugni kar deta hai, usne tankhwah khoyi hai, lekin Allah ki nazar mein bataur Qawwam uski izzat dar-haqeeqat barh gayi hogi.)

**Manzar-nama 3: "Parhezgar" Controller**
Ek shohar bahut mazhabi hai aur apni Qiwamiyat ki position ko mukammal control hasil karne ke liye istemaal karta hai. Woh apni biwi ki zindagi ke faislay Shariat ki zarooriyat se kahin aage ja kar karta hai, yeh dawa karte hue ke uski "hifazat" karna uska farz hai. Woh khandan ke liye har faisla yak-tarfa taur par karta hai. Usne sakht qawaneen ka ek qila tameer kiya hai, jisay woh taqwa ka qila samajhta hai. Taham, uski biwi mehsoos karti hai ke woh ek khubsurat sajay hue qaid-khanay mein hai, jahan uski koi raaye ya awaaz nahi.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Hum ne Quran ki apni lughat se yeh qaim kiya hai ke Qawwam woh hai jo "**Insaaf** (Al-Qist) par mazbooti se khada ho." Kya apni marzi ka yak-tarfa nifaaz, chahe niyat nek hi kyun na ho, insaaf ka amal hai? Sarparasti (Qiwamah) aur Malkiyat (Milkiyyah) ke darmiyan lakeer kahan hai? Kis mod par kisi ko gunah se "bachana" unki Khuda ki di hui izzat aur **Ikhtiyar** (Agency) cheen kar Zulm ki ek shakal ban jata hai?

#### **[Juz] Uboori Pul (The Transitional Bridge)**

In bigday hue aainon mein khud ko dekhna ek intehayi be-chain kar dene wala tajurba hai. Phir bhi yeh be-chaini mayusi ki wajah nahi hai. Yeh roohani zindagi ki alamat hai, us rooh ki pehli halchal jo nakami ke Manhaj se munh mod kar sachayi ke Manhaj ki taraf dekh rahi hai. Kyunke Allah, apni kamil rehmat mein, bimari ki tashreeh (anatomy) zahir nahi karta jab tak ke woh ilaaj ka zinda namuna bhi faraham na kare. Aayiye ab in naqis modelon ki bimari se Nabvi Namunay ki kamil dawa ki taraf ruju karein.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Qiwamiyat ka Nabvi Namuna faraiz ki list nahi hai; yeh ek zinda haqeeqat hai, ek azeem darakht ki tarah, jo apni gehri jaron se lekar apni oonchi shakhon tak mukammal taur par juda hua hai.

Sabse pehle, Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne iski gehri, nazar na aane wali jarein Aakhirat ke Ilahiati bister (bedrock) mein qaim ki. Apne aakhri, aafaqi khutbay mein, Hajjat-ul-Wida ke mauqay par, unhone shohar ke haqooq ki baat nahi ki, balki uski khaufnaak zimmedari ki baat ki. Unhone mardon ko hukum diya: *"Aurton ke maamlay mein Allah se daro, kyunke tum ne unhein Allah ki **Amanat** ke taur par liya hai."* Yeh Qiwamiyat ka jadd ka nizam (root system) hai. Yeh duniyawi ikhtiyar ya mardana ana ki mitti mein nahi, balki **Taqwa** ki gehri mitti mein paiwast hai. Yeh apni zindagi ka khoon Allah ke khauf se leta hai aur is ilm se ke shohar ek Ameen (Trustee) hai jisay us muqaddas rooh ke bare mein jawab dena hoga jo uski nigrani mein di gayi hai.

In gehri jaron se aajizi aur khidmat ka mazboot, nazar aane wala tana (trunk) uga. Jab poocha gaya ke Allah ke Rasool ﷺ apne ghar mein kya karte thay, to Syeda Aisha (RA) ne ek aise shakhs ka zikr kiya jo khidmat mein masroof rehta: woh apne jootay khud tanktay, apni bakri ka doodh khud dhotay, aur apni zarooriyat khud poori karte. Yeh us jadd ka amli izhaar hai. Unki Qiwamiyat ka tana hukum aur istehqaq ki lakdi se nahi, balki aajizana, fa'aal khidmat ke sakht reshon se bana tha. Woh apne khandan ke liye "khade" thay unka bojh andar se utha kar.

Aur us mazboot tanay se danishmandana aur reham-dilana qayadat ki oonchi shakhein phelin. Apni biwi par tohmat (Waqia-e-Ifk) ke takleef-deh buhraan ke dauran, jab unka ghar hangama-khezi ka shikaar tha, unhone yak-tarfa authority ke taur par kaam nahi kiya. Unhone dikhaya ke Muhafiz sun kar rehnumayi karta hai, apne ghar ki khawateen se mashwara karta hai. Yeh Qiwamiyat ki woh shakhein hain jo bahar nikalti hain, hifazati saya faraham karti hain, aur aakhir-kaar pooray khandan ke liye insaaf aur rehmat ka phal lati hain.

Yeh hai mukammal Nabvi Namuna. Yeh zalim ka tootne wala danda nahi, aur na hi dast-bardar hone wale ka khokhla narkat (reed). Yeh ek zinda, saans leta hua darakht hai: iski jarein Khuda ke khauf mein gehri hain, iska tana aajizana khidmat se mazboot hai, aur iski shakhein mushawarati rehmat ke saath pheli hui hain. Yeh dast-bardari, control, aur nazuk ana ki bimariyon ka kamil ilaaj hai. Yeh muqaddas bojh hai, jisay kamil tareeqay se uthaya gaya hai.

> **(Muhafiz ka Note:** Zara gaur karein, is Nabvi model ke kamaal par. Yeh ek mukammal nizam (ecosystem) hai. Yeh mutazad khubiyon ki list nahi, balki ek yakjaan haqeeqat hai. Jadeed zehan taqat ko khidmat ke saath, aur ikhtiyar ko rehmat ke saath jodne mein mushkil mehsoos karta hai. Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ne inhein ek haqeeqat ke taur par mujassam kiya. Hamare Deen ka maqsad yahi hai: mutazad khubiyon ko balance karna nahi, balki unhein ek wahid, khubsurat, Khuda-shanaas rooh mein zam kar dena.)

#### **[Juz] Mohar (Al-Khatm)**
**([Component] The Seal)**

**Manhaj ki Tabdeeli:**

*   **Se:** Qiwamiyat bataur ikhtiyar ka Istehqaq.
*   **Tak:** Qiwamiyat bataur zimmedari ka muqaddas bojh.

*   **Se:** In-charge hone ka haq.
*   **Tak:** Insaaf par khade hone ka farz.

*   **Se:** Kamiyabi ko control ki satah se mapna.
*   **Tak:** Kamiyabi ko ghar mein **Sakinah** ki satah se mapna.

*   **Se:** Zalim ka tootne wala danda.
*   **Tak:** Nabvi Qiwamiyat ka zinda darakht — jo Khuda ke khauf mein jad pakday hue hai, aajizana khidmat par tameer hai, aur mushawarati rehmat ka phal deta hai.

**Do Duayein:**

Qiwamiyat ka muqaddas bojh mard ki diyanat-dari ka hatmi imtehaan hai uske wajood ke teen dairon mein. Woh apne gharanay ke **Deen** ka zimmedar hai, unki **Duniya** ke adilana intezam ka, aur unhein ek khubsurat **Aakhirat** ki taraf le jane ka. Chunke yeh zimmedari mukammal (total) hai, is liye isay sirf Allah se mukammal madad mang kar hi poora kiya ja sakta hai. Lehaza, hum is baab ka ikhtitam Nabi Kareem ﷺ ki apni jame' dua ke saath karte hain:

*   **Nabvi Dua (Saleh Maamlaat ke liye):**
    > *Allahumma aslih li deeni alladhi huwa 'ismatu amri, wa aslih li dunyaya allati fiha ma'ashi, wa aslih li akhirati allati fiha ma'adi.*
    > **"Aye Allah, mere liye mere Deen ko durust farma de jo mere maamlaat ka muhafiz hai, aur mere liye meri Duniya ko durust farma de jis mein meri ma'ash (zindagi) hai, aur mere liye meri Aakhirat ko durust farma de jis taraf mujhe lautna hai."** (Sahih Muslim)

*   **Sada Zaban mein Dua (Muhafiz-Khadim ke liye):**
    > "Aye Allah, **Ar-Raqib** (Nigehban), Tu ne yeh Amanat hamare kandhon par rakhi hai. Humein isay insaaf aur rehmat ke saath uthane ki taqat ata farma. Hamare dilon ko ikhtiyar ki mohabbat aur control ki khwahish se paak kar de. Humein wo muhafiz bana jo hifazat karein, wo zalim nahi jo zulm karein. Humein wo khadim bana jo bojh uthayen, wo aaqa nahi jo bojh dalein. Humein mashwara karne ki aajizi, rehnumayi karne ki hikmat, aur khidmat karne ki shafqat ata farma, taake hamari Qiwamiyat Tere kamil insaaf ka aks aur Teri raza hasil karne ka zariya ban jaye. Ameen."

### **Baab 4: Ghar ki Malika (Rabbat al-Bayt)**
**(Chapter 4: The Queen of the House)**

#### **[Juz] Bunyadi Usool (Al-Asl)**
**([Component] The Anchor)**

**Maakhuz:** Sahih Muslim, *Kitab al-Rida'*

**Arabi Matn:**
> الدُّنْيَا مَتَاعٌ وَخَيْرُ مَتَاعِ الدُّنْيَا الْمَرْأَةُ الصَّالِحَةُ

**Tarjuma:**
> "Duniya ek mataa' (fayda uthane ki cheez) hai, aur duniya ki behtareen mataa' **Nek Aurat** (*Al-Mar'ah As-Saliha*) hai."

---
---

#### **[Juz] Wazahat (Al-Bayan)**
**([Component] The Illumination)**

Allah ﷻ ne tumhare liye tumhare gharon ko sukoon ki jagah (*Sakanan*) banaya hai. Yeh ghar ka Khudayi Maqsad hai. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne apni gehri hikmat mein us wahid sabse bade zariye ki nishandahi ki jis se yeh maqsad hasil hota hai, jab unhone elaan kiya ke poori duniya mein behtareen mataa' **Nek Aurat** hai.

Taham, aala izzat ke is maqam par do mahazon se hamla kiya gaya hai. Pehla **"Saqafati Pinjra"** hai, jis ne aksar biwi ko ek **Ghair-Mar'ee** (Invisible) khadima ke darjay par gira diya hai, jiski mehnat ko mamooli samjha jata hai. Doosra jadeediyat ki **"Bazari Zehniyat"** hai, jis ne usay us waqt tak ghair-paidawar (unproductive) qarar diya hai jab tak ke woh kamati na ho, khandan ki aabyari ke aala fun ko tankhwah ke khaam paimanay se mapti hai.

Is se pehle ke hum in bigaad ko door karein, ek ahem hadd qaim karni hogi. Aayiye wazeh karein: Biwi ka bataur **"Panah-gaah ki Kaashtkar"** hona khala mein poora kiya jane wala hukum nahi hai. Yeh ek Khudayi masawat ka doosra hissa hai. Yeh mukammal taur par shohar ke bataur **"Muhafiz-Khadim"** hone ki bunyad par khada hai jaisa ke pichle baab mein bayan kiya gaya. Ek panah-gaah na-insaafi ke saaye mein tameer nahi ki ja sakti. Ek malika apni saltanat par hukumat nahi kar sakti agar woh badshah se darti ho. Lehaza, is ba-izzat kirdaar ki behes ek aadil aur saleh Meesaq ke wajood par mabni hai. Jahan shohar apni Qiwamiyat ki hifazat aur insaaf faraham karne mein nakaam rehta hai, wahan biwi ki bunyadi jad-o-jehad Sakinah ki kaashtkari nahi, balki **Adl** (Insaaf) ka mutalba hai, jo uska Khuda-daad haq hai.

Is insaaf ki bunyad ke saath, hum Khudayi Manhaj ke haqeeqi **"Teesre Raaste"** ki taraf ruju kar sakte hain. Yeh **"Rabbat al-Bayt"** ka tasawwur hai — ek classici aur gehra aizaazi laqab jis ka matlab hai **"Ghar ki Malika"** ya "Muntazima". Yeh khadima ka tasawwur nahi, aur na hi kisi maashi unit ka, balki ba-waqar, makhsoos daira-e-ikhtiyar ki qayadat ka hai. Ab waqt aa gaya hai ke us khoyi hui izzat ko bahal kiya jaye.

---
---

#### **[Juz] Ishtiaal (Al-Istifzaz)**
**([Component] The Provocation)**

Rabbat al-Bayt ki izzat ek roshan choti hai, lekin bahut se logon ke liye haqeeqi zindagi saayon ki wadi hai. Roshni ko samajhne ke liye, humein pehle iske bigaad ke dard ki tashkhees karni hogi. Darj zail manzar-namon par gaur karein.

> **(Muhafiz ka Note:** In manzar-namon mein bayan ki gayi thakawat aur na-insaafi ka ehsas haqeeqi aur gehra hai. Yeh jaiz ghussa na-shukri ki alamat nahi hai; yeh **Fitrah** ki awaaz hai jo ek khoye hue Manhaj ke khilaf pukaar rahi hai. Khudayi Manhaj koi naya bojh nahi dalta, balki izzat ki bahali aur ek aisi panah-gaah ka wada karta hai jo uske kaashtkar ki rooh ko bhi utna hi sukoon deti hai jitna uske makeeno ko.)

**Manzar-nama 1: Nadeeda Fun (The Invisible Art)**
Ek biwi apni koshish ghar ko haqeeqi *Sakan* banane ke liye waqf karti hai. Woh safayi rakhti hai, ehtimam se khana banati hai, aur apne khandan ke liye aman ki fiza qaim karne ke liye kaam karti hai. Uska shohar, taham, isay ek mamool ki, mutawaqqa halat samajhta hai. Woh shayad hi kabhi uski koshish ka aitraaf karta hai. Ghar ki halat par uske tabsiray sirf tab hote hain jab koi cheez ghalat ho — koi cheez apni jagah par na ho, ya khana der se mile. Sakinah ki uski anthak kaashtkari **Ghair-Mar'ee** (invisible) ho gayi hai; sirf iski ghair-maujoodgi hi notice ki jati hai. Woh khud ko panah-gaah ki kaashtkar se kam aur kisi **Nizam Chalanay walay** (System Administrator) ki tarah mehsoos karti hai jisay feedback sirf tab milta hai jab koi kharabi ho.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Agar biwi ka bunyadi kirdaar Sakinah ki kaashtkari hai, to shohar ka bunyadi mutabaqat rakhne wala kirdaar kya hai? Kya yeh mehez is sukoon ka sarif (consumer) banna hai, ya us funkaar ka fa'aal muhafiz aur qadardan banna hai jo isay takhleeq karta hai? Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne behtareen mard ki tareef yeh ki ke woh "apne ghar walon ke liye behtareen" ho. Shohar ki apni biwi ki rozana, ghair-mar'ee mehnat ko dekhne, tasleem karne aur izzat dene mein nakami uske apne "behtareen" hone ke maqam ko kaise mutasir karti hai?

**Manzar-nama 2: "Sab Kuch Hasil Karne" ka Fareb**
Ek Muslim khatoon apne professional career mein intehayi kamiyab hai, jise uski community ek role model ke taur par sarahati hai jis ke paas "sab kuch hai." Haqeeqat mein, woh roohani aur jismani taur par toot chuki hai. Kaam ke sakht din ke baad, woh ghar aati hai **"Doosri Shift"** ke liye: ghar ka intezam, bacchon ka homework, aur agle din ki tayyari. Uska ghar saaf aur munazzam hai, lekin yeh karkardagi (efficiency) ki jagah hai, **Sakinah** ki nahi. Naram guftagu, sabr se sunne, ya ghar ki roohani aabyari ke liye koi tawanayi baqi nahi bachi. Khandan ka intezam acha hai lekin roohani taur par ghizayat ki kami hai. Uska shohar uske career ko "support" karta hai (yani aitraaz nahi karta), lekin woh tamam gharelu mamlaat ko uski makhsoos zimmedari samajhta hai.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Is khandan ki soorat-e-haal kamiyabi ki jadeed tareef aur qadar-o-qeemat ki Nabvi tareef ke darmiyan bunyadi tazad ko kaise zahir karti hai? Kya shohar ne waqayi apni biwi ko "support" kiya hai, ya usne mehez usay do full-time naukriyan karne ki ijazat di hai jabke woh khud ek ke saath jari hai? Aur kya yeh humein us hatmi, takleef-deh sawal poochne par majboor nahi karta: kya Sakinah ki kaashtkari mehez ek kaam hai jisay career ke kinaron par "fit" kiya jana hai, ya yeh ek aala fun hai jo apni makhsoos jagah, tawanayi aur tawajjo ka mutalba karta hai?

#### **[Juz] Uboori Pul (The Transitional Bridge)**

Ghair-mar'ee funkaar ki thakawat aur dohare bojh talay dabi hui aurat koi kahaniyan nahi hain; yeh ek khoye hue Manhaj ki zinda haqeeqatein hain. Na-qadri ya bojh talay dabne ka yeh ehsas zaati nakami ki alamat nahi, balki us kirdaar par ek jaiz aur saleh ghussa hai jisay uski izzat se mehroom kar diya gaya hai. Khudayi Manhaj koi naya bojh nahi, balki us izzat ki bahali hai.

#### **[Juz] Ilaaj (Al-Tiryaq)**
**([Component] The Antidote)**

Khudayi Manhaj "Teesra Raasta" pesh karta hai, izzat ka ek raasta jo un do azeem bigaad ke bar-aks khada hai jinhone biwi ke maqam ko ghata diya hai. Yeh qayadat ka Manhaj hai, ghulami ka nahi; aur muqaddas maqsad ka hai, maashi paidawar ka nahi.

Pehle, humein us **Saqafati Pinjray** ko todna hoga jis ne biwi ko khadima bana diya hai. Yeh tasawwur ke biwi qanooni taur par shohar aur uske wasee khandan ke liye khana pakane, safayi karne aur khidmat karne ki paband hai, Muqaddas Qanoon se inhiraf hai. Classici Fuqaha, khaas taur par Hanafi maktaba-e-fikr mein, is maamlay par bilkul wazeh thay. Biwi gharelu kaam kaaj karne ki **qanooni taur par paband nahi hai**. Lehaza ghar mein uski khidmat naukar ke muahiday ki takmeel nahi hai; yeh **Ihsan** aur **Mawaddah** (fa'aal mohabbat) ka ek gehra amal hai. Saqafati model jo is khidmat ka haq ke taur par mutalba karta hai, woh dohri chori ka irtikaab karta hai: yeh uske kaam se izzat aur uski niyat se Khudayi ajr chura leta hai.

> **(Muhafiz ka Note: Mehnat ka Waqar.** Yeh ek tazad maloom hota hai: qanoon kehta hai ke koi zimmedari nahi, phir bhi Nabi ﷺ ki beti, Syeda Fatima (RA) ki mehnat ki tareef ki jati hai? Jawab qanooni haq aur roohani fazilat ke darmiyan farq hai. Qanoon usay naukarani ke maqam se bachata hai, taake woh azadana taur par ek malika ke maqam ka intekhab kar sake jo mohabbat ki wajah se apni saltanat ki khidmat karti hai. Fatima (RA) ki mehnat kisi mard ki ghulami nahi thi; yeh apne ghar ke liye gehra Sadaqah aur Allah ki bandagi thi. Khudayi Manhaj mein jhaaroo bedi nahi hai; yeh ek **Shahi Aasa** mein badal jata hai.)

Doosra, humein **Bazari Zehniyat** ko mustarad karna hoga jo usay kamayi ke baghair ghair-ahem samajhti hai. Yeh jadeed, la-deen (secular) nazriya insaan ki qadar ko **Sarmaya** (Capital) paida karne ki salahiyat se mapta hai, jo gehri roohani jahalat ki ek shakal hai — haqeeqi qadar ki nishaniyon ko padhne se qasir hona. Is ne Muslim zehan mein jagah bana li hai, jis se **Gharelu Khatoon** ke kirdaar ko "kam-himmat" (unambitious) samjha jane laga hai. Bazaar us cheez ki qadar karta hai jo sarmaya paida kare; Khudayi Manhaj us cheez ki qadar karta hai jo **Sakinah** aur nek insaan paida kare. Ek panah-gaah ki kaashtkari ke aala fun ko tankhwah ke khaam paimanay se mapna roohani taur par andha hona hai.

Nabvi "Teesra Raasta" haqeeqi basarat bahal karta hai. Yeh **"Rabbat al-Bayt"** ka Manhaj hai — ba-izzat "Ghar ki Malika". Is model mein, ghar uski saltanat hai. Woh iski mulazima nahi, balki iski bunyadi kaashtkar aur rehnuma hai. Uska mission eent aur gare ke dhanchay ko rooh ki panah-gaah mein badalna hai — ek aisi jagah jo daulat nahi, balki sukoon paida kare; mulazim nahi, balki saleh momin paida kare. Yeh koi "naukri" nahi; yeh ek muqaddas pesha hai, ek roohani fun hai, aur ek phalti phoolti Ummat ka bunyadi sutoon hai. Shohar is malika ka muhafiz aur uske fun ka qadardan hai, naukar ka aaqa nahi.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Rabbat al-Bayt ka Manhaj Nabi Kareem ﷺ ki zindagi mein koi ek kirdaar nahi tha, balki ek kayi pehluon wali haqeeqat thi. Unka gharana zahir karta hai ke nek biwi ba-yak-waqt **Roohani Ahmiyat** ki hamil shakhsiyat, **Hikmat-e-Amli** (Strategic Intellect) ki sathi, aur ek aisi karkun hai jiski **Mehnat** Khudayi zikr se muqaddas ho jati hai. Yeh teen pehlu Ghar ki Malika ke mukammal Nabvi vision ko tashkeel dete hain.

Uski roohani ahmiyat aisi hai ke uske daira-e-ikhtiyar ke androoni mamlaat Ilahi tawajjo ka markaz hain. Yeh Shehad ke waqiye se zyada kahin wazeh nahi, jis ki wajah se Surah At-Tahrim ka aaghaz hua. Jab apni biwiyon ke darmiyan ek muahiday ne Nabi Kareem ﷺ ko apne liye kisi halal cheez ko haram karne par majboor kiya, sirf apne ghar ka aman qaim rakhne ke liye, to Allah ﷻ ne khud Wahi ke zariye mudakhilat ki. Sabaq gehra hai: ghar ka "niji" jazbati mausam ek aisa maidan hai jisay Khuda barah-e-raast dekhta hai aur rehnumayi karta hai, aur iske kaashtkar ke taur par biwi ka kirdaar ek muqaddas zimmedari hai.

Uski hikmat-e-amli ek aisi taqat hai jo poori Ummat ki rehnumayi kar sakti hai. Sulah Hudaibiya ke baad, jab community apne sabse bade siyasi buhraan mein thi, Sahaba gham se nidhaal thay. Buhraan ka hal kisi awami chowk mein nahi, balki unki biwi, Umm Salamah (RA) ke khaimay mein nikla. Unke shandar mashwaray ne — ke Aap ﷺ pehle khud amal karein, taake doosre pairwi karein — ta'attul ko toda aur community ko bacha liya. Is lamhay mein, Rabbat al-Bayt koi ghair-fa'aal **Gharelu Khatoon** nahi thi; woh Ummat ke rehnuma ki **Wazeer** (Chief Counselor) thi.

Aur uski jismani mehnat ukhrawi ajr ka raasta hai. Jab unki pyari beti, Syeda Fatima (RA), haath chakkii peesne ki wajah se zakhmi hone par unke paas aayin, to Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne duniyawi aasaish nahi di. Unhone unke ghar ka daura kiya aur unhein ek aisa tohfa diya jo "khadim se behtar" tha: sone se pehle padhi jane wali **Tasbih**. Yeh mehez tasalli nahi thi; yeh gehra roohani **Keemiya-gari** (alchemy) ka amal tha. Unhone unhein sikhaya ke kaise apne gharelu kaam ki jismani thakawat ko azeem roohani taqat ke zariye mein badla jaye, aur chakki peesne ke kaam ko ibadat ke darjay tak buland kiya jaye.

Yun, Nabvi Namuna mukammal hota hai. Yeh humein woh biwi deta hai jiski roohani halat Wahi ke qabil hai, woh biwi jiski aqal poori Ummat ko buhraan se bacha sakti hai, aur woh biwi jiski duniyawi mehnat ukhrawi ajr mein badal jati hai. Yeh Rabbat al-Bayt ka huma-geer vision hai — naukar nahi, kamanay wali nahi, balki Muslim ghar ka roohani, fikri, aur jismani dil. Yeh woh izzat hai jo kho gayi thi, aur yeh woh izzat hai jo humein bahal karni hai.

*   **Tak:** Qadar ko **Sakinah** mein mapna.

*   **Se:** Ghar bataur **Karkhana**.
*   **Tak:** Ghar bataur panah-gaah.

*   **Tak:** Muqaddas fun ki qayadat.

**Do Duayein:**

Is fani duniya mein panah-gaah ki kaashtkari ka hatmi maqsad agli duniya mein hamare mustaqil ghar ki bunyad rakhna hai. Haqeeqi Rabbat al-Bayt woh hai jis ka dil uske abdi thikanay se juda ho, bhale hi uske haath duniya ke kaam mein masroof hon. Is usool ki is se badi koi misaal nahi milti siwaye Asiyah (AS), Firaun ki biwi ke. Agarche woh zameen ke sabse zalim ghar mein rehti thin, lekin unki rooh pehle hi apne Rab ke paas reh rahi thi. Lehaza unki dua har panah-gaah ke kaashtkar ke liye hatmi dua hai:

Hamare gharon ko sirf makan nahi, balki zameen par Teri salamti ke safarat-khanay bana de, aur is duniyawi panah-gaah ko hamare haqeeqi ghar ke liye pehla qadam bana: Jannat mein Tere qareeb ek ghar. Ameen."

### **Baab 5: Insaaf ka Tawazun: Meesaq aur Rishtedar**
**(Chapter 5: The Just Balance: The Covenant and the Kin)**

#### **[Juz] Bunyadi Usool (Al-Asl)**
**([Component] The Anchor)**

Momin ka dil aksar Islam ki do muqaddas tareen wafadariyon ke liye maidan-e-jung bana rehta hai. Pehla maazi ka na-qabil-e-ada qarz hai, yani walidain ki ta'zeem aur khidmat ka meesaq. Doosra mustaqbil ka na-tootne wala wada hai, yani shareek-e-hayat ke saath husn-e-sulook aur dekh-bhaal ka meesaq. Quran dono faraiz ko mutlaq (absolute) ikhtiyar ke saath qaim karta hai.

**Pehla Meesaq: Maazi ka Qarz**
**Maakhuz:** Quran, Surah Al-Isra, 17:23
> وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعْبُدُوا إِلَّا إِيَّاهُ وَبِالْوَالِدَيْنِ إِحْسَانًا
> **"Aur tere Rab ne faisla kar diya hai ke tum uske siwa kisi ki ibadat na karo aur walidain ke saath Ihsan (bhalayi) karo."**

**Doosra Meesaq: Mustaqbil ka Wada**
**Maakhuz:** Quran, Surah An-Nisa, 4:19
> ...وَعَاشِرُوهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ...
> **"...Aur unke saath bhallay tareeqay (Ma'roof) se zindagi basar karo..."**

Jab maazi ka qarz mustaqbil ke wade se mutasadim (conflicting) nazar aata hai, to momin ka imtehaan "Munsifana Wafadari" ke aala fun mein hota hai. Yeh baab ek meesaq ko doosre par tarjeeh dene ke bare mein nahi hai. Yeh us Khudayi Grammar ko seekhne ke bare mein hai jo humein dono ka ehtram karne ki ijazat deti hai.

---

#### **[Juz] Wazahat (Al-Bayan)**
**([Component] The Illumination)**

Walidain ki ta'zeem ke hukum aur shareek-e-hayat ke saath husn-e-sulook ke hukum ke darmiyan phans jana aisa mehsoos ho sakta hai jaise rooh do hisson mein phat rahi ho. Yeh ehsas ke aap wafadariyon ki jung mein phans gaye hain, izdawaji meesaq ke sabse takleef-deh imtehanat mein se ek hai. Yeh ek **Jhoota Tazaad** hai, jo Shaitan aur saqafati jahalat ka bichaya hua jaal hai, jo momin ko ek ghair-Islami intekhab pesh karta hai: **Tumhari Maa ya Tumhari Biwi.**

Khudayi Manhaj is intekhab ko mustarad karta hai. Woh chabi jo is qaid-khanay ko kholti hai, hamare Muqaddas Qanoon mein ek bunyadi farq hai, be-panah wazahat aur rehmat ka usool: **"Al-Birr"** aur **"At-Ta'ah"** ke darmiyan farq.

**Al-Birr** Neki aur Husn-e-Sulook hai. Yeh walidain ke saath izzat, ehtram, naram guftagu, maali madad, aur be-los khidmat ka wasee samundar hai. Khudayi Manhaj mein, "Al-Birr" ka farz **Mutlaq aur Ghair-Mashroot** hai. Yeh woh qarz hai jo kabhi mukammal taur par ada nahi kiya ja sakta. Yahan tak ke agar walidain ghair-Muslim hon jo apne bacchay ko Allah se door karne ki koshish karein, tab bhi Quran hukum deta hai ke duniya mein unke saath "Ma'roof" (achay tareeqay) se pesh aaya jaye.

**At-Ta'ah**, taham, Itaat (Obedience) hai. Yeh kisi hukum ki pairwi karne ka makhsoos amal hai. Aur Khudayi Manhaj mein, kisi bhi makhlooq ki itaat, bashمول walidain, **Mashroot aur Mehdood** hai. Iski hadd mutlaq aur atal hai: Allah ﷻ ka Qanoon.

Lehaza momin ka raasta yeh hai: **Mashroot "Itaat" ke saath Ghair-Mashroot "Birr".**

Yeh is jhootay tazaad ka hal hai. Insaan ko har haal mein apne walidain ke liye meherbani, khidmat aur izzat ka paikar banay rehna chahiye. Lekin agar koi walidain apne bacchay ko gunah ka hukum dein, ya kisi doosre ke khilaf na-insaafi (Zulm) ka hukum dein — jaise ke apni biwi ke saath ghair-munsifana sulook karna — to itaat na sirf zaroori nahi; balki haram hai. Is hukum ki tameel karna meesaq ki muqaddas hudood se tajawuz karna aur us "Amanat" ke haqooq ko pamal karna hoga jo uska shareek-e-hayat hai.

#### **[Juz] Wirsa (Al-Turath)**
**([Component] The Heritage)**

Mashroot itaat ka usool koi jadeed riayat nahi hai. Yeh Sunni aqeeday ka bunyadi rukn hai. Hamari riwayat ke azeem Aimma ne isay mashwaray ke taur par nahi, balki ek mutlaq usool ke taur par bayan kiya.

**Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal**, apni *Musnad* mein, woh faisla-kun usool naqal karte hain jo us talwar ka kaam karta hai jo mutasadim wafadariyon ki girah ko kaat deti hai:

**Maakhuz:** *Musnad Ahmad*.
> لَا طَاعَةَ لِمَخْلُوقٍ فِي مَعْصِيَةِ الْخَالِقِ
> **"Khaliq ki na-farmani mein kisi makhlooq ki koi itaat nahi."**

Jabke riwayat yeh talwar faraham karti hai, iski gehri hikmat **Hifazat** mein hai. Azeem Tabi'i, **Al-Hasan al-Basri**, ne hatmi hifazati mashwara diya:

**Maakhuz:** Al-Hasan al-Basri ki hikmat.
> **"Apni beti ka nikah 'Taqwa' wale shakhs se karo, kyunke agar woh us se mohabbat karega to uski izzat karega, aur agar usay na-pasand karega to us par Zulm nahi karega."**

Riwayat ki hikmat mukammal hai. Yeh dhaal aur talwar dono faraham karti hai. Dhaal Hasan Basri ki hikmat hai: Meesaq ko Taqwa ki bunyad par tameer karna. Aur talwar Imam Ahmad ka usool hai: har us hukum ki na-farmani ka haq jo na-insaafi ki taraf le jaye.

#### **[Juz] Ishtiaal (Al-Istifzaz)**
**([Component] The Provocation)**

Munsifana wafadari ke usoolon ka imtehaan khala mein nahi hota; yeh haqeeqi insani rishton ki aag mein tapaye jate hain. Meesaq aur Rishtedaron ke darmiyan jagah aksar jazbati pecheedgiyon ka maidan hoti hai. Darj zail manzar-namon par gaur karein.

**Manzar-nama 1: Hadd se badhne wali Buzurg Khatoon (Rassa-kashi)**
Ek naujawan joda shohar ki maa (saas) ke saath rehta hai. Maa koi **Manfi Kirdaar** (Negative Character) nahi hai; woh ek mukhtalif nasal ki khatoon hai jo apni mohabbat aur fikr ka izhaar apni bahu par musalsal "madadgar" tanqeed ke zariye karti hai. Bahu ki tarbiyat bahut narm hai, uske haath mein zaiqa nahi hai, woh shohar ki khidmat kafi nahi kar rahi. Bahu mehsoos karti hai ke woh apne hi ghar mein mehman hai, aur bataur "Rabbat al-Bayt" uske ikhtiyar ko musalsal kamzor kiya ja raha hai. Woh apne shohar se iltija karti hai ke woh apni maa se baat kare, koi hadd muqarrar kare. Shohar maflooj (paralyzed) hai. Woh apni maa se mohabbat karta hai aur khuloos-e-niyat se yaqeen rakhta hai ke unki ta'zeem uska bunyadi mazhabi fareeza hai. Woh apni biwi se kehta hai, *"Woh meri maa hain. Bas sabr karo. Cheezon ko mushkil mat banao."*

*   **Ishtiaal:** Shohar samajhta hai ke uski khamoshi apni maa ke saath "Birr" (neki) ka amal hai. Lekin kya aisa hai? Ya yeh apni biwi ke saath "Zulm" ka amal hai, bataur "Qawwam" uski hifazat karne aur usay "Sakinah" ka ghar faraham karne ke farz mein barah-e-raast nakami? Apni maa ke liye apne farz ki naqis tafheem ko tarjeeh de kar, kya usne apni biwi ke saath "Ma'roof" ke saath rehne ke wazeh hukum ki khilaf-warzi ki hai? Kya uska apni biwi se sabr ka mutalba Taqwa ka amal hai, ya yeh munsifana tawazun qaim karne ki himmat na hone ko chupane ki dhaal hai?

**Manzar-nama 2: Maali Uljhao (The Financial Entanglement)**
Ek naujawan joda biwi ke walid ka shukr-guzar hai, jinhone unke ghar ke liye maali madad ki. Taham, yeh sakhawat an-kahi shara'it ke saath aayi hai. Susar mehsoos karte hain ke unhein is shadi ki **faisla-saazi mein mustaqil maqam** mil gaya hai. Woh har cheez par na-mangay, zabardast mashwaray dete hain: shohar ke career ke faislay, bacchon ki parvarish, chuttiyon ke **mansubay**. Jab joda koi aisa faisla karta hai jis se woh ittefaq nahi karte, to woh apni "mayusi" ka izhaar karte hain, unhein yaad dilate hain ke unhone unke liye kya kuch kiya hai. Joda mehsoos karta hai ke woh phans gaye hain, apne "Diyaar" (Embassy) ke liye khud-mukhtar faislay karne se qasir hain.

*   **Ishtiaal:** Quran rishtedaron par kharch karne ki tareef karta hai, lekin kya yeh is kharch ko control ke aalay ke taur par istemaal karne ki ijazat deta hai? Kis mod par walidain ki sakhawat tohfa nahi rehti aur ek len-den ban jati hai jo ikhtiyar khareedti hai? Madad qabool karke, kya jode ne anjane mein apni Khuda-daad **Ikhtiyar** (Agency) aur apne Meesaq ki azadi ka kuch hissa bech diya hai?

#### **[Juz] Ilaaj (Al-Tiryaq): Munsifana Wafadari ke Teen Sutoon**
**([Component] The Antidote: The Three Pillars of Just Allegiance)**

Is uljhay hue manzar-namay se guzarne ka raasta koi ek hukum nahi, balki muqaddas faraiz ka ek majmua hai. Jab yeh teen sutoon qaim ho jate hain, to ghar mazbooti se khada rehta hai.

**1. Jode ki Dhaal: Mutahidda Mahaz (The United Front)**
Kisi bhi kamiyab hikmat-e-amli ki bunyad ittehad hai. Shaitan ka pasandeeda harba "taqseem karo aur fatah karo" hai, taake Meesaq ke "Hum" ko "Main ba-muqabla Tum" mein badal de. Wahid difaa yeh hai ke joda ek thos, **Mutahidda Mahaz** banaye. Yeh unki mushtarqa dhaal hai.
*   **Niji Mushawarat, Awami Ittehad:** Tamam bade khandani faislay aur hassas masail par pehle niji taur par baat cheet aur ittefaq kiya jana chahiye. Wasee khandan ke "awami" stage par qadam rakhne se pehle jode ko ek mutahidda team hona chahiye.
*   **"Hum" ki Taqat:** Ek baar faisla ho jane ke baad, usay ek unit ke taur par bataya jana chahiye. Unhein apni lughat se *"Meri biwi nahi chahti..."* ya *"Mere shohar ka khayal hai..."* jaise jumlay nikal dene chahiye. Yeh jumlay khandan ke afraad ko dawat dete hain ke woh ek shareek-e-hayat ko "masla" samjhein aur dabao dalein. Sahi, dhaal banane wala jumla hamesha yeh hota hai: **"Hum ne faisla kiya hai ke..."** ya **"Hum mehsoos karte hain ke yeh behtar hoga agar..."**. Yeh ek na-tootne wala mahaz pesh karta hai.

**2. Shohar ka Bojh: Aadil Sutoon (The Just Pillar)**
Ittehad ki dhaal qaim hone ke baad, bairooni tawazun barqarar rakhne ki bunyadi zimmedari shohar par aati hai. Woh woh sutoon hai jo do Meesaqon ko jodta hai. Uska kirdaar ghair-fa'aal judge banna nahi, balki ek fa'aal aur aadil tawazun qaim karne wala hai.
*   **Woh Biwi ka Muhafiz hai:** Uski biwi uski **Amanat** hai. Ghar ke andar uska pehla farz uski jazbati, roohani aur jismani falah-o-behbood ko yaqeeni banana hai. Is mein usay ghair-munsifana tanqeed aur ghair-haqeeqi tawaquaat se bachana shamil hai, yahan tak ke uske apne pyare khandan se bhi. Woh uski dhaal hai. Uske saath na-insaafi ke samne uski khamoshi parhezgari nahi; yeh uski Qiwamiyat ki nakami hai.
*   **Woh Walidain ka Khadim hai:** Apne walidain ke saath uska "Birr" ka farz mutlaq hai. Usay unki dekh-bhaal, izzat aur khidmat ka bunyadi agent hona chahiye. Usay fa'aal taur par unke liye makhsoos waqt aur wasail waqf karne chahiye, is baat ko yaqeeni banate hue ke woh mohabbat aur tahaffuz mehsoos karein, na ke la-waris.
*   **Woh Alfaaz ka Muhafiz hai (Hafiz-ul-Kalam):** Usay fitnay ke khilaf rukawat banna chahiye. Woh apni maa ki baatein apni biwi tak, ya apni biwi ki baatein apni maa tak nahi pohanchata. Woh samajhta hai ke har shikayat ko aage pohanchane ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Woh dono fareeqon ki mamooli mayusiyon ko jazb kar leta hai, aur narazgi ki chingariyon ko ghar ki aag ka indhan banne se rokta hai.

**3. Biwi ka Fun: Ba-Waqar Safeer (The Gracious Ambassador)**
Jabke shohar Aadil Sutoon hai, biwi Ba-Waqar Safeer hai. Uska kirdaar ghair-fa'aal bardasht ka nahi, balki Allah ki khatir aur apne ghar ke aman ke liye fa'aal aur strategic pul banane ka hai.
*   **Fa'aal Meherbani:** Woh meherbani dikhaye jane ka intezaar nahi karti; woh iski pehal karti hai. Apni saas ke liye ek chota, socha samjha tohfa, unki sehat poochne ke liye phone call, ya unki hikmat ki mukhlasana tareef mohabbat ke taqatwar beej hain. Yeh us Qurani usool ko mujassam karne ka aala fun hai: *"Burayi ko us cheez se door karo jo behtar hai."* Uski fa'aal achayi, Allah ke fazl se, aag ko bhadakne se pehle hi bujha sakti hai.
*   **Husn-e-Zan:** Jab sasural ki taraf se koi tabsira mubham ya thoda tanqeedi mehsoos ho, to uska pehla rad-e-amal behtareen mumkinah niyat farz karna hona chahiye. Nafs foran bad-tareen nateejay ki taraf chalang lagayega. Ek safeer ka dil **Husn-e-Zan** (Benefit of the doubt) deta hai, aur mumkinah tanazaat ko us oxygen se mehroom kar deta hai jiski unhein bhadakne ke liye zaroorat hoti hai.
*   **Panah-gaah ki Hifazat:** Uska bunyadi mission "Sakinah" ki kaashtkari hai. Woh samajhti hai ke mamooli ladaiyon mein ulajhna, chahe woh haq par hi kyun na ho, uske apne ghar ki fiza ko zehreela kar sakta hai. Uska waqar kamzori ki alamat nahi; yeh uski taqat ka muzahira hai aur apni panah-gaah ke aman ki hifazat ke liye ek strategic intekhab hai.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Walidain ke haqooq aur aala sachayi ke haqooq ke darmiyan is takleef-deh tazad se guzarne ka hatmi namuna mehez Seerat ki koi kahani nahi; yeh ek aisa amal hai jisay Quran ne khud la-zawal banaya aur tasdeeq ki. Yeh sabse pehle aur sabse mehboob Sahaba mein se ek, **Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (RA)** ki kahani hai.

Jab Sa'd ne Islam qabool kiya, to unki maa, jin se woh be-panah mohabbat karte thay, toot gayin. Unhone **Jazbati Dabao** ki intehayi shakal ikhtiyar ki, qasam khayi ke woh khana peena chhod dengi jab tak ke woh apne deen se phir na jayen, chahe iska matlab unki maut hi kyun na ho. Is ne Sa'd ko ek na-mumkin position mein daal diya: apni maa se aqeedat ba-muqabla apne Khaliq se aqeedat.

Unka jawab munsifana wafadari ka masterclass tha. Woh dagmagaye nahi. Unhone unhein meherbani aur fikr dikhana jari rakha, lekin bunyadi maslay par, woh atal thay. Unhone unhein kaha, dil tootne ke bawajood lekin pukhta imaan ke saath: *"Aye meri maa! Allah ki qasam, agar aap ki sau (100) jaanein hotin aur woh ek ek karke aap ke jism se nikaltin, tab bhi main kisi cheez ke liye apna Deen nahi chhodta."*

Azeem imtehaan ke is lamhay mein, Allah ﷻ ne ek Khudayi faisla nazil kiya, jis ne Sa'd ke amal ki tasdeeq ki aur qayamat tak tamam momineen ke liye aafaqi usool qaim kar diya. Surah Luqman mein, Allah farmata hai:

> *"Lekin agar woh tujh par dabao dalein ke Tu mere saath us cheez ko shareek thehraye jis ka tujhe koi ilm nahi, to unki itaat na kar, lekin duniya mein unke saath **Ma'roof** (achay tareeqay) se saath nibha..."* (Quran, 31:15)

Yeh **Mashroot Itaat ke saath Ghair-Mashroot Birr** ke usool ka zinda namuna hai.

Sa'd ne apni maa ke gunahgarana hukum (Deen chhodne) ki na-farmani (*La Ta'ah*) ka hatmi amal kiya. Phir bhi usi ayat mein, Allah ne unhein munsifana meherbani (*Sahib-huma fid-dunya ma'roofa*) ka aala mayaar barqarar rakhne ka hukum diya. Unhein unka hukum manne se inkaar karna tha lekin unka mohabbat karne wala, khayal rakhne wala aur farman-bardar beta bane rehna tha.

Yeh woh Khudayi Grammar hai jo jhootay tazaad ko hal karti hai. Yeh humein sikhati hai ke na-insaafi ke khilaf mazboot hadd qaim karna — chahe woh na-insaafi Shirk ki dawat ho ya shareek-e-hayat ke saath zalimana sulook ka hukum — be-adabi ka amal nahi hai. Yeh Allah ke saath hatmi wafadari ka amal hai. Aur yeh humein sikhati hai ke is mazboot hadd ko ghussay ya nafrat ke saath nahi, balki ek aise dil ke saath thama jana chahiye jo rehmat aur munsifana meherbani ke liye koshish karta rahe.

#### **[Juz] Mohar (Al-Khatm)**
**([Component] The Seal)**

**Manhaj ki Tabdeeli:**

*   **Se:** Maa aur Biwi ke darmiyan ek jhoota intekhab.
*   **Tak:** Do muqaddas meesaqon ke darmiyan ek munsifana tawazun.

*   **Se:** Walidain ki ghair-mashroot itaat.
*   **Tak:** Mashroot itaat (*Ta'ah*) ke saath ghair-mashroot husn-e-sulook (*Birr*).

*   **Se:** Shohar bataur khamosh tamashayi.
*   **Tak:** Shohar bataur **Aadil Sutoon**.

*   **Se:** Joda bataur taqseem-shuda wajood.
*   **Tak:** Joda bataur **Mutahidda Mahaz**.

*   **Se:** Mohabbaton ka tazaad.
*   **Tak:** Mohabbaton ki tarteeb, jis mein Allah ki mohabbat sabse aala hai.

**Do Duayein:**

Munsifana tawazun ko talash karna mehez ek sard, qanooni hisaab kitaab nahi; yeh dil ki wafadariyon ka gehra imtehaan hai. Yeh do jaiz mohabbaton ki aazmaish hai. Wahid qutub-numa jo rooh ki is ilaqay mein rehnumayi kar sakta hai woh teesri, badi mohabbat hai jo baqi sab par hukumat karti hai: Allah ki mohabbat. Jab Khuda ki mohabbat dil ka haqeeqi "North" hoti hai, to baqi tamam mohabbatein — walidain ke liye, shareek-e-hayat ke liye, zaat ke liye — apne munasib, munsifana aur khubsurat madar (orbit) mein aa jati hain. Lehaza, hum is baab ka ikhtitam Nabi Kareem ﷺ ki apni dua ke saath karte hain:

*   **Nabvi Dua (Allah ki Mohabbat ke liye):**
    > *Allahumma inni as'aluka hubbaka wa hubba man yuhibbuka wal-'amal alladhi yuballighuni hubbaka...*
    > **"Aye Allah, main Tujh se Teri mohabbat mangta hoon, aur unki mohabbat jo Tujh se mohabbat karte hain, aur us amal ki mohabbat jo mujhe Teri mohabbat tak pohancha de..."** (Jami' at-Tirmidhi)

*   **Sada Zaban mein Dua (Munsifana Tawazun ke liye):**
    > "Aye Allah, **Al-Hakam** (Hatmi Faisla karne wale), hamare dilon mein munsifana tawazun qaim kar de. Humein hikmat ata farma ke hum apne walidain ki izzat ek khubsurat, ghair-mashroot 'Birr' ke saath karein, aur Tere Qanoon ki hudood par mazbooti se qaim rehne ki himmat ata farma. Humein qata-rehmi (rishta todne) ke gunah se, aur us se bade gunah yani us meesaq ke saath na-insaafi se بچا (bacha) jo Tu ne hamari amanat mein diya hai. Teri mohabbat woh qutub-numa ho jo baqi tamam mohabbaton ki rehnumayi kare. Hamare do dilon ko Taqwa ke **Mutahidda Mahaz** mein dhaal de, taake hamara ghar aman ki panah-gaah ho, mutasadim wafadariyon ke hangamay se pak, sirf Teri taraf hamari hatmi wafadari ka saboot. Ameen."

## Teesra Darwaza: Muqaddas Maqsad (Al-Ghayah) - Manzil
**(Gate III: The Sacred Purpose - The Destination)**

### **Baab 6: Diyaar-e-Rehmat ka Maqsad**
**(Chapter 6: The Embassy's Mission)**

#### **[Juz] Bunyadi Usool (Al-Asl)**
**([Component] The Anchor)**

**Maakhuz:** Quran, Surah Al-Furqan, 25:74

**Arabi Matn:**
> وَالَّذِينَ يَقُولُونَ رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

**Tarjuma:**
> "Aur woh log jo dua karte hain: 'Aye hamare Rab! Humein hamari biwiyon aur hamari aulad se aankhon ki thandak ata farma, aur humein parhezgaron ka **Imam** (Peshwa/Rehnuma) bana.'"

#### **[Juz] Wazahat (Al-Bayan)**
**([Component] The Illumination)**

Allah ﷻ hamare wajood ka hatmi maqsad ek wahid, wazeh bayan mein farmata hai: *"Maine Jinn aur Insaano ko iske siwa kisi kaam ke liye paida nahi kiya ke woh meri ibadat karein."* (Al-Quran 51:56). Yeh ibadat mehez namaz aur rozay tak mehdood nahi; yeh zindagi ka mukammal rukh hai, jahan rozi kamanay se lekar bacchay ki parvarish tak har amal Allah ki raza ke liye ki jane wali aqeedat mein badal jata hai. Yeh hatmi **Hadaf** (Mission) hai.

Is azeem, aafaqi maqsad ka itlaq hamare gharon ki niji fiza aur hamari shadi ke meesaq par kaise hota hai? Jadeed duniya ne humein sikhaya hai ke shadi ko ek alag **Mansubay** (Project) ke taur par dekhein, zaati khushi ya khandan banane ki niji koshish. Lekin **Khudayi Manhaj** mein, shadi ka maqsad zindagi ke maqsad se alag nahi hai; yeh iski sabse taqatwar gariyon mein se ek hai.

Quran khud humein sabse zyada nek momineen, yani "Ibad-ur-Rehman" ki dua mein jawab دیتا (deta) hai. Woh dua karte hain: *"Aye hamare Rab, humein hamari biwiyon aur aulad se aankhon ki thandak ata farma, aur humein parhezgaron ka Imam bana."*

Yeh maqsad apni kamil, mutawazan shakal mein hai. Yeh is duniya ki jaiz khushiyon ki khwahish se shuru hota hai — ek nek khandan ka gehra sukoon aur khushi jo "aankhon ki thandak" lati hai. Lekin yeh wahin nahi rukta. Yeh hairat-angez azaim ki darkhwast par muntaj hota hai: Khuda se darne walon ke liye **"Imam"** banna. Imam ek rehnuma, ek nishan, ek mayaar hota hai. Yeh safeer banne ki dua hai. Yeh pooray khandani unit ke liye ek fa'aal **"Diyaar-e-Rehmat"** (Abode of Mercy) banne ki iltija hai, jo itna hidayat-yafta aur Allah ke samne tasleem hone mein itna khubsurat ho ke woh poori community ke liye roshni aur rehnumayi ka zariya ban jaye.

Yeh Khudayi Manhaj ka **Aakhirat-Markooz** (Akhirah-centric) maqsad hai: ek niji ghar ki tabdeeli ek awami "Emaan ke Minar" mein. Is se kam kisi bhi maqsad ko qabool karna us izzat ko bunyadi taur par na-samajhna hai jo pesh ki ja rahi hai.

#### **[Juz] Ilaaj (Al-Tiryaq)**
**([Component] The Antidote)**

Khudayi Manhaj "Teesra Raasta" pesh karta hai, ek haqeeqi maqsad jo un do azeem duniyawi bigaad ke bar-aks khada hai jinhone shadi ke maqsad ko **Yargamaal** (hijack) kar liya hai.

Pehla **"Saqafati Pinjra"** hai aur iska maqsad **"Khandani Wirasat"** (Dynastic Legacy) hai. Yeh zamana-e-jahiliyat ki goonj hai, duniyawi but-parasti ki ek shakal jo shadi ki kamiyabi ko is salahiyat se mapti hai ke woh naam ke liye waris paida kare, nasl-dar-nasl daulat jama kare, aur khandan ki samaji haisiyat ko barhaye. Islami shadi ka maqsad duniyawi saltanat ke liye waris paida karna nahi, balki Allah ﷻ ke liye mukhlis ibadat-guzar paida karna hai. Wahid haqeeqi wirasat woh nek bacchah hai jo aap ki maut ke baad aap ke liye dua kare. Yeh khoon ki lakeeron aur duniyawi izzat ka pinjra hai, jo muqaddas meesaq ko maazi mein qaid kar deta hai.

Doosra, aur shayad zyada makkar bigaad, **"Bazari Zehniyat"** hai aur iska maqsad **"Zaati Taskeen"** (Personal Fulfillment) hai. Yeh jadeed manhaj, jo insaan ko ek sarif (consumer) samajhta hai, zaati khushi ko hatmi hadaf banata hai. Shareek-e-hayat ek **Khidmatguzar** (Service Provider) hai, aur shadi ek muahida hai jisay sirf tab tak barqarar rakha jana chahiye jab tak ke woh **Jazbati Munafa** (Emotional Profit) deta rahe. Yeh **Nafs** ki but-parasti ki ek bareek lekin tabah-kun shakal hai. Yeh khushi ko hadaf aur Allah ko us tak pohanchne ka zariya banata hai. Yeh zaat ke bazaar ka bunyadi len-den hai, jahan Allah ko apni khushi ke mansubay mein ek mamooli hissedar bana diya jata hai.

Nabvi "Teesra Raasta" haqeeqi basarat bahal karta hai. Yeh **"Shareek-fil-Taqwa"** (Partnership in Piety) ka manhaj hai. Is muqaddas sharakat mein, miyan biwi sirf aashiq nahi hain; woh sabse ahem safar par team ke saathi hain. Unka hatmi, mushtarqa maqsad Allah ki raza aur Jannat ka husool hai.

Yeh manhaj har izdawaji maamlay ko naye siray se tarteeb deta hai:
*   Ikhtilaf ab jeetne ki jung nahi, balki mushtarqa maqsad ke liye khatra hai jisay hikmat se hal karna zaroori hai.
*   Shareek-e-hayat ki khami par sabr koi bojh nahi; yeh Aakhirat ke liye nekiyon ke mushtarqa account mein taweel-mudati sarmaya-kari hai.
*   Apne saathi ko Fajr ke liye jagana **Nuktacheeni** (nagging) nahi hai; yeh ek wafadar saathi ka amal hai jo apne mushtarqa safar ki kamiyabi ko yaqeeni bana raha hai.

Ghar ab duniyawi aaram ke liye resort nahi, balki "Diyaar-e-Rehmat" ka **Markaz** (Headquarters) hai. Yeh Diyaar ka mission hai: shadi ko duniya mein aarzi qayam-gaah se badal kar Aakhirat mein mustaqil sarmaya-kari banana.

#### **[Juz] Uboori Pul (The Transitional Bridge)**

Ek poori zindagi aur ek pooray ghar ka rukh Aakhirat ki taraf modna insani koshishon mein sabse mushkil kaam hai. Yeh duniya aur nafs ki **Bunyadi Tarteeb** (default settings) ke khilaf elaan-e-jung hai. Aisa mission sirf nazriye (theory) se qaim nahi reh sakta. Isay ek zinda, saans lete hue namunay ki zaroorat hai, ek aisa manhaj jo Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ke jism aur rooh mein mujassam ho, jinhone humein dikhaya ke ghar haqeeqat mein kaisa lagta hai jab uska qutub-numa (compass) abadiyat par set ho.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Aakhirat-Markooz mission ka Nabvi Namuna koi ek usool nahi, balki ek mukammal nizam (ecosystem) hai. Iska ek **Androoni Culture** hai jo iski fiza ki tareef karta hai, aur ek **Bairooni Amal** hai jo iski harkat ki tareef karta hai. Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ne dono ko mukammal wazahat ke saath dikhaya.

Pehle, unhone "Diyaar" ka androoni culture qaim kiya. Yeh Nabvi hujray ki wazeh **Sadgi** (Asceticism) mein nazar aata hai, ek aisi jagah jo jaan boojh kar is duniya ki fani **Zeenat** (adornments) se khali thi. Jab Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) Nabi Kareem ﷺ ke paas aaye aur unhein ek khurdari chatayi par letay hue dekha jis ne unke mubarak pehlu par nishan chhod diye thay, to woh rone lagay. *"Ya Rasool Allah,"* unhone kaha, *"Qaisar-o-Kisra aisi aish-o-ishrat mein rehte hain, jabke aap..."* Nabi Kareem ﷺ, jo tek lagaye hue thay, uth kar baith gaye aur apne gharanay ka **Manshoor** (Manifesto) bayan kiya: *"Aye Ibn-e-Khattab! Kya tum shak mein ho? ...Kya tum is par raazi nahi ke unke liye duniya ho, aur hamare liye Aakhirat?"*

Yeh Nabvi gharanay ka zinda **Nisaab** (curriculum) tha. Diyaar-e-Rehmat ka **Androoni Mahol** iski roohani tarjeehaat ka jismani izhaar tha. Bataur **Qawwam**, Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne ghair-duniyawi tawajjo ka culture parwan charhaya, apne khandan ko lectures se nahi, balki apni zinda misaal ki na-qabil-e-tardeed sachayi se sikhaya.

Is "duniya se be-raghbati" ke androoni culture se Diyaar ka bairooni amal phoot-ta hai: iska **Bunyadi Hadaf** (Prime Directive). Allah ﷻ is mission ka hukum ek khaufnaak aur bedaar kar dene wali wazahat ke saath deta hai:

> يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا قُوا أَنفُسَكُمْ وَأَهْلِيكُمْ نَارًا
> **"Aye Emaan walo! Bachao apne aap ko aur apne ghar walon ko (Jahannum ki) aag se..."** (Quran 66:6)

Hukum *"Qu"* hai — ek fa'aal, taqatwar fail (verb) jis ka matlab hai "hifazat karna, dhaal banna, bachana." Yeh hatmi **Hifazati Muhim** (Security Mission) hai. Do mushtarqa safeeron — miyan biwi — ka bunyadi mushtarqa farz yeh yaqeeni banana hai ke woh aur unki zer-e-kafalat afraad hatmi musibat se bach jayen.

Yeh do manhaj alag haqeeqatein nahi hain; yeh sabab aur nateeja hain. Duniya se be-raghbati ka androoni culture, Aakhirat ki nijat ke bairooni mission ke liye zaroori shart hai. Jo gharana duniya ki zeenat ki mohabbat mein mubtila ho, woh khud ko Aakhirat ki aag se bachane ke bare mein sanjeeda nahi ho sakta. Apne khandan ko aag se bachane ka pehla amal unke dilon se iska indhan nikalna hai. Yeh hai Diyaar ka hatmi maqsad: zameen par wirasat banana nahi, balki apne pooray khandan ko Khuda ke paas wapas lana.

#### **[Juz] Safeer ki Kasauti (The Ambassador's Litmus Test)**

Khudayi Manhaj dekhne ke baad, safeeron ko ab apne safarat-khanay ka jaiza lena hoga. Yeh maazi ki nakamiyon ka audit nahi, balki mustaqbil ki hum-aahangi (alignment) ke liye tashkhees hai.
*(Shart: Apne maazi ko is test se na parakhein; sirf apni mustaqbil ki niyat ko parakhein.)*

Apne aap se khuloos ke saath poochein:

*   **Waqt ka Imtehaan:** Hamara mushtarqa, ghair-masroof waqt kahan jata hai? Kya hamara **Jadwal** (Schedule) Allah ki taraf ruju karne wali sharakat-dari ki akasi karta hai, ya duniyawi masroofiyat ke anbaar ki?
*   **Maal ka Imtehaan:** Hamari zarooriyat ke baad, hamara izafi maal kahan jata hai? Kya hamare **Akhrajat** hamari Aakhirat mein mushtarqa sarmaya-kari ki akasi karte hain, ya fani duniyawi dikhaway ke peeche bhagne ki?
*   **Guftagu ka Imtehaan:** Hamari sabse pur-josh, der raat ki guftagu ka mauzu kya hota hai? Kya hum Aakhirat ke liye apni wirasat ke bare mein mil kar khwab dekh rahe hain, ya sirf agli **Tafreeh** (Vacation) ke mansubon ke bare mein?
*   **Dua ka Imtehaan:** Jab hum mil kar haath uthate hain, to hum kya mangte hain? Kya hamari mushtarqa duayein bunyadi taur par duniyawi aasaish ke liye hain, ya hidayat, maghfirat, aur husn-e-khatima ke liye?

> **(Muhafiz ka Note:** In sawalat ko mehez quiz na samjhein. Yeh roohani tashkhees hain, aap ke meesaq ki rooh ki **Gehri Tashkhees** (Deep Diagnosis). Jawab takleef-deh ho sakte hain. Us takleef se munh na modein. Yeh Allah ki taraf se rehmat hai, raasta seedha karne ki pukaar. Tashkhees ka maqsad muzammat karna nahi, balki ilaaj par majboor karna hai. Aakhri baab woh ilaaj hai.)

#### **[Juz] Mohar (Al-Khatm)**
**([Component] The Seal)**

**Manhaj ki Tabdeeli:**

*   **Se:** Duniya ke liye sharakat-dari.
*   **Tak:** Aakhirat ke liye mushtarqa mansuba.

*   **Se:** Maqsad ko duniyawi asason (assets) mein mapna.
*   **Tak:** Maqsad ko **Saleh Aamaal** mein mapna.

*   **Se:** Shareek-e-hayat bataur zaati taskeen ka zariya.
*   **Tak:** Shareek-e-hayat bataur **Nijat mein Saathi**.

*   **Se:** Ek niji mansuba.
*   **Tak:** Nek logon ke liye ek awami **Diyaar-e-Rehmat**.

**Do Duayein:**

Diyaar ka mission, apne wazeh tareen alfaz mein, ek hifazati operation hai. Iska bunyadi hadaf nijat hasil karna hai, Aakhirat ki aag se apne pooray khandan ke liye **Sifarti Tahaffuz** (Diplomatic Immunity) hasil karna. Yeh mission musalsal bedari aur hatmi musibat se Allah ki panah mangne ka mutalba karta hai. Lehaza, is baab par mohar lagane ke liye sabse mauzoon dua Nabi Kareem ﷺ ki apni, fauri aur jame' panah ki dua hai:

*   **Nabvi Dua (Hatmi Hifazat ke liye):**
    > *Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min 'adhabi jahannam, wa min 'adhabil-qabr, wa min fitnatil-mahya wal-mamat, wa min sharri fitnatil-masihid-dajjal.*
    > **"Aye Allah, main Teri panah mangta hoon Jahannum ke azaab se, aur qabr ke azaab se, aur zindagi aur maut ki aazmaishon se, aur Masih Dajjal ke fitnay ke shar se."** (Sahih Muslim)

*   **Sada Zaban mein Dua (Rehmat ke Safeeron ke liye):**
    > "Aye Allah, **Al-Awwal wal-Akhir** (Pehle aur Aakhri), hamare dilon ka rukh Apni taraf mod de. Hamari shadi ko aisi sharakat bana jis ka bunyadi maqsad Teri raza ho. Hamare ghar ko aisa Diyaar bana jis ka qutub-numa Teri Jannat par set ho. Humein is duniya ki zeenat se bacha jo humein hamara haqeeqi mission bhula de. Humein wafadar saathi bana jo ek doosre ke Emaan ki hifazat karein, jo nekiyon mein ek doosre se aage barhein, aur jo, Teri Rehmat se, Teri Jannat mein ikatthay dakhil hon, aag se mehfooz aur salamti ke saath. Ameen."

### **Baab 7: Meesaq ki Tajdeed (Muhasabah)**
**(Chapter 7: The Covenant Renewal)**

#### **[Juz] Ta'aruf: Tajdeed ki Zaroorat**
**([Component] The Introduction: The Necessity of Renewal)**

Ek shadi, momin ke dil mein Emaan ki tarah, ek zinda wajood hai. Yeh patthar ki koi yaadgar nahi jo ek baar tameer ho jaye aur phir door se uski tareef ki jaye. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne apni gehri hikmat mein humein behtareen istiaara (metaphor) diya. Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Emaan tum mein se kisi ke dil mein purana ho jata hai jaise kapda purana ho jata hai, lehaza Allah se sawal karo ke woh tumhare dilon mein Emaan ki tajdeed kar de."*

Meesaq rooh ka libaas hai. Yeh rozana pehna jata hai. Yeh duniya ki ragad ka samna karta hai, hamari kotahiyon se daaghdaar hota hai, aur hamari ghaflat se phat sakta hai. Is libaas ko baghair **Murammat** (mending) kiye chhod dena duniya mein aise Emaan ke saath chalna hai jo be-naqaab ho aur aisi rooh ke saath jo sard ho. Jadeed dhyan batanay wali cheezon (distractions) ka musalsal toofan aur Zaat ki parastish is bigaad ko tez karti hai.

Lehaza, "Neki mein Sharakat-dari" koi title nahi jis ka dawa kiya jaye, balki ek libaas hai jiski dekh-bhaal ki jaye. Iske liye roohani dekh-bhaal ke ek fa'aal, tay-shuda aur muqaddas **Mamool** (Routine) ki zaroorat hai. Yeh karobari **Karkardagi ka Jaiza** (Performance Review) nahi aur na hi shikayaat ke muqadmay ka forum hai. Yeh hamari riwayat ke muqaddas funoon ki taraf wapsi hai, jo teen sutoonon par tameer hai:

1.  **Al-Muhasabah:** Do sharakat-daron ka naram aur bahami ehtisaab.
2.  **An-Nasiha:** Allah ki khatir mukhlasana, mohabbat bhara mashwara dena.
3.  **Al-Islah:** Durust karne, murammat karne, aur mil kar ek behtar raasta tay karne ka mushtarqa azm.

Yeh baab is "Meesaq ki Tajdeed" ka dhancha faraham karta hai. Yeh hamare Emaan ke libaas ko rafu karne (mending) ke muqaddas amal ke liye rehnuma hai, taake yeh Allah ki taraf hamare mushtarqa safar par garmaish, hifazat, aur khubsurati ka zariya bana rahe.

#### **Pas-e-Manzar**
**(The Framing)**

Meesaq ki tajdeed ka dhancha koi sakht formula nahi, balki ek lachak-daar, zinda aala (tool) hai. Iska itlaq khud Meesaq ki halat ki bunyad par mukhtalif hoga. Ek sehat-mand sharakat-dari ke liye jisay sirf mamooli, roz-marra ke dararon ki murammat ki zaroorat hai, yeh amal naram **Dekh-bhaal** (*Maintenance*) ka hai. Lekin us Meesaq ke liye jo gehre, bunyadi Manhaj ke adam-tawazun ka shikaar hai, yeh amal jurrat-mandana aur aksar takleef-deh **Tajdeed-e-Simt** (*Realignment*) ka ban jata hai. Zail mein diye gaye do khakay mehez kahaniyan nahi; yeh case studies hain. Gaur karein ke ek hi muqaddas technology ko do mukhtalif, lekin yaksaan taur par ahem maqasid hasil karne ke liye kaise istemaal kiya jata hai.

### **Tajdeed ke Khakay (Portraits in Renewal)**

#### **Khaka 1: Dekh-bhaal (A Portrait of Maintenance)**

Yeh Mangal ki raat hai. Bacchay so chuke hain, aur ghar aakhir-kaar pur-sukoon hai. Zayd aur Fatimah apne rehashi kamray mein baithe hain, unke darmiyan is subah hone wale mamooli ikhtilaf ki wajah se ek khamosh, an-kahi kashidgi hai. Woh dono thakay hue hain. "Badi Baat Cheet" ka khayal bhari lagta hai, lekin unhone ek doosre se, aur Allah se wada kiya tha.

Zayd pehle uthta hai. *"Main wuzu karne ja raha hoon,"* woh narmi se kehta hai. Fatimah sar hilati hai aur uske peeche jati hai. Unke chehron aur bazuon par thanda pani na sirf ek rasm poori karta hai; yeh din ki thakawat ko dhona shuru kar deta hai, unke dilon ko kisi muqaddas cheez ke liye tayyar karta hai. Woh kamray ke alag alag konon mein apni do rakatein ada karte hain, unke khamosh sajday ikhlaas ki iltija aur ana ke khilaf dhaal hain.

Jab woh dobara ikatthay baithte hain, to mahol pehle hi badal chuka hai. Zayd pehle bolta hai, uski awaaz thodi hichkichahat wali, lekin saaf hai. *"Is mulaqat ke liye hamari niyat,"* woh unke juday hue haathon ko dekhte hue shuru karta hai, *"Allah ki khatir hamare Meesaq ko mazboot karna, aur Jannat ke raaste par ek doosre ki madad karna hai."*

Woh shukr-guzari se shuru karte hain. Fatimah usay batati hai ke dabao wale kaam ke din ke dauran uske saada paigham ne usay kaisa mehsoos karaya ke uska khayal rakha ja raha hai aur us se mohabbat ki ja rahi hai. Zayd usay batata hai ke usne usay apne bete ko Qurani kahani ki sabr se wazahat karte hue dekha aur uske Emaan ke liye tareef ka gehra ehsas mehsoos kiya. Kamray mein maujood kashidgi pighalne lagti hai, jiski jagah ek naram garmaish le leti hai.

Phir mushkil hissa aata hai. Yeh khud-ehtisabi ke liye Zayd ki baari hai. *"Ek tareeqa jis se main behtar hona chahoonga,"* woh kehta hai, *"woh yeh hai ke jab main pehli baar ghar aaun to aap aur bacchon ke saath zyada hazir rahoon. Main janta hoon ke main aksar mashghool rehta hoon. Main is mein aap ki madad chahta hoon."*

Ab mohabbat bharay mashwaray ke liye Fatimah ki baari hai. *"Ek cheez jo mere dil ko aap ke dil se aur bhi zyada juda hua mehsoos karne mein madad degi,"* woh narmi se kehti hai, *"woh yeh hai ke agar hum ek doosre ki choti ghaltiyon par kam tanqeed karne par mil kar kaam kar sakein."*

Ek lamhay ke liye, Zayd ki ana bhadak uthti hai. Uski zaban par darjano jawabaat aate hain: *"Lekin tum hi to thi jis ne..."* Woh apne seenay mein garmi ki ek lehar mehsoos karta hai. Usay usool yaad aata hai. Woh apni biwi ko dekhta hai, ek saans leta hai jo hathiyar dalne ki tarah hai, aur woh alfaaz kehta hai, khamoshi se lekin wazeh taur par: **"JazakAllah Khair is baat ko share karne ke liye. Main is par gaur karunga."**

Nafs par fatah ke is lamhay mein, kamra ek gehre sukoon se bhar jata hai.

Woh ek chotay, mushtarqa maqsad par muttafiq hote hain: raat ke khanay ke baad tees minute tak, dono phone daraz mein rakh diye jayenge. Woh isay ek chotay card par likhte hain. Aakhir mein, woh dua ke liye haath uthate hain, jo shukr-guzari, tauba aur umeed ka ek khamosh, ghair-rasmi imtizaj hai. Woh uski peshani par bosa de kar khatam karta hai. Unhone har masla hal nahi kiya. Lekin unke Meesaq ka libaas, jo hafte ki ragad se thoda daaghdaar aur bharbhara ho gaya tha, dhoya gaya, murammat kiya gaya, aur dobara khubsurat bana diya gaya. "Sakinah" wapas aa gayi hai.

#### **Khaka 2: Tajdeed-e-Simt (A Portrait of Realignment)**

Bilal aur Sumayyah ki zindagi behtareen lag rahi thi. Woh ek jadeed, kamiyab joda thay, jo ek doosre se gehri mohabbat karte thay, ek khubsurat ghar aur roshan, khush-bash bacchon ke saath. Lekin unke khubsurat ghar mein khamoshi bhari thi. Woh apna mission apne khandan ke liye duniyawi aaram aur kamiyabi ka qila tameer karna samajhta tha, jo uski mehnat ka saboot ho. Woh us qilay ke "Diyaar" banne ki tamanna karti thi, ek aisi jagah jis ka mushtarqa roohani maqsad agli taraqqi ya tazeen-o-araish se aage ho.

Unka Tajdeed ka session kashida hai. Unka wuzu tayyari se kam aur kisi takrao ke liye tayyar hone jaisa lagta hai. Unki shukr-guzari haqeeqi hai lekin inkishaaf karne wali: woh ghar ko itni muassir tareeqay se chalane par uski tareef karta hai, jis se usay apne career par tawajjo markooz karne ka mauqa milta hai; woh us khubsurat zindagi ki tareef karti hai jo woh faraham karta hai. Woh do mukhtalif darakhton ke phalon ki tareef kar rahe hain.

Session aage badhta hai, lekin asal buhraan **Hissa C: Mission ki Halat** par aata hai. Bilal se kaha jata hai ke woh ek mushtarqa roohani maqsad tajweez kare. *"Achay log banna,"* woh peshkash karta hai, *"achay bacchon ki parvarish karna jo kamiyab hon."* Sumayyah ki aankhein aansuon se bhar jati hain. *"Lekin kis **Maqsad** ke liye, Bilal?"* woh poochti hai, uski awaaz naram lekin kaanp rahi hai. *"Is tamam kamiyabi ka hatmi nukta kya hai?"*

Iske baad ki khamoshi mukhtalif thi. Yeh ghussay wali nahi thi; yeh ek khala ki khokhli awaaz thi. Pehli baar, unke Meesaq mein haqeeqi, bunyadi daraar be-naqaab hui. *"Mujhe lagta hai ke hum ek kamiyab karobar chala rahe hain,"* woh apne aansuon ke zariye kehti hai, *"Aakhirat ke liye sharakat-dari nahi. Aur mujhe nahi lagta ke main ab yeh mazeed kar sakti hoon."*

Koi aasaan hal nahi hai. Koi ek maqsad nahi jis par woh muttafiq ho sakein jo itni wasee khaleej ko paat sake. Pesh-raft koi hal nahi hai; yeh asal maslay ki mushtarqa, takleef-deh tashkhees hai. Ek taweel, mushkil khamoshi ke baad, yeh Bilal hai jo bolta hai, uski awaaz ek nayi, khaufnaak, aur imandarana aajizi se bhari hai. *"Phir humein wapas shuru mein jana hoga,"* woh kehta hai. *"Humein ek hi naqsha talash karna hoga."*

Unka "ek qabil-e-husool maqsad" aajizana aur gehra hai. Woh har raat Quran ki ek ayat, tarjumay ke saath, mil kar padhne aur ek doosre se ek hi sawal poochne ka ahed karte hain: **"Yeh ayat hamare hatmi maqsad ke bare mein kya kehti hai?"**

Yeh manzil par pohanchne ki garmaish nahi thi, balki ek door-daraz subah ki madham, khaufnaak, aur khubsurat roshni thi. Woh apni manzil par nahi pohanchay thay, lekin pehli baar, woh ek hi naqshay ko dekh rahe thay, is wazahat ke saath jo hosh-ruba bhi thi aur muqaddas bhi ke yeh jad-o-jehad ka ikhtitam nahi, balki unke ghar ke safar ka haqeeqi aaghaz tha.

#### **[Juz] Dhancha: Tajdeed ka Mamool**
**([Component] The Framework: A Ritual of Renewal)**

Yeh dhancha ek rehnuma hai, ek muqaddas guftagu ke liye tajweez-karda saakht. Yeh teen marahil par mabni hai: dil ki tayyari, ikhlaas ke saath session ka in'iqad, aur dua ke saath Meesaq par mohar lagana.

**Marhala 1: Tayyari (Niyat)**
Ek lafz bolne se pehle, roohani zameen tayyar honi چاہیے۔ Yeh sabse ahem marhala hai. Jo guftagu khalis niyaton ke saath shuru hoti hai woh Allah ki hifazat mein hoti hai.
1.  **Waqt aur Jagah tay karein:** Aise waqt par ittefaq karein jab aap dono pur-sukoon hon aur jaldi mein na hon ya koi rukawat na ho. Apne ghar mein ek niji, pur-aman jagah ka intekhab karein. Tamam **mobile phone aur aalaat** band kar dein.
2.  **Wuzu karein:** Is mulaqat ko aise hi lein jaise aap namaz ke liye jate hain — jismani aur roohani pakeezgi (Taharat) ki halat mein.
3.  **Do Rak'at (Infradi taur par) padhein:** Ikatthay baithne se pehle, har shareek-e-hayat ko do rak'at *Salat-ul-Hajah* padhni chahiye. Apne sajday mein, zaati aur mukhlasana dua karein. Allah se ikhlaas mangein, aur ana (Nafs) ko door karne ki iltija karein.
4.  **Apni Niyat ki Tajdeed karein (Ikatthay):** Jab aap aakhir-kaar ikatthay baithein, to buland awaaz mein mushtarqa niyat bayan karke shuruat karein. Aap keh sakte hain: *"Is mulaqat ke liye hamari niyat Allah ki khatir hamare muqaddas Meesaq ko mazboot karna hai. Hamara maqsad Jannat ke raaste par ek doosre ki madad karna, maafi mangna, shukr-guzari ka izhaar karna, aur apne 'Diyaar' ko uske muqaddas mission ke saath dobara hum-aahang karna hai."*

**Marhala 2: Nashist (Mushawarat)**
Yeh guftagu jaan boojh kar musbat se tameeri, aur aakhir mein tamannayi ki taraf badhne ke liye tarteeb di gayi hai.
1.  **Aaghaz (Dua):** Ayat-e-Mawaddah (Rum 30:21) ki tilawat karein aur ittehad ke liye Nabvi dua padhein (*Allahumma allif bayna qulubina...*).
2.  **Hissa A: Fazl ki Halat (Shukr-guzari):**
    *   Yeh hissa fa'aal taur par achayi ko talash karne ke liye waqf hai. Har shareek-e-hayat bolne ke liye bila-ta'attul baari leta hai.
    *   **Sawalat:** *"Guzishta [muddat] mein sochte hue, aap ka ek makhsoos amal jis ne mujhe gehra pyar aur qadar ka ehsas dilaya woh tha..."* / *"Aap mein ek khoobi jo maine haal hi mein dekhi hai jiski main tareef karta hoon..."*
3.  **Hissa B: Safeeron ki Halat (Muhasabah):**
    *   Yeh nashist ka sabse nazuk hissa hai. Yeh do ahem usoolon ke tehat chalta hai: khud-ehtisabi ka usool, aur istiqbaal ka usool.
    *   **Istiqbaal ka Usool:** Jo shareek-e-hayat mashwara sun raha hai usay sirf ek jawab دینے ki ijazat hai: **"JazakAllah Khair is baat ko share karne ke liye. Main is par gaur karunga."** Aap difaa, tardeed, ya jawabi hamla nahi kar sakte.
    *   **Pehla Shareek-e-Hayat:**
        *   **Qadam 1 (Behtar hone ki koshish):** *"Ek tareeqa jis se main Allah ki khatir aap ke shareek-e-hayat ke taur par apne kirdaar mein behtar hona chahoonga woh hai... Main is mein aap ki madad chahta hoon."*
        *   **Qadam 2 (Mohabbat bhara mashwara):** *"Ek cheez jo mere dil ko aap ke dil se aur bhi zyada juda hua mehsoos karne mein madad degi woh hai agar hum mil kar [falan cheez] par kaam kar sakein."*
4.  **Hissa C: Mission ki Halat (Mizan):**
    *   Yeh hissa jode ko unke hatmi maqsad ke saath dobara hum-aahang karta hai. Ek team ke taur par, is ek sawal ke jawab par ittefaq karein: **"Woh kaun sa ek thos, qabil-e-husool roohani maqsad hai jis par hum aane wali [muddat] mein mil kar kaam karne ka ahed kar sakte hain?"**

**Marhala 3: Ahed (Azm)**
Nateejay ke baghair nashist sirf baatein hain. Yeh marhala ek naye, mushtarqa azm ke saath tajdeed par mohar lagata hai.
1.  **Azm ka Khulasa:** Us ek qabil-e-husool maqsad ko wazeh taur par bayan karein jis par aap ne ittefaq kiya hai. Isay kaghaz ke tukday par likhein.
2.  **Mushtarqa Dua:** Ikatthay haath uthayen. Apne alfaz mein, apne dilon se, Allah ﷻ se dua karein ke Woh aap ki tauba qabool kare aur aap ko is naye azm ko poora karne ki taufiq de.
3.  **Mawaddah ke saath Mohar:** Nashist ka ikhtitam jismani mohabbat ke amal se karein — haath pakadna, galay milna, peshani par bosa.

#### **[Juz] Mamool ki Hifazat: Nakami aur Ikhtilaf ka Protocol**
**([Component] Guarding the Ritual: Protocol for Failure & Disagreement)**

Yeh muqaddas mamool ek maidan-e-jung hai. Jaise hi aap Allah ki khatir apne Meesaq ki tajdeed ka irada karte hain, aap ne apni ana ke khilaf jung ka elaan kar diya hai. Aap ka Nafs is aajizi ke amal se nafrat karega.

**1. Yargamaal shuda Nashist ka Protocol (Hungami Tawaqquf)**
Agar kisi bhi mauqay par yeh guftagu ilzaam-tarashi, tanz, oonchi awaazon ya ghussay mein badal jaye, to amal ko Nafs ne **yargamaal** bana liya hai. Behes "jeetne" ki koshish na karein. Aap ko foran ruk jana chahiye. Ek shareek-e-hayat mein yeh kehne ki taqat honi chahiye, **"Hum rehmat ke raaste se hat gaye hain. Allah ki khatir, humein abhi ruk jana chahiye."** Nashist multawi kar dein. Bahar na bhagein. Bas kisi aur din dobara koshish karne ke azm ke saath guftagu khatam karein.

**2. Inkaar ka Protocol**
Yeh gehra takleef-deh ho sakta hai: ek raazi hai, aur doosra ikhlaas ke saath shamil hone se inkaar karta hai. Agar aisa hota hai, to raazi shareek-e-hayat ka kirdaar zabardasti karna, khushamad karna, ya sharminda karna nahi hai. Aap dil ki tabdeeli par majboor nahi kar sakte. Aap ka kirdaar is inkaar ko apni tardeed ke taur par nahi, balki Meesaq ki halat ke bare mein ahem maloomat ke taur par pehchanna hai.

Aap ka farz dohra ho jata hai. Pehla, aap apne shareek-e-hayat ke dil ke naram hone ke liye mukhlasana dua karte hain. Doosra, aap Meesaq ke usoolon ko yak-tarfa taur par mujassam karte hain. Aap Fadl ke liye koshish jari rakhte hain, Adl qaim rakhte hain, aur Allah ke saath apne talluq par kaam karte hain. Aap ka musalsal **Ihsan**, Allah ki marzi se, woh dawat ban sakta hai jo unka dil khol de.

**3. Ta'attul ka Protocol (Impasse)**
Aap ko maloom ho sakta hai ke aap dono tayyar hain lekin Hissa C mein kisi mushtarqa maqsad par muttafiq nahi ho sakte. "Kamil" ko "Achay" ka dushman na banne dein. Is qadam ka maqsad aap ki shadi ke har maslay ko hal karna nahi hai. Yeh tameer karne ke liye ittehad ka ek nukta talash karna hai. Agar aap kisi bade maqsad par muttafiq nahi ho sakte, to chota sochein. Kya aap hafte mein ek baar Quran ki ek ayat ikatthay padhne par muttafiq ho sakte hain? Roohani muahiday ka sabse chota mumkinah nukta talash karein aur uska ahed karein.

#### **[Juz] Nabvi Namuna (The Prophetic Paradigm)**

Yahan tak ke zameen par sabse muqaddas gharana, Allah ke Rasool ﷺ ka ghar, kamil hone ki jamid (static) yaadgar nahi tha. Yeh ek zinda, saans leta hua Meesaq tha, aur tamam zinda cheezon ki tarah, isay kabhi kabhi gehri aur iradi tajdeed ki zaroorat hoti thi. Iska hatmi namuna **"Takhyir"** (Ikhtiyar dene) ka waqia hai.

Jab unki biwiyan, Ummahat-ul-Momineen, unke paas ek group ki shakal mein duniyawi asason mein izafay ka mutalba lekar aayin, to yeh ek buhraan tha. "Diyaar" ki tawajjo ghair-duniyawi mission se hat kar duniya ki zeenat ki taraf ja rahi thi. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne lecture ya riayat (concession) ke saath jawab nahi diya. Unhone **Alaihadgi** (withdrawal) ke daur ke saath jawab diya, ek mahine ki gehri, khamosh judayi jo ijtimayi muhasabay ki ek shakal thi, jis ne har ek ko Meesaq ki halat par gaur karne par majboor kiya.

Phir Khudayi mudakhilat aayi, ek Wahi jo hatmi "Meesaq ki Tajdeed" ke session ke taur par kaam karti thi. Allah ﷻ ne koi samjhota pesh nahi kiya. Usne ek wazeh aur mutlaq intekhab pesh kiya, hatmi "Mission ki Halat" ki hum-aahangi:

> *"Aye Nabi! Apni biwiyon se keh dijiye, 'Agar tum duniya ki zindagi aur iski zeenat chahti ho, to aao, main tumhein kuch de dilun aur achay tareeqay se rukhsat karun. Lekin agar tum Allah aur uske Rasool aur Aakhirat ka ghar chahti ho, to beshak, Allah ne tum mein se neki karne walon ke liye bada ajr tayyar kar rakha hai.'"* (Quran 33:28-29)

Yeh hai tajdeed ki roohani technology ka hatmi sabaq. Nabi Kareem ﷺ ne duniyawi maslay ko duniyawi hal se hal nahi kiya. Unhone unki be-cheeni ki shara'it par baat cheet nahi ki; unhone us se buland-tar baat ki. Unhone humein sikhaya ke duniya ki kashish ki wajah se kamzor hone wale Meesaq ko rafu karne ka wahid tareeqa isay **Aakhirat** ki mutlaq haqeeqat mein dobara langar-andaz (re-anchor) karna hai. Haqeeqi tajdeed samjhota nahi hai. Yeh **Yadahani** hai. Yeh dil ka ek shaoori, aur kabhi kabhi takleef-deh, modna hai apne haqeeqi "North" ki taraf: Allah ki raza aur Aakhirat ka ghar.

### **Ikhtitamiya: Jannat mein ek Diyaar-e-Rehmat**
**(Epilogue: An Embassy in Jannah)**

Imtehaan mukammal ho chuka hai. Aamaal ka toomar (scroll) mohar-band ho chuka hai. Safeer, jala-watni ki sar-zameen mein apna taweel commission poora karne ke baad, aakhir-kaar wapas bula liye gaye hain. Jahaaz apne aakhri sahil par pohanch chuka hai, aur is duniya ka samundar abdi baaghat ko raasta de raha hai. Safar mehez khatam nahi hua; iska jawab diya gaya hai.

Aur jaise hi Watan ke darwaze khulte hain, Badshah ke muhafiz dastay, yani Farishtay, wapas aane wale safeeron ka istiqbaal unke mission ke audit ke saath nahi, balki uski kamiyabi ke elaan ke saath karte hain, har us mushkil par ek hatmi aur khubsurat lafz ke saath jo unhone Us ki khatir bardasht ki:

> *"Woh hamesha rehne wale baaghat mein dakhil honge, woh aur jo unke baap dada, unki biwiyon aur unki aulad mein se nek honge. Aur farishtay har darwaze se unke paas aayenge, [yeh kehte hue], 'Salam ho tum par uske badle jo tum ne sabr kiya. Pas kya hi khoob hai yeh aakhri ghar.'"*
> (Al-Quran, 13:23-24)

Yeh har us lamhay par hatmi lafz hai jab ghussa piya gaya, har woh amal jab be-los sakhawat ki gayi, har woh dua jo raat ke pehar mein doosre ke liye mangi gayi. In sab ka jawab ek lafz se diya jata hai: **"Salam"** (Salamti).

Phir Badshah ka aakhri, sabse zyada reham wala amal aata hai. Woh miyan biwi ke dilon mein, unki mushtarqa tareekh ki yaad mein haath dalta hai, aur apne Khudayi haath se, woh hatmi **Tatheer** (Purification) karta hai:

> *"Aur hum unke seenon mein jo kuch bhi keena (ya ranjish) hogi usay nikal denge..."*
> (Al-Quran, 7:43)

"Hangama-khez **Khittay**" (Zone) ka har zakhm, sakht lafz ka har nishan, ghalat-fehmi ka har baqi-manda dard — yeh sab dhoya jata hai. Do samundar, jo kabhi toofan mein mile thay, ab khalis roshni aur kamil mohabbat ke ek hi, pur-sukoon samundar mein zam ho jate hain. Shanakht ka **Barzakh** baqi rehta hai, lekin ragad hamesha ke liye khatam ho jati hai. Meesaq mukammal ho jata hai.

Yeh hai Jannat mein Diyaar-e-Rehmat. Yeh ab kisi ghair-mulki sar-zameen mein chowki nahi, balki Watan mein ek ghar hai. Iski deewarein jad-o-jehad aur sabr se nahi, balki aman aur Zaat-e-Ilahi ki qurbat se bani hain. Unki rafaqat ab khushi ka ek abdi, be-sakhta izhaar hai, Badshah ke barah-e-raast huzoor mein ek kamil **Mawaddah**. Unka mushtarqa mission mukammal ho chuka hai. Inaam koi safar nahi; inaam **Ghar** hai.

Allah Ta'ala apne be-payan fazl-o-karam se isay hamara aur un tamam logon ka anjaam banaye jo Us se mohabbat karte hain.

Ameen.

### **Hissa Awwal: Manhaj par ek Note**
**(Part A: A Note on Methodology)**

Shuru Allah ke naam se, jo bada Meherbaan, nihayat Reham wala hai.

Yeh kitab ek wahid, ahem sawal se wajood mein aayi: Insaan izdawaji meesaq ki la-zawal, muqaddas sachaiyon ko aisi zaban mein kaise bayan kare jo jadeed duniya ke shor mein suni ja sake, baghair iske ke riwayat ki salmiyat par koi samjhota ho? Hamare Deen ka mawad kamil hai, lekin woh bartan jo hum isay le jane ke liye banate hain, unhein hamare waqt ki zarooriyat ke mutabiq hona chahiye. Yeh note un tameerati usoolon, ya "Karigari" ko share karne ki ek aajizana koshish hai, jo is khaas bartan ki tameer mein istemaal hue.

**Teen Darwazon ka Safar**
Is kitab ko feham ke teen alag alag darwazon ke safar ke taur par tarteeb diya gaya hai. Yeh intekhab padhne ke tajurbay ko mauzuaat ke ghair-fa'aal mutalay se ek fa'aal, mantiqi aur roohani pesh-raft mein tabdeel karne ke liye kiya gaya tha. Yeh is classici usool ka ehtram karta hai ke insaan ko pehle kisi cheez ki *Mahiyat* (Pehla Darwaza: Meesaq) ko samajhna chahiye, is se pehle ke woh iske andar maujood *Aamileen* (Doosra Darwaza: Safeer) ko samjhe, aur aamileen ko samajhne ke baad unke hatmi *Maqsad* (Teesra Darwaza: Mission) ko samjhe.

**Muwafiqat-pazeer Fun-e-Tameer**
Har baab ka fun-e-tameer jaan boojh kar lachak-daar rakha gaya hai. Agarche har baab ek mustaqil **Bunyadi Dhanchay** (Bunyadi Usool, Wazahat, Nabvi Namuna, Mohar) par tameer kiya gaya hai taake ek mustaqil taal faraham ki ja sake, maine **Mutaghayyar Aaza** (Variable Organs) — jaise Ishtiqaq, Fiqh, ya Wirsa — ko sirf tab istemaal kiya hai jab us baab ki makhsoos daleel ne iska mutalba kiya. Yeh tareeqa-e-kaar ek sada lekin gehre yaqeen se paida hua hai: **Mawad ko hai'at (form) ka ta'ayyun karna chahiye, na ke iske bar-aks.** Lisaniyat ke baab ko qanoon ke baab se mukhtalif auzaaron ki zaroorat hoti hai.

**Usoolon ka Bahami Rabt**
Poori kitab mein, aap ko ek takneek milegi jisay main **"Lens aur Namuna Protocol"** kehta hoon, jahan ek bunyadi matn (Namuna) ka tajziya sanwi Islami usool (Lens) ke wazeh tanazur mein kiya jata hai. Yeh hamari riwayat ki ek bunyadi sachayi ko zahir karne ke liye kiya gaya: **Islam mein koi usool tanha nahi khada hota.** Insaaf Qiwamiyat ko roshan karta hai, Amanat Meesaq ki sangeeni ko wazeh karti hai. Deen ek mukammal taur par buna hua **"Taana-Baana"** (Tapestry) hai, aur yeh tareeqa-e-kaar qari ko iske chand khubsurat, juday hue dhagon ko dikhane ki koshish hai.

Maine is mukhtasar kaam mein ek aisa dhancha tameer karne ki koshish ki hai jo fikri taur par mazboot aur roohani taur par asar-angez ho. Yeh khidmat ka ek amal hai jo is umeed mein pesh kiya gaya hai ke yeh bartan us qeemti hikmat ke laiq ho jo is mein maujood hai aur yeh ke, apne Khaliq ke fazl se, yeh qari ki hifazat se sahil tak rehnumayi kare.

Aur Allah behtar janta hai.

### **Hissa Soyam: Kaleedi Qurani o Nabvi Istilahaat ka Ishariya**
**(Part C: Index of Key Terminology)**

Yeh ishariya un bunyadi Arabi istilahaat ka fauri hawala faraham karta hai jo Khudayi Manhaj ki lughat tashkeel deti hain.

**I. Meesaq ki noiyat**
*   **Al-Mithaq (المیثاق):** Ek pukhta, paband aur bhari ahed, jo Allah ko gawah bana kar liya gaya ho. Yeh shadi ko ek sada samaji muahiday se buland karke ek muqaddas wade ke maqam par le jata hai. (Maakhuz: Quran 4:21)
*   **Al-Amanah (الامانہ):** Ek muqaddas aur bhari amanat jo ek fareeq ki taraf se doosre ko di gayi ho, jis ke liye Ameen (Trustee) ko Allah ke samne jawabdeh hona padega. Yeh shohar ki Qiwamiyat ko haq ke taur par nahi, balki ek khaufnaak zimmedari ke taur par pesh karta hai.

**II. Tarz-e-Amal ke do Qutub**
*   **Al-Adl (العدل):** Mutlaq insaaf aur tawazun ka usool. Yeh izdawaji meesaq ki atal bunyad hai, woh na-guzir farsh jis par tamam aala khubiyan tameer ki jati hain. (Maakhuz: Quran 4:135)
*   **Al-Fadl (الفضل):** Razakarana sakhawat, fazl, aur insaaf ki zaroorat se zyada dene ki halat. Yeh "Baaghbaan ki Zehniyat" ki rooh hai. (Maakhuz: Quran 2:237)

**III. Zindagi ke do Khittay**
*   **Al-Ma'roof (المعروف):** Woh cheez jo Shariat aur achi, saleh riwayat dono ke nazdeek achi aur munsifana samjhi jaye. Yeh Meesaq ka "Zarkhez Maidan" hai. (Maakhuz: Quran 4:19)
*   **Al-Ihsan (الاحسان):** Roohani fazilat ki halat, jiski tareef Nabi ﷺ ne yun ki ke "Allah ki ibadat aise karo jaise tum Usay dekh rahe ho." Yeh Meesaq ki "Roshan Choti" hai.

**IV. Do Safeer**
*   **Qiwamah (القوامہ):** Shohar ki muqaddas, bojhal zimmedari ke woh apne khandan ke liye insaaf, tahaffuz aur rizq ka bunyadi muhafiz aur muntazim ho. Yeh "Muhafiz-Khadim" ka fa'aal kirdaar hai. (Maakhuz: Quran 4:34)
*   **Rabbat al-Bayt (ربۃ البیت):** Biwi ke liye ek aizaazi laqab, jis ka matlab hai "Ghar ki Malika". Yeh biwi ke kirdaar ko khadima ke taur par nahi, balki ghar ki rooh ki kaashtkari karne wali ba-waqar rehnuma ke taur par bayan karta hai.

**V. Do Azeem Khatray**
*   **Az-Zulm (الظلم):** Na-insaafi, ziyadati; kisi cheez ko uske sahi maqam ke alawa kisi aur jagah rakhna. Yeh Meesaq ki muqaddas hudood ki khilaf-warzi hai.
*   **Al-Hawa (الہویٰ):** Man-mani khwahish; Nafs ka woh jhukaao jo Wahi ki rehnumayi mein na ho. Yeh Insaaf ka bunyadi androoni dushman hai.

**VI. Do Azeem Samraat (Fruits)**
*   **As-Sakinah (السکینہ):** Sukoon aur itminan ki ek gehri halat jo Allah ki taraf se dil mein utari jati hai. Yeh ek achi tarah se kaasht kiye gaye Meesaq ka hatmi roohani phal hai. (Maakhuz: Quran 48:4)
*   **Qurrat A'yun (قرۃ اعین):** Ek Arabi muhawra jis ka matlab hai "Aankhon ki Thandak", jo gehri khushi aur itminan ki alamat hai. Yeh Khudayi Manhaj ke andar khandan ke liye jaiz duniyawi tamanna hai. (Maakhuz: Quran 25:74)

**Ta'aruf: Aakhri Imtehaan**

Yeh kitab ghair-fa'aal mutalay ke liye nahi likhi gayi. Isay ek muqaddas meesaq ki fa'aal kaashtkari ke liye ek auzaar, ek naqsha aur ek qutub-numa banne ke liye design kiya gaya tha. Yeh aakhri mashq is design ka nukta-e-urooj hai. Yeh "Aakhri Imtehaan" hai.

Neeche ek farzi jode ka ek pecheeda Case Study diya gaya hai. Yeh masail ki ek uljhi hui girah hai, jis mein is kitab mein zer-e-behes bahut se mauzuaat ko ikattha kiya gaya hai. **Manzar-nama: An-kahi Baaton ka Ghar**

Yusuf aur Amina ki shadi ko unki community ek "misali" shadi samajhti hai. Yusuf ek kamiyab professional hai jo ek achay mohallay mein ek khubsurat, kushada ghar faraham karta hai. Amina, jis ne apne teen bacchon ki parvarish ke liye apna career chhod diya, apne behtareen zauq, ba-adab bacchon, aur community ke ijtimaat ke liye tayyar karda lazeez khanon ke liye mashhoor hai. Unka ghar duniyawi kamiyabi ka safarat-khana hai. Lekin andar se, hawa sard hai.

Yusuf ki boodhi maa unke saath rehti hain. Woh ek mukhtalif nasal ki khatoon hain, aur woh apni mohabbat aur fikr ka izhaar Amina par musalsal "madadgar" tanqeed ke zariye karti hain: bacchon ki tarbiyat bahut narm hai, khanay mein masalay riwayati nahi hain, usay Yusuf ke thaka dene wale din ke baad uske aaram ke liye mazeed kuch karna chahiye. Amina in rozana ke nashtaron ko khamosh, sulagti hui narazgi ke saath jazb karti hai.

Yusuf tanau ko dekhta hai. Woh apni maa se mohabbat karta hai aur woh yaqeen rakhta hai ke woh apni biwi se mohabbat karta hai. Uska hal **Gurez** (Avoidance) hai. Jab Amina us se apni maa ke tabsiron ke bare mein baat karne ki koshish karti hai, to woh baat khatam kar deta hai. *"Woh ek boodhi aurat hain, woh aisi hi hain,"* woh kehta hai. *"Bas sabr karo. Tum choti baat ka batangad bana rahi ho."* Usay ghussa aata hai: woh unhein yeh zindagi faraham karne ke liye itni mehnat karta hai; kam az kam Amina ghar mein aman to rakh sakti hai. Woh yaqeen rakhta hai ke uski maali farahmi uske farz ki takmeel hai.

Amina bilkul tanha mehsoos karti hai. Woh khud ko ek khubsurat hotel ki bila-muawza, ghair-sarahi gayi manager mehsoos karti hai, na ke ghar ki malika. Uski panah-gaah ki kaashtkari ek kadwa kaam ban gayi hai, ek aisa fun jo aise saama'een ke liye pesh kiya jata hai jo kabhi daad nahi dete. Woh mohabbat jo woh kabhi Yusuf ke liye mehsoos karti thi, ahista ahista khatam ho gayi hai aur uski jagah na-insaafi ke gehre ehsas ne le li hai. Woh khud ko **"Score-keeping"** karte hue pati hai, zehni taur par apni tamam qurbaniyon ko uski **Tankhwah** (salary) ke wahid ta'awun ke khilaf darj karti hai. Unki guftagu khalisatan len-den tak mehdood ho gayi hai, jo bacchon ke schedule aur gharelu akhrajat tak mehdood hai.

Unka mushtarqa khwab ab Aakhirat ke bare mein nahi hai. Yeh agli tazeen-o-araish, agli taraqqi, bacchon ko behtareen university mein dakhil karane ke bare mein hai. Woh ek kamiyab **Karobari Idara** (Corporation) hain, lekin sharakat-dari roohani taur par diwaliya hai. Ek shaam, apni saas ke ek khaas taur par mushkil tabsiray ke baad, Amina Yusuf se kehti hai, *"Main ab yeh mazeed nahi kar sakti."* Yusuf, thaka hua, jawab deta hai, *"Tum mujh se aur kya chahti ho? Main tumhein sab kuch to deta hoon."* Iske baad ki khamoshi un do safeeron ki awaaz hai jo apne mission ko bhool chuke hain, aur ek sard aur khubsurat safarat-khanay mein mukhtalif zabanain bol rahe hain.

**Aazmaish: Aap ka Tajziya**

Manzar-namay ko gaur se padhein. Phir, is kitab ke usoolon aur frameworks ka istemaal karte hue, darj zail sawalat ke jawab dein.

1.  Baab 2 ke **"Jughrafiyai Naqshay"** ke model ka istemaal karte hue, batayein ke is jode ka Meesaq fil-haal kis khittay mein chal raha hai: Ihsan ki Roshan Chotiyan, Ma'roof ke Zarkhez Maidan, ya Fiqh ki Khaayi ka Kinara? Apne jawab ka jawaz pesh karein.
2.  Baab 3 se **"Nabvi Qiwamiyat ke Darakht"** ke lens ke zariye Yusuf ke tarz-e-amal ka tajziya karein. Darakht ke kaun se hissay — Taqwa ki jarein, Khidmat ka tana, ya Mushawarati Rehmat ki shakhein — kamzor ya bimar hain?
3.  Baab 4 se **"Rabbat al-Bayt"** ke manhaj ke lens ke zariye Amina ki halat ka tajziya karein. Muhafiz (Guardian) ki taraf se tahaffuz aur tareef faraham karne mein nakami ne "Sakinah" paida karne ki salahiyat ko kaise mutasir kiya hai?
4.  Baab 1 se **"Kat-hairay ki Zehniyat"** ne unke ta'aluqaat mein kaise jad pakad li hai? "Score-keeping" ki ek makhsoos misaal ki nishandahi karein aur wazahat karein ke **"Al-Fadl"** ka usool kaise mukhtalif raasta faraham karega.
5.  Baab 5 se **"Safeer ke Litmus Test"** ka istemaal karte hue, unke "Diyaar-e-Rehmat ke Mission" ki halat ki tashkhees karein. Char teston (Waqt, Maal, Guftagu, Dua) mein se kis mein woh sabse zyada wazeh taur par nakaam hain?
6.  Tasawwur karein ke aap ek qabil-e-aitemaad, danishmand buzurg hain. Yusuf aur Amina ke liye ek tafseeli, marhala-war mansuba tayyar karein ke woh apne wahid sabse bade maslay ko hal karne ke liye Baab 6 se **"Meesaq ki Tajdeed" (Muhasabah)** ke framework ko kaise istemaal karein.
7.  Amli iqdamaat aur frameworks se hat kar, dil ki woh wahid, bunyadi **Roohani Bimari** kya hai jo is pooray tanazay ki jad hai? Agar aap Yusuf aur Amina dono ko ikatthay sirf ek mukhlasana nasihat (*Nasiha*) kar sakein, ek jumla jo unke mushtarqa maslay ke dil par lage, to woh kya hoga?
