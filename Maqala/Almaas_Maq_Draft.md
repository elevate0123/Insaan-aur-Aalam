### **Daur-e-Hazir mein Deen-e-Islam par Mazbooti se Qayam Rehne ka Aasaan Tareeqa**

Shaikh Muhammedalmaas Ashrafi 

**Muqaddimah: Daur-e-Jadeed ka Tazaad - Zaahiri Taur par Juda Hua, Baatini Taur par Toota Hua**

Zayd ek nek-niyat naujawan hai. Woh namaz ke liye khada hota hai. Jism toh qiyaam mein hai, magar uska zehn bikhra hua hai. Dimaag mein kaam ki aakhri taareekh, social media par dekhi hui ek behes, aur ghar ki koi pareshani, sab ek saath chal raha hai. Khushu o khuzu ki woh kaifiyat jiske baare mein usne padha hai, usey door-door tak mehsoos nahi hoti. Uski shakhsiyat tukdon mein bati hui hai.

Woh apne walidain ke paas baithta hai, magar haqeeqat mein wahan maujood nahi hota. Uski ungliyan phone ki screen par chalti rehti hain, aur uska zehn anjaan logon ki aaraa aur zindagiyon mein gum hota hai. Apne sab se qareebi rishton ke beech baith kar bhi woh tanhayi mehsoos karta hai. Woh apne deen ko seekhna chahta hai, magar online ilm ka samandar usey uljha deta hai. Ek aalim ki video dekhta hai toh doosra uski tardeed karta hua nazar aata hai. Har taraf aaraa ka ek toofan hai, magar koi ek mustanad aawaz nahi jo dil mein utar jaaye. Uske andar ek shadeed khalishar aur shakhsiyat ka bikhrao hai. Zayd ka masla sirf technology ka nahi; uska masla andarooni sukoon aur ek waazeh, qaabil-e-aitmaad raaste ke fuqdaan ka hai. Allama Iqbal ne is daur ke insaan ki isi kaifiyat ki nishandahi ki thi:

>*Dhoondne wala sitaron ki guzar-gahon ka,*
>*Apne afkaar ki duniya mein safar kar na saka*

Zayd ki yeh kahani aaj ke har us Musalman ki kahani hai jo apne imaan ke saath mukhlish hai magar daur-e-jadeed ke shor mein apni simt kho baitha hai. Hamare daur ka sab se bada fitna yahi baatini khalishar aur shakhsiyat ka bikhrao hai. Yeh ek aisi duniya hai jo hamein hazaar mukhtalif simton mein kheenchti hai, hamari tawajjoh, hamari wafadari, aur hamari pehchaan ko tukdon mein baant deti hai.

Aise mein, is maqaale ka unwaan "Aasaan Tareeqa" shayad kuch logon ko ajeeb lage. Lekin hamein "aasaan" ke ma'ani ko dobara samajhne ki zaroorat hai. Is daur ki asal mushkil is khalishar aur andarooni toot-phoot ki takleef hai. Lihaza, 'aasaan tareeqa' woh nahi jismein mehnat na ho, balki 'aasaan tareeqa' woh hai jo hamein is bikhrao se bacha kar ek-su (integrated) kar de. Woh seedha raasta jo hamari fitrat ke ain mutaabiq ho aur hamein bhatakne se bachaye, wahi sab se aasaan raasta hai, chahe us par chalne mein jismaani aur nafsaani mashaqqat hi kyun na ho. Yeh maqaala usi aasaan, seedhe, aur fitri raaste ki nishandahi karega. Yeh dikhayega ke Islam ka Nabawi ﷺ khaka kis tarah hamein ek bikhri hui shakhsiyat se ek mukammal aur yakja shakhsiyat (Integrated Self) banne ka safar karwata hai. Yeh safar Kalma-e-Tayyiba ki unifying vision se shuru hota hai, Arkaan-e-Islam ke amali nizaam mein parwaan chadhta hai, aur Ahl-e-Sunnat ki zinda riwayat ke zariye hum tak pahunchta hai.

**Hissa Awwal: Yakja Karne Wala Vision - Kalma-e-Tayyiba Bator-e-Ek Mukammal Khaake ke**

Zayd ka sangharsh, aur hamara sangharsh, buniyadi taur par ek worldview ka bohraan hai. Iska ilaaj bhi ek sahi aur mukammal worldview se hi shuru hoga. Islam mein yeh nazariya Kalma-e-Tayyiba mein posheeda hai.

**1. Nafsiyaati Bunyaad (`Lā ilāha illallāh`):**
Yeh elaan-e-Tawheed bikhri hui jadeed nafsiyat ka hatmi ilaaj hai. Dil mein jo ek bechaini aur khalishar aaj har shakhs mehsoos karta hai, uski jad Qur'an ne chaudah sau saal pehle hi bayaan farma di thi. Jaisa ke farmaya gaya: ”أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ“ (Yaqeenan, Allah ke zikr hi se dilon ko itminaan milta hai). Is baat ko samjhane ke liye Qur'an ek zabardast tamseel pesh karta hai:

**ضَرَبَ اللَّهُ مَثَلًا رَّجُلًا فِيهِ شُرَكَاءُ مُتَشَاكِسُونَ وَرَجُلًا سَلَمًا لِّرَجُلٍ ۖ هَلْ يَسْتَوِيَانِ مَثَلًا**
(Surah Az-Zumar, 29)
”Allah ne ek misaal bayaan ki hai: ek woh shakhs jiske kayi aaqa hain jo aapas mein jhagadte rehte hain, aur ek woh shakhs jo poori tarah ek hi aaqa ka ghulam hai. Kya in dono ki misaal barabar hai?”

Yeh tamseel aaj ke insaan ki nafsiyati haalat ka aaina hai. "Kayi jhagdaalu aaqaaon wala shakhs" aaj ka jadeed insaan hai jo bak-waqt apne nafs, apne boss, samaaji tawaqqu'aat, aur social media ke algorithms ki ghulami kar raha hai. Har aaqa us se ek mukhtalif mutaaliba karta hai, jiska nateeja nafsiyati thakan aur daaimi khalishar hai. Iske bar-aks, "ek hi aaqa wala shakhs" woh momin hai jiski zindagi ek hi maqsad ke gird ghoomti hai. Uski zindagi mein yaksooyi aur sukoon hai. Dil ki is yaksooyi ko hi Imam Abu Talib al-Makki ne apni azeem kitaab *Qut al-Qulub* mein qalb ki buniyadi ghiza qarar diya hai, jiske baghair rooh bikhri rehti hai. Tawheed sirf ek aqeeda nahi; yeh ek sehatmand aur mukammal shakhsiyat (qalb-e-saleem) ka amali khaka hai.

**2. Samaaji Bunyaad (`Muhammadur Rasūlullāh`):**
Yeh elaan-e-Risalat ek sehatmand aur marboot ma'ashre ka khaka faraham karta hai. Isse hamein do cheezein milti hain:
*   **Ek Muttahid Tareeqa-e-Kaar (Sunnat):** Sunnat, Tawheed par mabni zindagi guzaarne ka amali namoona hai. Yeh hamein is bojh se azaad karti hai ke hum apni akhlaaqiyaat khud ijaad karein. Yeh ek waazeh, khudayi taur par manzoor-shuda =="operating system"== hai jo zindagi ke har shobay mein rehnumayi karta hai.
*   **Ek Muttahid Jama'at (Al-Jama'ah):** Allah aur Uske Rasool ﷺ ki mohabbat par qayam hone wali jama'at jadeed tanhayi ka ilaaj hai. Yeh digital qabeelon ke bar-aks hai jo raaye aur ta'assub par bante hain. Nabawi ﷺ jama'at mohabbat, jawabdehi aur haqeeqi bhai-chaare par qayam hoti hai.

**Hissa Dom: Amal ka Maidaan - Arkaan-e-Islam Bator-e-Ek Jaame' Nizaam ke**

Kalma-e-Tayyiba ka yeh yakja karne wala vision sirf ek falsafa nahi hai; yeh ek aisi haqeeqat hai jise jiya jaata hai. Is vision ko amali zindagi mein utaarne ke liye Allah ne hamein Arkaan-e-Islam ka ek jaame' tarbiyati program ataa kiya hai. Yeh arkaan hamari shakhsiyat ko andar se bahar tak aur fard se jama'at tak marboot (integrate) karte hain. Hum is nizaam ko samajhne ke liye teen arkaan ka tajziya karenge.

**1. Salaat - Umoodi aur Ufuqi Ittehad ka Amal:**
Aaj ki bikhri hui zindagi mein namaz ek jaldi mein adaa ki jaane wali rasm ban gayi hai. Lekin iska haqeeqi maqsad ek momin ke din ka markazi tanzeemi usool banna hai. Jaisa ke Hujjat-ul-Islam Imam Ghazali (rehmatullah alaih) farmate hain, baghair huzoor-e-qalb ke namaz ek be-rooh jism ki manind hai. Namaz ki is rooh ko samajhne ke liye iski haqeeqat ko pehchanna zaroori hai: ”الصلاۃ معراج المؤمن“ (Namaz momin ki mi'raj hai).
Jis tarah Nabi Akram ﷺ ka Mi'raj maaddi duniya ki hudood se nikal kar baargah-e-ilaahi tak ek safar tha, usi tarah namaz momin ki rozana ki mi'raj hai. Yeh woh paanch muqarrar lamhaat hain jab momin duniya ke khalishar se khud ko munqata' (detach) karta hai aur apne ek haqeeqi aaqa ke huzoor yakja ho jaata hai. Yeh amal fard ko **umoodi taur par (Vertically)** Allah se jodta hai.
Phir, is namaz ki sab se kaamil shakl jama'at hai. Jama'at usi umoodi taur par jude hue fard ko leti hai aur usey **ufuqi taur par (Horizontally)** doosre momineen ke saath jod deti hai. Kandhe se kandha mila kar, ek hi simt mein, ek hi imam ke peeche khada hona infradi aur samaaji ittehad ka rozana ka sab se taaqatwar amali muzahira hai.

**2. Zakaat o Sadqah - Roohani aur Maaddi Duniya ka Ittehad:**
Jadeed zindagi ka ek bada almiya zindagi ko do tukdon mein baant dena hai: ek "maaddi" duniya aur ek "roohani" duniya. Zakaat ka nizaam is taqseem ko khatm karta hai. Qur'an ka hukm hai: ”خُذْ مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ صَدَقَةً تُطَهِّرُهُمْ وَتُزَكِيهِم بِهَا“. Lekin ek Hadees iski gehraayi ko mazeed kholti hai:

**”الصدقۃ برھان“** (Sahih Muslim)
(Sadqah ek daleel hai).

Yahan sawaal paida hota hai: kis cheez ki daleel? Yeh imaan ke sidq (sachayi) ki thos, haqeeqi duniya ki daleel hai. Yeh is baat ka saboot hai ke Allah aur Aakhirat par ek shakhs ka yaqeen uske nafs ki maal jama karne ki fitri jibilat par ghaalib aa gaya hai. Yeh us marboot shakhsiyat ka saboot hai jahan roohani iqdaar maaddi zindagi par hukmarani karti hain. Yeh infradi tazkiyah ka amal bak-waqt samaaji yakjehti ka sab se bada zariya hai, jo ma'ashre ko ameer aur ghareeb ke tabqon mein bikharne se bachata hai aur usey ek jism ki tarah jod deta hai.

**3. Sawm (Roza) - Jismaani aur Ma-baad-ut-Tabi'i (Metaphysical) Duniya ka Ittehad:**
Modern zindagi ka ek aur bikhrao jism aur rooh ke darmiyan paida ho gaya hai. Jism ki khwahishat ko poora karna hi zindagi ka maqsad samjha jaata hai. Roza is falsafe par ek karari zarb hai.
**The "Fragmented" View:** Aam taur par, roze ko sirf ek jismaani mashq samjha jaata hai—ek rasm-e-farsooda, bhook pyaas ka ek imtihaan, ya zyada se zyada wazan kam karne ka ek tareeqa.
**The "Integrated" View:** Islam mein, Roza jism ko rooh ke taabe' karne ka sab se taaqatwar amali training program hai. Iska maqsad bhook nahi, jaisa ke Qur'an ne wazeh kar diya:

**يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ ... لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ**
(Surah Al-Baqarah, 183)
"Aye imaan walo! Tum par roze farz kiye gaye... taake tum Taqwa ikhtiyar karo."

Roze ka maqsad **Taqwa** hai. Aur Taqwa kya hai? Taqwa Allah ka woh har-waqt ka ehsaas hai jahan insaan ka baatini shaoor uske zaahiri jismaani a'amaal par haakim ho. Yeh ek marboot shakhsiyat ki ain tareef hai. Roza is integration ke liye saalana "boot camp" hai. Yeh ek intehayi zaati (personal) amal hai, ek banday aur uske Rab ke beech ka raaz, jaisa ke Hadees-e-Qudsi mein aata hai: "Roza mere liye hai, aur main hi iski jaza doonga."
Lekin is intehayi zaati amal ko poori Ummah ek saath, ek hi waqt mein anjaam deti hai. Ramzan ka maheena poori duniya ke Musalmano ko ek mushtarak nazm-o-zabt aur roohani tajrube ke dhaage mein piro deta hai, jiski takmeel Eid ki ijtemayi khushi par hoti hai. Yeh mushtarak tajruba ek aisi ijtemayi shanakht paida karta hai jise digital qabeele kabhi pesh nahi kar sakte. Yeh zaat aur samaaj, jism aur rooh ke ittehad ka ek aur shaandaar namoona hai.

**Hissa Som: Zinda Riwayat - Waariseen-e-Ilm Bator-e-Ittehad ki Kunji ke**

Yeh arkaan-e-Islam par mushtamil tarbiyati nizaam ek aisi amali hidayat hai. Lekin sawal yeh hai ke is par amal karne ka sahi tareeqa, iski rooh ke saath, hum tak kaise pahunchega? Yeh koi "khud karne ki kit" nahi hai. Yeh ek zinda riwayat hai jo mustanad insaani rehnumaon ke zariye seena-ba-seena muntaqil hoti hai. Is usool ki sab se badi daleel Qur'an ne Surah Al-Kahf mein Hazrat Musa aur Hazrat Khizr (alaihimus-salam) ke qisse mein di hai.
Yeh qissa ilm ki nau'iyat par ek khudayi tamseel hai. Hazrat Musa (AS) zaahiri, naazil shuda qanoon (Shari'ah) ke maalik hain. Hazrat Khizr (AS) baatini, ilhaami hikmat (haqeeqat) ke haamil hain. Is qisse ka kuleedi sabaq yeh hai ke ek azeem Nabi, jo zaahiri uloom ke ustaad hain, unhein bhi Allah ke mansoobe ki gehri, baatini jehaton ko samajhne ke liye ek rehnuma ki zaroorat padi. Isse saabit hua ke mukammal, marboot ilm ke liye zaahir aur baatin dono zaroori hain.
Yahin aakar hamare daur ki kayi tehreekon ki gumrahi waazeh ho jaati hai.
Ek giroh woh hai jo "Musa baghair Khizr" ke raaste par chalta hai. Yeh Modernists aur Rationalists hain jo Shari'ah ko toh maante hain magar baatini uloom aur Tasawwuf ka inkaar karte hain. Unke paas Islam ka ek khushk, qanooni khol bach jaata hai jo rooh se khaali hota hai. Yeh aql ka raasta hai jis mein qalb nahi.
Doosra giroh iske bilkul bar-aks hai, jo "Khizr baghair Musa" ke peeche chalta hai. Yeh jaahil soofi hain jo baatini ahwaal ka daawa karte hain jabki Shari'ah ke khule ahkaamaat ki khilaf-warzi karte hain. Unka raasta jazbaat ka hai jis mein nazm-o-zabt nahi, jo la-mahala gumrahi ki taraf le jaata hai. Yahin A'la Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan (rehmatullah alaih) ka woh usool yaad aata hai ke jo Shari'at ka paaband nahi, woh kisi silsile ka wali ho hi nahi sakta, chahe hawa mein hi kyun na ude.
Haqeeqi raasta, Ahl-e-Sunnat ka marboot raasta, woh hai jo Nabawi ﷺ meeraas ko tukdon mein baantne se inkaar karta hai. Yeh **'Ba-Shara' Sufi'** ka raasta hai—woh jo baatini ahwaal ka maahir bhi ho aur zaahiri qanoon ka ek aajiz khadim bhi. Yeh Imam Ghazali, Shaykh Abdul Qadir Jilani, aur un azeem waariseen ka raasta hai jo bak-waqt buland-paya Ulama bhi the aur gehrey roohani peshwa (Awliya) bhi. Unki talaash karna dar-asal us mukammal, ghair-munqata' silsile ki talaash hai jo sab se marboot insaani shakhsiyat, Rasulullah ﷺ, tak ja pahunchta hai.

**Haqeeqi Waariseen ki Amali Pehchaan:**
Lekin ek aam insaan ke liye, jo pehle hi online 'scholars' ke shor mein uljha hua hai, yeh sawal paida hota hai ke haqeeqi Ulama aur Awliya ki pehchaan kaise ki jaaye? Yahan hamare Aslaaf ne hamein chand amali kasautiyan (criteria) ataa ki hain:
**1. Shari'at ka Aaina:** Pehli aur sab se ahem nishani yeh hai ke us rehnuma ki apni zindagi Sunnat ka aaina ho. Uske qaul aur fael mein tazaad na ho. Jaisa ke buzurg farmate hain, "Uska haal uske qaal se pehle Allah ki taraf bulata hai." Jo shakhs khud Shari'at ke zaahiri ahkaam (maslan namaz-e-ba-jama'at, sachayi, amanat-dari) mein sust ho, woh baatini raaste ka rehnuma nahi ho sakta.
**2. Duniya se Be-Raghbati:** Doosri nishani yeh hai ke ek haqeeqi rehnuma aapke maal, aapki shohrat, ya apni zaati anaa ki taskeen ka talabgaar nahi hota. Uska maqsad aapki roohani falah hota hai, na ke apna duniyawi faida. Yeh un 'peers' ke bilkul bar-aks hai jo mureedon ko apni shakhsiyat-parasti (personality cult) mein mubtala kar dete hain.
**3. Suhbat ka Asar:** Yeh aakhri aur hatmi kasauti hai. Kya unki majlis mein baithne se aapke dil mein Allah aur Uske Rasool ﷺ ki mohabbat badhti hai? Kya unki suhbat aapko apni ibadat mein zyada sanjeeda banati hai? Kya isse duniya ki mohabbat aapke dil se kam hoti hai? Jaisa ke Imam Ghazali ne *Ihya Ulum al-Din* mein likha hai, aalim-e-rabbani ki suhbat aakhirat ki yaad dilati hai. Asli pehchaan mureed ke apne dil par padne wale asar mein hai.
In kasautiyon par parakh kar jab ek Musalman kisi rehnuma se waabasta hota hai, toh uska safar mehfooz ho jaata hai.

**Khaatimah: Takmeel ka Safar**

Khulasa yeh hai ke hamare bikhre hue daur mein saabit-qadmi ka raasta ek marboot shakhsiyat banne ka safar hai. Is safar ka khaka Kalma-e-Tayyiba mein hai, iski amali tarbiyat Arkaan-e-Islam mein hai, aur iski rehnumayi Ahl-e-Sunnat ki zinda riwayat mein hai.
Aaiye Zayd ki taraf wapas chalte hain. Usne apne andarooni khalishar ka ilaaj shuru kar diya hai. Usne apne phone ko ek taraf rakha aur apne muhalle ki masjid ke haftawaar dars mein ja baitha. Woh ek mustanad, muqaami aalim ki zabaan se Qur'an ki tafseer sun raha hai. Barson mein pehli baar, uske zehn mein chalne wali mutazaad aawazein khamosh hain. Usey wazahat aur is se bhi badh kar, ta'alluq ka ehsaas ho raha hai. Woh ab maloomat ke samandar mein gum ek tanha fard nahi; woh ek jama'at ka hissa hai. Uske dil mein sukeenat ki pehli leher utar rahi hai.
Yeh takmeel ka safar ek zindagi ka safar hai. Yeh kisi ek bade qadam se nahi, balki ek niyyat wale pehle qadam se shuru hota hai. Aaj hi apna pehla qadam muntakhab kijiye:
1.  **Huzoor ka Qadam:** Is hafte, apni paanch namazon mein se koi ek namaz muntakhab karein. In chand minton ke liye, phone band kar dein, darwaza band karein, aur niyyat karein ke aap mukammal taur par apne ek haqeeqi aaqa ke huzoor haazir hain.
2.  **Suhbat ka Qadam:** Is maheene, majaazi tanhayi ki zanjeerein todein. Nek logon ki suhbat mein ba-nafs-e-nafees baithne ka ek mauqa talaash karein. Yeh aapki muqaami masjid ka dars, zikr ki mehfil, ya kisi buzurg ki ziyarat ho sakti hai.
3.  **Khidmat ka Qadam:** Is maheene, apni jama'at ke liye baghair kisi tareef ki ummeed ke, khidmat ka koi ek chota sa kaam talaash karein. Kisi ki be-laus madad karna khud-gharzi mein mubtala dil ka tez-tareen ilaaj hai.
4.  **Tadabbur ka Qadam:** Is hafte, das minute nikaal kar Qur'an ki is tamseel par ghaur karein: "kayi aaqaaon wala shakhs" (Surah Az-Zumar, Ayat 29). Khud se poochein: meri apni zindagi mein woh "kayi aaqa" kaun hain?

Saabit-qadmi ka yeh raasta mehnat-talab zaroor hai, lekin chunke yeh sachayi aur fitrat ka raasta hai, isliye bil-aakhir yahi ek mushkil duniya mein chalne ke liye sab se seedha, sab se zyada faida-mand, aur isi liye sab se aasaan raasta hai.
