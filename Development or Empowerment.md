# AL-KHAZANA AL-KUBRA
**(Hikmat-e-Amli ka Azeem Zakheera: Tamkeen banaam Tanmiya)**
*Scope: Infradi (Individual), Khandani (Family), aur Ijtemai (National).*

---

## HISSA AWWAL: BUNYADI FALSAFA (Core Philosophy)
*Ye samajhna ke hum "Sajawat" ke peeche bhag rahe hain ya "Taqat" ke.*

1.  **Tanmiya (Development) kya hai?**
    *   Ye mahez sadkein banana, shopping malls khade karna, aur GDP ke numbers badhana hai, jabke nizaam (system) aur wasail (resources) dushman ke haath mein hon.
    *   **Tashbeeh (Metaphor):** Ye "Sone ka Pinjra" (Golden Cage) hai. Aap ko waqt par khana mil raha hai, lekin aap azaad nahi hain. Maalik jab chahe daana band kar de, jab chahe zibah kar de.
2.  **Tamkeen (Empowerment) kya hai?**
    *   Ye Allah ki zameen par Allah ka nizaam qaim karne ki "Salahiyat" aur "Taqat" hai. Iske liye khud-mukhtari (independence) shart hai.
    *   **Tashbeeh:** Ye wo "Kulhari" (Axe) hai jis se aap apni lakdi khud kaat-te hain aur apna ghar khud banate hain. Ye mushkil hai, magar ye Izzat hai.

---

## HISSA DOUM: INFRADI SATAH (Level 1: The Individual)
*Agar eent (brick) kamzor hogi, to deewar gir jayegi. Pehle khud ko "Tamkeen" karein.*

### **1. Taqatwar Momin (Physical & Mental Power)**
> **Hadees:** *"Al-Mu'min al-qawiyu khairun..."* (Sahih Muslim)
> **Tarjuma:** "Taqatwar Momin Allah ko zayada mehboob aur behtar hai kamzor Momin se."

*   **Be-baak Tajziya (Ruthless Analysis):**
    *   Kya aap jismani taur par fit hain? Ya aap "Developed" hain (motape ka shikaar)? Ek sust aur kamzor Musalman Consumer to ho sakta hai, Mujahid nahi.
    *   Kya aap zehni taur par azaad hain? Ya aap subah shaam TikTok aur Reels par waqt zaya kar rahe hain?
*   **Faisla:** Gym jana, Martial Arts seekhna, aur apni sehat banana sirf "shauq" nahi, ye *Tamkeen* ki tayyari hai.

### **2. Haath ka Hunar (The Prophetic Profession)**
> **Hadees:** *"Kisi ne us se behtar khana nahi khaya jo apne haath ki kamayi se khaya ho."* (Bukhari)

*   **Masla:** Aaj humara naujawan "Clerk" banna chahta hai, "Maalik" nahi. Hum "Skills" (Hunar) ko haqeer samajhte hain.
*   **Haqeeqat:** Dawood (A.S) lohay ki zirra (armor) banate thay. Zakariya (A.S) badhai (carpenter) thay.
*   **Faisla:** Har naujawan ke paas ek "Hard Skill" honi chahiye (Coding, Farming, Mechanics, Manufacturing). Degree sirf kaghaz hai, Hunar asli taqat hai.

### **3. Ma'ashi Ghulami (Personal Debt)**
*   **Usool:** Nabi ﷺ ne maqrooz (debtor) ki namaz-e-janaza padhane se inkaar kiya tha shuru mein.
*   **Aaj ka Haal:** Humari gaadi bank ki hai, ghar bank ka hai, mobile installments par hai. Hum "Azad" nahi, hum Bank ke "Ghulam" hain.
*   **Tamkeen:** Credit Card tod do. Kam khao, magar azaad raho. *"Qarz le kar shaan dikhana, khud-kushi ki ek qism hai."*

---

## HISSA SOUM: KHANDANI SATAH (Level 2: The Family)
*Ghar ek Qila (Fortress) hai.*

### **1. Ghar ka Sarbarah (The Shepherd)**
> **Hadees:** *"Tum mein se har shakhs raa'i (shepherd/zimmedar) hai..."* (Bukhari)

*   **Tajziya:** Baap ghar ka "Hukmaran" hai. Agar aap apne drawing room mein Shariat nafiz nahi kar sakte (TV par fahashi, be-pardagi), to aap Hukmaran se mulk mein Shariat nafiz karne ka mutalba kaise kar sakte hain?
*   **Tamkeen:** Apne ghar ko "Consumer Unit" mat banao, ise "Tarbiyat Gah" banao.

### **2. Khawateen ka Kirdaar (Architects of Men)**
*   **Ghalt fehmi:** "Taraqqi" ye kehti hai ke aurat tab kamyab hai jab wo corporate office mein boss ke liye paisa kamaye.
*   **Haqeeqat:** "Tamkeen" ye kehti hai ke aurat tab kamyab hai jab wo naye "Salahuddin Ayyubi" ki parwarish kare.
*   **Economic Power:** Ghar ki ma'eeshat (Economy) aurat ke haath mein hai. Agar wo faisla kar le ke "Ghair-Mulki Brands" nahi aayenge, to dushman ki ma'eeshat hil jayegi.

---

## HISSA CHAHARUM: QAUMI SATAH (Level 3: The Nation)
*Riyasat, Siyasat aur Ma'eeshat.*

### **1. Qurani Dastoor (The Divine Mandate)**

**A. Tayyari ka Hukam (Military Industry)**
> **Ayat:** *"Wa a'iddu lahum ma'istata'tum min quwwah..."* (Al-Anfal: 60)
> **Tarjuma (Kanzul Iman):** "Aur unke liye tayyar rakho jo quwwat tumhe ban pade."
> **Tajziya:** Lafz **"A'iddu"** (Tayyar karo/Banao) hai. Khareedna "Tayyari" nahi hai.
> *   Agar hum tank aur missile dushman se khareed rahe hain, to hum ne is ayat par amal nahi kiya.
> *   Jo qaum apni goli (bullet) khud nahi bana sakti, wo Gunahgaar (Athim) hai.

**B. Lohay ki Taqat (Heavy Industry)**
> **Ayat:** *"Wa anzalna al-hadeeda feehi ba'sun shadeed..."* (Al-Hadid: 25)
> **Tajziya:** Allah ne "Lohay" (Iron) ko "Shadeed Taqat" (Warfare) se joda hai.
> *   Baghair apni Steel Mills aur Heavy Industry ke, koi mulk "Azad" nahi ho sakta.

**C. Khazana aur Rizq (Economy)**
> **Ayat:** *"Ij'alnee 'ala khaza'in il-ard..."* (Yusuf: 55)
> **Tajziya:** Yusuf (A.S) ne "Khazana" (Finance Ministry) manga. Kyun? Kyunke agar anaaj (food) aur paisa dushman ke control mein hoga, to Insaaf nahi ho sakta.
> *   "Jo tumhe khilata hai, wo tum par hukumat karta hai."

### **2. Nabawi Manhaj (The Prophetic Model)**

**A. Kulhari wala Waqiya (The Axe Model)**
*   **Sunnah:** Nabi ﷺ ne Ansari sahabi ko bheek (welfare) nahi di. Unka ghar ka saaman bikwa kar "Kulhari" (Means of Production) le kar di.
*   **Sabak:** Hukumat ka kaam logon ko "BISP/Welfare" par lagana nahi hai, balki unhein "Karobar" aur "Auzaar" (Tools) dena hai. Bheek mangne wali qaum kabhi sar utha kar nahi chal sakti.

**B. Madina ki Market (Strategic Independence)**
*   **Sunnah:** Nabi ﷺ ne Yahudiyon ki market todne ke liye Musalmano ki alag, tax-free market banayi.
*   **Sabak:** Humein apna "Islamic Common Market" banana hoga. Dollar ki ghulami chhor kar Gold ya Barter system par aana hoga.

---

## HISSA PANJUM: TARIKHI IBRAT (Historical Warnings)
*Mazi ki ghaltiyan.*

**1. Andalus (Spain) ka Anjaam**
*   **Manzar:** Unke paas Alhambra jaise mehal thay, sadkon par lights thi (Bohat "Developed" thay).
*   **Ghalti:** Unhone apna difaa (defense) kiraye ke faujiyon par chhor diya aur Christian badshahon ko "Tax" (Tribute) diya.
*   **Nateeja:** Wo tabah ho gaye. *"Baghair Talwar ke Taraqqi, qasai ke samne khadi moti bher (sheep) ki tarah hai."*

**2. Ottoman Debt (Qarz ka Jaal)**
*   **Waqiya:** Khilafat-e-Usmania ne mehal banane aur fauj ko modern karne ke liye Europe se soodi qarz (loans) liye.
*   **Nateeja:** Jab qarz wapas nahi kar sake, to Europe ne unke Khazane par qabza kar liya. Aaj ka IMF wahi purani "East India Company" hai.

---

## HISSA SHASHUM: FIKRI ASLAAH (Intellectual Weapons)
*Zehni ghulami todne ke liye.*

**1. Iqbal ka Shaheen (The Bird Parable)**
> *“Aaye taair-e-lahooti us rizq se maut achi,*
> *Jis rizq se aati ho parwaaz mein kotahi.”*
> **Mafhoom:** Aye Musalman! Wo rozi (aid/loan) lene se maut behtar hai jo teri azadi ki udaan ko rok de.

**2. Ibn Khaldun ka Qaida**
> *"Maaghloob (haari hui) qaum hamesha Ghalib (jeetne wali) qaum ki naqal karti hai - libaas mein, sha'air mein, aur taur tareeqon mein."*
> **Tajziya:** Humari ye daud (rat race) ke hum "Dubai" ban jayein ya "New York" ban jayein, ye ehsas-e-kamtari (Inferiority Complex) hai.

---

## HISSA HAFTUM: LUGAT (Key Vocabulary)
*In alfaaz ko yaad kar lijiye:*

*   **Zaati Tamkeen (ذاتی تمکین):** Personal Self-Sufficiency (Khud-kafalat).
*   **Qaumi Tamkeen (قومی تمکین):** National Sovereignty.
*   **Mustahlik (مستہلک):** Consumer (Maal zaya karne wala).
*   **Muntij (منتج):** Producer (Maal banane wala).
*   **Kashkol (کشکول):** Bheek ka pyala (Qaumi Qarz).
*   **Hunr (ہنر):** Skill/Craft (Asli Daulat).
*   **Ghulami-e-Zehni (غلامی ذہنی):** Mental Slavery.
*   **Wahn (وہن):** Dunya ki mohabbat aur maut ka darr.

---

**Khulasa (Summary for the Speaker):**
Shuruwaat **Fard (Individual)** se karein: *"Kya tum taqatwar ho?"*
Phir **Ghar** par aayein: *"Kya tumhara ghar Qila hai?"*
Phir **Qaum** par aayein: *"Kya humari fauj aur ma'eeshat azaad hai?"*
Aur aakhir mein **Hal (Solution)** dein: *"Bheek ka pyala todo, aur Kulhari uthao."*

Here is the full **Khutbah (Sermon)**, drafted in **Roman Urdu**.

It weaves the **soulful sorrow of Maulana Nadwi** (to awaken the heart) with the **structural rigor of Maulana Maududi** (to awaken the mind). It is tailored for the modern context, specifically addressing the challenges and opportunities for Muslims in places like India.

***

# KHUTBAH: KASHKOL-E-GADA'I YA NABI (ﷺ) KI KULHARI?
**(The Begging Bowl or The Prophet's Axe?)**

### Isme Syed Nadwi aur Syed Maududi ka zikr hai, bas naam hata kar baate ki ja sakti hai...
### **PART 1: KHUTBAT-AL-HAAJAH (The Opening)**
*Assalamu Alaikum Wa Rahmatullah.*

**Al-hamdu lillahi nahmaduhu wa nasta'inuhu wa nastaghfiruh...**
*(Standard Arabic Opening)*

**Amma Ba'ad.**
**Fa A'udhu Billahi Min Ash-Shaytan Ir-Rajim.**
**Bismillah Ir-Rahman Ir-Rahim.**

> **"Wa a'iddu lahum ma'istata'tum min quwwah..."**
> *(Aur tayyar rakho unke muqable ke liye jo kuch quwwat tumse ban pade...)* [Al-Anfal: 60]

---

### **PART 2: THE LAMENT (Nadwi Style - The Diagnosis)**

**Azizan-e-Millat!**

Aaj jab hum apne shehron par nazar daalte hain—chahe wo Mumbai ki chamakti sadkein hon, Hyderabad ke shaadi-khanay hon, ya Dubai ke oonche towers hon—humein kya nazar aata hai?
Humein eentein (bricks) nazar aati hain. Humein lights nazar aati hain. Humein mehal nazar aate hain.
Hum seena taan kar kehte hain: *"Dekho, Musalman kitni taraqqi kar raha hai."*

Lekin Maulana Ali Miyan Nadwi (R.A) ne ek baar Europe ki buildings dekh kar kaha tha:
*"Tumne sheher to basa liye, magar aadmi kho diya."*

Aaj humara haal Andalus (Spain) ke aakhri daur jaisa hai. Unke paas Alhambra jaisa mehal tha, magar difaa (defense) ke liye wo dushman ke mohtaj thay.
Aaj humare paas *Tanmiya* (Development) hai—humare paas iPhone hai, AC gaadiyan hain, brand new kapde hain.
Magar humare paas *Tamkeen* (Power/Empowerment) nahi hai.

**Faraq samjhiye!**
*   **Tanmiya (Development)** ye hai ke aap ke paas "Sone ka Pinjra" (Golden Cage) ho, jismein waqt par khana milta hai.
*   **Tamkeen (Empowerment)** ye hai ke aap ke paas jungle mein apna "Qila" (Fortress) ho, jahan koi aap ki ijazat ke baghair daakhil na ho sake.

Aaj Ummah "Developed" hai magar "Ghulam" hai.
Kyun? Kyunke hum "Consumer" (Mustahlik) ban gaye hain.
Humari subah Google ke alarm se shuru hoti hai, humara nashta Nestle se aata hai, humari dawa Pfizer ki hai, aur humari soch Facebook ki hai.

Humare paas cheezein hain, magar nizaam (system) humara nahi hai.

---

### **PART 3: THE ANALYSIS (Maududi Style - The Root Cause)**

**Mere Bhaiyon!**

Is zillat ki wajah kya hai?
Wajah ye hai ke hum ne **"Tayyari"** (Preparation) ka matlab bhula diya hai.

Allah ne Quran mein farmaya: **"Wa A'iddu..."** (Tayyar karo/Banao).
Allah ne ye nahi kaha **"Ishtaru"** (Khareedo).

Aaj humara naujawan samajhta hai ke agar usne achi degree le li aur kisi Multinational Company mein "Clerk" ya "Manager" lag gaya, to wo kamyab hai.
Ye kamyabi nahi hai, ye **"Economic Slavery"** (Ma'ashi Ghulami) hai.

Maulana Maududi (R.A) farmaya karte thay ke agar tumhara talimi nizaam dushman ka banaya hua hai, aur tumhara ma'ashi nizaam (banking system) dushman ke control mein hai, to tum azaad hokar bhi ghulam ho.

Zara sochiye!
Hindustan mein Musalman "Reservation" ke liye rota hai. "Humein naukri do, humein naukri do."
Arre, tum us Nabi (ﷺ) ki ummat ho jisne haath failane wale ko **"Dene Wala"** (Employer) bana diya tha!
Tum naukri maangne wale kyun ban gaye? Tum naukri dene wale (Job Creators) kyun nahi bane?

Humare paas **Wipro ke Azim Premji** aur **Cipla ke Yusuf Hamied** jaise log kyun kam hain?
Kyunke humne apne bacchon ko "Safe Job" dhoondna sikhaya, "Risk" lena nahi sikhaya. Humne unhein "Consumer" banaya, "Producer" nahi.

---

### **PART 4: THE EVIDENCE (The Prophetic Model)**

**Aiye, Seerat ke us waqiye ki taraf jo humari aankhein khol de.**

Ek Ansari sahabi (R.A) Nabi Kareem (ﷺ) ke paas aaye. Bhooke thay. Maanga ke kuch madad karein.
Aaj ka daur hota to hum unhein "Zakat Fund" se paise de dete, ya "BPL Card" bana dete.
Lekin Rasoolullah (ﷺ) ne kya kiya?

Aap (ﷺ) ne poocha: *"Ghar mein kuch hai?"*
Unhone kaha: *"Ek taat (cloth) aur ek pani ka pyala."*
Aap (ﷺ) ne wo dono cheezein mangwayin aur **Neelaam** (Auction) kar di. Do dirham mein.
Ek dirham ka khana diya, aur dusre dirham se kaha: **"Jao, Kulhari (Axe) khareedo."**

Nabi (ﷺ) ne khud apne mubarak haathon se us kulhari mein lakdi ka dasta lagaya.
Ghaur kijiye! **Technology Transfer!** Nabi (ﷺ) ne tool bana kar diya.
Aur farmaya: *"Jao lakdiyan kaato aur becho."*

15 din baad wo shakhs aaya, uske paas 10 dirham thay. Wo khush-haal ho chuka tha.
Aap (ﷺ) ne farmaya: *"Ye tere liye behtar hai is se ke Qayamat ke din bhook ka daagh tere chehre par ho."*

**Is Hadees se 3 Inqilabi Sabak milte hain:**
1.  **Bheek/Welfare ka Khatma:** Nabi (ﷺ) ne "Handout" nahi diya, "Tool" diya.
2.  **Asset Liquidation:** Ghar ka bekaar saaman bech kar "Business Capital" banaya.
3.  **Production:** "Consumer" ko "Producer" mein badal diya.

Aaj humare paas lakho Muslims hain jo "Gulf" (Middle East) mein mazdoori karte hain. Paisa bhejte hain, yahan bada ghar banate hain, aur phir paise khatam hone par wapas mazdoori karne chale jate hain.
Ye **Tanmiya** hai.
Agar wahi paisa kisi "Factory," "Small Business," ya "Technology" mein lagta, to wo **Tamkeen** hoti.

---

### **PART 5: THE CALL TO ACTION (Way Forward)**

**To ab humein karna kya hai?**

Main aaj yahan se teen tabqon (groups) ko mukhatib karna chahta hoon.

**1. Naujawanon se (To the Youth):**
Apne haathon mein **Hunar** (Skill) paida karo. Sirf degree ke peeche mat bhago.
Dawood (A.S) lohay ki zirra (armor) banate thay. Ye unka hunar tha.
Aaj ka loha "Code" hai, "AI" hai, "Manufacturing" hai.
Agar tumhare paas degree hai magar tum koi cheez "Bana" (Create) nahi sakte, to tumhari degree raddi ka tukda hai.
Apne andar **"Ijaad"** (Invention) ki salahiyat paida karo, **"Jugaad"** ki nahi.

**2. Khawateen se (To the Women):**
Ghar ki ma'eeshat (Economy) aapke haath mein hai.
Aap faisla karein ke humare ghar mein wo cheez nahi aayegi jo dushman ki company ki ho, jab tak ke majboori na ho.
Apne bacchon ko "Brands" ka ghulam mat banao. Unhein "Salahuddin" aur "Fateh" banao.
Agar aapne "Saadgi" (Simplicity) apna li, to aapke mard qarz (debt) ke jaal se azaad ho jayenge.

**3. Tajiron aur Maaldaaron se (To the Businessmen):**
Apni Zakat aur Sadqaat ko "Khana khilane" (Feeding) tak mehdood mat rakho.
Ghareeb ko "Machli" mat do, usay "Kaanta" (Fishing rod) le kar do.
Micro-Finance shuru karo. Chote karobar khade karo.
Humari community ko **"Kashkol-e-Gada'i"** (Begging Bowl) se nikaal kar **"Yad-e-Ulya"** (Dene wala haath) banao.

---

### **PART 6: THE CONCLUSION (Iqbalian Climax)**

**Azizan-e-Watan!**

Yaad rakhiye, izzat maangne se nahi milti, izzat kamayi jaati hai.
Dunya kamzor ki aah nahi sunti, taqatwar ki baat sunti hai.

Allah ne lohay (Iron) mein "Ba'sun Shadeed" (Badi Taqat) rakhi hai.
Jab tak humari apni industry nahi hogi, humara apna media nahi hoga, humara apna nizaam nahi hoga, humari Masjidein bhi mehfooz nahi hongi.

Allama Iqbal ne isi dard ko mehsoos karte hue kaha tha:

> *“Aaye taair-e-lahooti us rizq se maut achi,*
> *Jis rizq se aati ho parwaaz mein kotahi.”*

(Aye asmani parinde! Wo rozi, wo naukri, wo aid lene se maut behtar hai, jis se teri azaadi ki udaan ruk jaye).

Aiye aaj ye ehad (promise) karein ke hum "Mustahlik" (Consumer) nahi, "Muntij" (Producer) banenge.
Hum "Taraqqi" ke dhoke mein nahi aayenge, hum "Tamkeen" hasil karenge.
Hum Bheek ka Pyala todenge, aur Nabi (ﷺ) ki Kulhari uthayenge.

**Barakallahu lee wa lakum fil Quranil Azeem...**

*(Closing Dua in Arabic followed by Urdu Dua)*

**Dua:**
*Ya Allah! Humari Ummah ko ma'ashi ghulami se nijaat de.*
*Ya Allah! Humare naujawanon ko hunar-mand bana.*
*Ya Allah! Humein lene wala nahi, dene wala bana.*
*Ya Allah! Humein dushman ki 'Taraqqi' se bacha, aur apni 'Tamkeen' ata farma.*

**Wa Aqeemus Salah.**
