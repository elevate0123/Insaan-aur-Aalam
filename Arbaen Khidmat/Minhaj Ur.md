# PROJECT DOSSIER: "Minhaj al-Khidmat" (Khidmat ka Rasta)
**Shaikh Ul Islam Trust ke liye ek Rohani Field Manual**

### 1. Bunyadi Falsafa aur Maqsad
*  **Masla:** Aaj kal ke khairati idare "Corporate" zaban, ana (ego) par mabni qayadat, shakhon (branches) ki aapsi raqabat, aur numayish (*Riya*) ka shikaar hain. Ghareebon ko sirf ek "number" samajhna ek rohani bimari hai.
*  **Hal:** Ek 40-stations wala rohani manual (*Arba'een*) jo sirf hosla-afzai ke liye nahi, balki *Muhasabah* (apne nafs ka hisab) ke liye ek aaina ban kar kaam kare. Ye "social work" ke zehen ko badal kar "Tazkiyah" (rohani pakeezgi) ke raste par le jayega.
*  **Maqsad:** Jab hum *Nafs* par waar karenge aur *Amanah* ke sakht Fiqh ko samjhayenge, tabhi Trust ki tamam shakhon mein mukammal *Ikhlas* (sincerity) paida hoga. Is se idare ki izzat bhi bachegi aur Khadimon ki Aakhirat bhi.

### 2. Zaban aur Lehja (Parameters)
*  **Aqeedah:** *Ahle Sunnah wal Jama'ah*. Lehja *Adab*, *Nisbat* (Awliya aur Nabi ﷺ se talluq), aur is yaqeen par mabni hoga ke Khadim sirf Allah ke khazane ka ek distributor hai.
*  **Tarjuma:** Quran ke tamam tarjume **Kanzul Iman** (Imam Ahmad Raza Khan) ke mutabiq honge taake Ambiya-e-Kiram ka maqam aur *Adab* barkarar rahe.
*  **Lehja:** Classical, bhari, aur dil ko cheer dene wala. Modern zaban ko sakhti se reject kiya gaya hai. Iska lehja Imam al-Ghazali ki nasehaton jaisa hoga.

### 3. Official Nomenclature (Istilahat)
*  **Khadim:** "Volunteer" ya "Member" ki jagah.
*  **Ameer / Khadim-e-Awwal:** "Branch Manager" ya "President" ki jagah.
*  **Wali-e-Nemat / Sa'il:** "Beneficiary" ya "Poor" ki jagah.
*  **Amanah:** "Donation," "Funds," ya "Budget" ki jagah.
*  **Tafakkur / Muhasabah:** "Self-Help" ya "Journaling" ki jagah.

### STAGE 1: Al-Bidayah (Androoni Bunyad)

**1. Niyat ki Pakeezgi (Al-Ikhlas)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur unhein sirf is baat ka hukm diya gaya tha ke woh Allah ki ibadat karein, apna deen sirf Usi ke liye khalis rakhein..."* (Surah Al-Bayyinah, 98:5)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Qayamat ke din sabse pehle jin shakhs ka faisla hoga... woh ek shakhs hoga jisne apna maal kharch kiya taake log kahein ke 'yeh toh bada sakhi hai.' Phir usay chehre ke bal ghaseeta jayega aur aag mein phenk diya jayega."* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Pehle hi safhay par *Ana* (ego) ko torna. Woh khaufnaak haqiqat jab khairat sirf shohrat aur naam ke liye ki jaye.

**2. Amanah ka Khauf (Al-Amanah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Ay iman walo! Allah aur Uske Rasool ke sath khayanat na karo, aur na hi apni amanaton mein jaan boojh kar khayanat karo."* (Surah Al-Anfal, 8:27)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Us shakhs mein koi iman nahi jis mein amanat-dari nahi, aur us mein koi deen nahi jo wada pura nahi karta."* (Musnad Ahmad)
*   **Tafakkur:** *Bait-ul-Mal* (awami sarmaya) ka sakht Fiqh. Trust ke zariye apne zaati masalih ke liye kaam karna iman mein khayanat hai.

**3. Numayish ki Bimari (Riya)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Ay iman walo! Apni sadqat ko ehsan jata kar aur dukh de kar zaya na karo, us shakhs ki tarah jo apna maal sirf logon ko dikhane ke liye kharch karta hai..."* (Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:264)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Mujhe tumhare liye sabse zyada darr 'shirk-e-asghar' (chhote shirk) ka hai." Sahaba ne pucha, "Woh kya hai?" Aap ﷺ ne farmaya, "Riya (dikhawa)."* (Musnad Ahmad)
*   **Tafakkur:** Jadeed zariya-e-numayish aur khud-pasandi ki bimari. Jab dikhawe ko "Chhota Shirk" kaha jaye, toh Khadim apni numayishi tasveeron ko zahir karne se pehle kanpega.

**4. Hisab-Kitab ka Tawazun (Tawazun)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Agar tum khule aam sadqa karte ho toh yeh bahut achhi baat hai; aur agar ise chhupa kar ghareebon ko dete ho, toh yeh tumhare liye sabse behtar hai..."* (Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:271)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Jisne Quran ko buland awaaz se padha woh uski tarah hai jisne khule aam sadqa kiya, aur jisne khamoshi se padha woh uski tarah hai jisne chhupa kar sadqa kiya."* (Sunan Abu Dawud)
*   **Tafakkur:** Idare ke hisab-kitab ki zaroorat aur dil ki pakeezgi ke darmiyan tawazun paida karna.

**5. Kisi Badle ki Chah na Rakhna (La Nuriidu Minkum Jaza'an)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"[Kehte hue ke], 'Hum tumhein sirf Allah ki raza ke liye khilate hain. Hum tumse na koi badla chahte hain aur na hi koi shukriya.'"* (Surah Al-Insan, 76:9)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** Sayyidah Ayesha (R.A) jab sadqa bhejti toh us khandan ki duayein yaad kar letin. Phir wohi duayein unke liye kartin aur kehti: *"Hum unki duayein unhi ko lautate hain, taake hamara sadqa sirf Allah ke liye rahe."* (Al-Musannaf)
*   **Tafakkur:** *Ahle al-Bayt* ka aala meyar. Khadim ko yeh sikhana ke woh *Sa'il* se shukriya ki umeed bhi na rakhe.

**6. Chune Jane ki Saadat (Istifa')**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur Allah ki raah mein us tarah jiddo-jahad karo jaisa uske liye haq hai. Usne tumhein chun liya hai..."* (Surah Al-Hajj, 22:78)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Jab Allah kisi bande ke liye bhalaayi chahta hai, toh usay (khidmat ke liye) istemal karta hai." Sahaba ne pucha, "Kaise istemal karta hai?" Farmaya, "Usay maut se pehle nek amaal ki taraf guide karta hai."* (Sunan at-Tirmidhi)
*   **Tafakkur:** Us Khadim ka gharoor khatam karna jo samajhta hai ke woh apna waqt aur maal de kar Trust par ehsan kar raha hai.

**7. Apne Aap par Naaz Karne ka Khatra (Ujb)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"[Yaad karo] Hunayn ka din, jab tumhari zyada tadad ne tumhein gharoor dilaya, magar woh tumhare kisi kaam na aaya..."* (Surah At-Tawbah, 9:25)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Teen cheezein tabah kar dene wali hain: nafs ki khwahishat, lalach, aur insan ka apne aap par naaz karna (Ujb)."* (Al-Tabarani)
*   **Tafakkur:** Us *Khadim-e-Awwal* ke liye ilaaj jo apni shohrat aur bade sarmaye ko dekh kar bhool jata hai ke kamyabi sirf Allah ki taraf se hai.

**8. Sadqa Pehle Apne Ghar Walon ko (Huquq al-Ahl)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Woh aapse puchte hain ke kya kharch karein. Keh dijiye, 'Jo kuch bhalaai kharch karo woh maa-baap, rishtedar aur yateemon ke liye hai...'"* (Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:215)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Ek dinar jo tum Allah ki raah mein kharch karo... aur ek dinar jo apne ghar walon par kharch karo—sabse zyada sawab usme hai jo tumne apne ghar walon par kharch kiya."* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Us shakhs ke liye hosh-yar karne wali baat jo makhlooq ke liye toh masiha hai, magar apne ghar walon ke liye ek ajnabi hai.

**9. Halal Rizq ka Adab (Rizq-e-Halal)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Ay iman walo! Un pakiza cheezon mein se kharch karo jo tumne kamayi hain..."* (Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:267)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** Nabi ﷺ ne ek aise shakhs ka zikr kiya jo lambe safar se aaya, dhool mein lipta hua, hath uthaye keh raha tha, *'Ay Mere Rab!'* Magar uska khana haram tha, libas haram tha... *"Toh phir uski dua kaise qabool hogi?"* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Trust apne Khadims ki duayon par chalta hai. Agar Khadim ka *Rizq* haram hai, toh Trust ki rohani dhaal mein surakh ho jayega.

**10. Khamosh Khadim (Al-Khafi)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur apne aap ko sabr ke sath un logon ke sath rakho jo subah aur shaam apne Rab ko pukarte hain, Uska chehra talash karte hain. Aur apni nazrein unse na hatao..."* (Surah Al-Kahf, 18:28)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Yaqeenan, Allah us bande se mohabbat karta hai jo parhezgar ho, be-niyaz ho, aur poshida (hidden/Al-Khafi) ho."* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Us khamosh Khadim ki qadar karna jo kabhi mehfilon mein nahi bulaya jata aur idare ki nazron mein anjaan hai, magar Asmanon mein mashhoor hai.

### STAGE 2: Al-Maydan (Maidan-e-Khidmat)

**11. Wali-e-Nemat aur Nazron ki Hifazat (Ghad al-Basar)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"...Tum unhein dekh kar samjhoge ke woh ghareeb hain kyunke woh sabr karte hain. Tum unhein unki nishani se pehchanoge, woh logon se zid kar ke nahi mangte..."* (Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:273)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Mujhe apne kamzoron mein talash karo, kyunke tumhein rizq aur madad unhi kamzoron ki wajah se milti hai."* (Sunan Abu Dawud)
*   **Tafakkur:** Ghareeb shakhs asal mein VIP hai. Woh woh zariya hai jis se Trust ko sarmaya milta hai. Khadim ko chahiye ke rashan dete waqt apni nazrein neechi rakhe, taake *Sa'il* ko ghareebi ki zillat mehsoos na ho.

**12. Ehsan Jatane ki Mana'at (Al-Mann wal-Aza)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Jo log Allah ki raah mein apna maal kharch karte hain aur phir uske baad ehsan nahi jatate aur na hi dukh dete hain, unka sawab..."* (Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:262)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Teen log aise hain jinse Allah Qayamat ke din kalaam nahi karega... [unmein se ek] woh shakhs hai jo logon ko apne diye hue sadqe yaad dilata hai (Al-Mannan)."* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Ek chhota sa takabbur bhara jumla ("Yaad hai pichle saal humne tumhari madad ki thi?") poore sawab ko mita deta hai. Khidmat ek bande ki tarah honi chahiye.

**13. Yateemon ki Azmat (Kafalah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Pas yateem par sakhti na karo. Aur mangne wale ko jhidko mat."* (Surah Ad-Duha, 93:9-10)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** Nabi ﷺ ne apni shahadat wali ungli aur darmiyani ungli ko mila kar farmaya: *"Main aur yateem ki kafalat karne wala Jannat mein is tarah honge."* (Sahih al-Bukhari)
*   **Tafakkur:** Aap sirf ek bachay ki madad nahi kar rahe; aap Nabi ﷺ ke mehmanon ki khidmat kar rahe hain. Yateem ke samne thar-tharana chahiye, kyunke unka asal Sarparast khud Rasool-Allah ﷺ hain.

**14. Shifa ke Hath aur Muskurata Chehra (Shifa & Tabassum)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"...Aur jisne ek jaan bachayi, usne goya puri insaniyat ko bachaya."* (Surah Al-Ma'idah, 5:32)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Allah Qayamat ke din farmayega: 'Ay bete Adam! Main beemar tha aur tune Meri ayadat nahi ki...' Kya tujhe nahi maloom ke Mere is bande ki beemari mein Main uske sath tha?'"* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Aspatalon aur blood camps mein Allah ko talash karna. Beemar ke liye ek narm chehra rakhna, chahe Khadim khud kitna hi thaka hua kyun na ho.

**15. Ilm ki Roshni (Nur al-Ilm)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"...Allah tum mein se iman walon aur jinhein ilm diya gaya, unke darjaat buland farmayega..."* (Surah Al-Mujadila, 58:11)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Yaqeenan, Allah, Uske farishte, asman aur zameen ke rehne wale, yahan tak ke apne bil ki chiyunti aur samandar ki machhli bhi us shakhs par durood bhejte hain jo logon ko bhalaayi sikhata hai."* (Sunan at-Tirmidhi)
*   **Tafakkur:** Asatiza ke liye nasehat. Ek nasal ko jahalat se bachane ka rohani wazan samjhein; yeh ehsas ke puri kaynat us shakhs ke liye dua karti hai jo zehno ki tameer karta hai.

**16. Sulah aur Islah (Ighathah & Islah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Momin toh aapas mein bhai bhai hain, pas apne bhaiyon ke darmiyan sulah (Islah) karao. Aur Allah se daro taake tum par raham kiya jaye."* (Surah Al-Hujurat, 49:10)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Kya main tumhein kisi aisi cheez ki khabar na doon jo roze, namaz aur sadqe se behtar hai?" Sahaba ne arz kiya: "Ji haan!" Farmaya: "Do logon ke darmiyan sulah karana (Islah Dhat al-Bayn)..."* (Sunan Abu Dawud)
*   **Tafakkur:** Khandani mashwaro (counseling) ka woh azeem sawab jo aksar nazar-andaz kiya jata hai—talaqon ko rokna, bhaiyon ko milana, aur gharon ko tabahi se bachana.

**17. Khud-Kifayati ka Tohfa (Tamkeen)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"...taake yeh maal tumhare amiron ke darmiyan hi na ghumta rahe..."* (Surah Al-Hashr, 59:7)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Kisi ne us khane se behtar khana nahi khaya jo usne apne hathon ki mehnat se kamaya ho. Aur Allah ke Nabi Dawud (A.S) apne hathon ki kamayi se khate the."* (Sahih al-Bukhari)
*   **Tafakkur:** Silai machine ya riksha dena. Sirf ek din ki roti dene ke bajaye, ek khandan ko is qabil banana ke woh dobara kisi ke samne hath phelane ki zillat na uthayein.

**18. Raaz-dari aur Parda-poshi (Sitr)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Beshak woh log jo yeh pasand karte hain ke iman walon ke darmiyan behayayi phailay, unke liye dardnak azab hai..."* (Surah An-Nur, 24:19)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Jisne kisi Musalman ke aib (faults) ko chhupaya, Allah duniya aur Aakhirat mein uske aib chhupayega."* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Field survey ke dauran jab kisi *Sa'il* ka koi purana gunah ya ghar ka raaz khule. Trust ke sarmaye ki hifazat karna zaroori hai, magar us Musalman ki izzat ko mitti mein milana sakht mana hai.

**19. Alam-geer Rahmat (Rahmatan lil-Alameen)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur Humne aapko (ay Nabi ﷺ) tamam jahanon ke liye rahmat bana kar bheja."* (Surah Al-Anbiya, 21:107)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** Ek shakhs ne kuen se apne jootay mein pani bhar kar ek pyase kutte ko pilaya. *"Allah ne uske is amal ki qadar ki aur usay maaf kar diya." Sahaba ne pucha, "Kya janwaron ki khidmat par bhi sawab hai?" Farmaya, "Har zinda jandar ki khidmat mein sawab hai."* (Sahih al-Bukhari)
*   **Tafakkur:** Sirf Ummah tak mahdood na rahein. Raston se takleef-deh cheezein hatana aur gair-muslimon ke samne Islamic kirdar ka behtareen namuna pesh karna.

**20. Khalq mein Khaliq ka Deedar (Ru'yatul Khaliq fil Khalq)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur woh kisi ko badle ki umeed mein ehsan nahi karta, balki sirf apne Rab-e-Ali ke chehre (raza) ki talash mein hota hai. Aur jald woh razi ho jayega."* (Surah Al-Lail, 92:19-21)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Raham karne walon par Rahm karne wala (Allah) raham farmayega. Zameen walon par raham karo, asman wala tum par raham karega."* (Sunan at-Tirmidhi)
*   **Tafakkur:** Chahe talib-e-ilm ho, beemar ho ya bewa—yeh yaqeen rakhna ke makhlooq par ki gayi har raham-dili dar-asal Khaliq ke sath ek seedha len-den hai.

### STAGE 3: Al-Tanzim (Intizamia aur Bhaichara)

**21. Khidmat ka Bhaichara (Ukhuwwah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur Allah ki rassi ko sab mil kar mazbooti se thaam lo aur tafarruqa (division) mein na pado..."* (Surah Al-Imran, 3:103)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Momin ek dusre ke liye shafqat, hamdardi aur mohabbat mein ek jism ki tarah hain. Jab jism ke ek hisse mein takleef hoti hai, toh pura jism be-khwabi aur bukhar se jawab deta hai."* (Sahih Muslim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Shakhon (branches) ki aapsi raqabat ka khatma. Agar Shakh-A kamyab hoti hai, toh Shakh-B ko khushi honi chahiye. Dunyawi shohrat ki hoad Trust ki barkat ko khatam kar deti hai.

**22. Hasad ka Zeher (Hasad)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Ya phir woh logon se hasad karte hain us cheez par jo Allah ne unhein apne fazl se di hai?..."* (Surah An-Nisa, 4:54)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Hasad se bacho, kyunke hasad nekiyon ko is tarah kha jata hai jaise aag lakdi ko kha jati hai."* (Sunan Abu Dawud)
*   **Tafakkur:** Jab kisi dusre Khadim ko taraqqi mile, ya kisi dusri shakh ko bada sarmaya mile. Bhai ki kamyabi par hasad karna aapki apni raaton ki jagran ka sawab jala deta hai.

**23. Ameer ki Ita'at (Ita'ah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Ay iman walo! Allah ki ita'at karo aur Rasool ki, aur unki jo tum mein se ikhtiyar (authority) wale hain..."* (Surah An-Nisa, 4:59)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Jisne meri ita'at ki usne Allah ki ita'at ki... Aur jisne Ameer ki ita'at ki usne meri ita'at ki, aur jisne Ameer ki na-farmani ki usne meri na-farmani ki."* (Sahih al-Bukhari)
*   **Tafakkur:** Ground worker ke liye sakht hidayat. Ittehad (unity) ke liye Ameer ka hukm manna zaroori hai, chahe aap unki hikmat-e-amli se ittefaq na karein, bashart-e-ke woh gunah ka hukm na dein.

**24. Mashwarat aur Amanah (Shura & Amanah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"...aur unse mashwara karo. Phir jab tum faisla kar lo, toh Allah par tawakkul karo..."* (Surah Al-Imran, 3:159)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Jis shakhs ne kisi ko ek giroh par muqarrar kiya, jabke un mein koi dusra shakhs Allah ki nazron mein zyada pasandida tha, usne Allah, Uske Rasool aur momineen ke sath khayanat ki."* (Al-Hakim)
*   **Tafakkur:** Ameeron ke liye tanbeeh. Dictatorship aur rishtedaari (nepotism) se bachna. Ameer ko chahiye ke apne Khadimon se mashwara kare aur kisi dost ya rishtedar ko qabil Khadim par tarjeeh na de.

**25. Ohde ki Talab ka Khatra (Talab al-Imarah)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aakhirat ka ghar Hum unke liye muqarrar karte hain jo zameen par gharoor aur fasad nahi chahte..."* (Surah Al-Qasas, 28:83)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Ay Abdur-Rahman! Ohde ki talab na karo, kyunke agar tumhein tumhari talab par ohda mila, toh tum usay apne bharose par chalaoge (Allah ki madad nahi milegi). Magar agar tumne talab nahi ki aur tumhein diya gaya, toh Allah tumhari madad farmayega."* (Sahih al-Bukhari)
*   **Tafakkur:** Ohde aur rutbe ki lalach. Jo shakhs mic, kursi ya sadarat (presidency) ke liye larta hai, woh Allah ki madad se mehroom ho jata hai. Qayadat ek bhari bojh hai jis se bhagna chahiye, na ke jise inaam samajh kar hasil kiya jaye.

**26. Bad-gumani ka Ilaaj (Husn al-Zann & Hifz al-Lisan)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Ay iman walo! Zyada bad-gumani se bacho. Beshak kuch gumaniyan gunah hoti hain. Aur ek dusre ki jasoosi na karo aur na ek dusre ki ghibat..."* (Surah Al-Hujurat, 49:12)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Bad-gumani se bacho, kyunke bad-gumani sabse jhooti baat hai. Jasoosi na karo, ek dusre ki ghibat na karo, hasad na karo... aur ay Allah ke bando, bhai bhai bano."* (Sahih al-Bukhari)
*   **Tafakkur:** Idare ki siyasat aur shakkon ka khatma. Yeh samajhna ke intizamia cheezein chupa rahi hai, ya WhatsApp par dusre members ki burayi karna, idare ki rohani dhaal ko tabah kar deta hai.

**27. Amiron ke Samne Izzat-e-Nafs (Istighna)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur apni nazrein un cheezon ki taraf na uthao jo Humne unki kuch qismon ko lutf ke liye di hain... Aur tumhare Rab ka rizq behtar aur hamesha rehne wala hai."* (Surah Ta-Ha, 20:131)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** Jibril (A.S) ne Nabi ﷺ se farmaya: *"Jaan lo ke momin ki izzat uski tahajjud hai, aur uski shaan logon se be-niyazi (Istighna) hai."* (Al-Hakim / Al-Bayhaqi)
*   **Tafakkur:** Donors ke sath talluq. Trust ko chahiye ke sarmaya dene wale ki izzat kare, magar Khadim ko chahiye ke unke samne jhuke nahi, khushamad na kare, aur sarmaye ke liye Islami usoolon par samjhota na kare. Asal Razzaq toh Allah hai.

**28. Ikhtilaf ka Adab (Adab al-Ikhtilaf)**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Aur Allah aur Uske Rasool ki ita'at karo, aur aapas mein jhagda na karo, warna tum buzdil ho jaoge aur tumhari quwwat khatam ho jayegi..."* (Surah Al-Anfal, 8:46)
*   **Hadith-e-Mubaraka:** *"Main Jannat ke kinare ek ghar us shakhs ke liye guarantee karta hoon jo jhagda karne se bache, chahe woh haq par hi kyun na ho..."* (Sunan Abu Dawud)
*   **Tafakkur:** Hikmat-e-amli (strategy) par ikhtilaf. Nek log kisi project ko chalane ke tariqe par ikhtilaf kar sakte hain. Yeh sabaq hai ke apni baat manwane ke bajaye bhaichare ko tarjeeh di jaye.

**29. Ohde se Hatne par Sabr (Tawadu')**
*   **Qurani Ayat:** *"Keh dijiye, 'Ay Allah! Tu hi badshahat ka malik hai, Tu jise chahe badshahat deta hai aur jis se chahe le leta hai...'"* (Surah Al-Imran, 3:26)

### Station 1: Niyat ki Pakeezgi (Al-Ikhlas)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Aur unhein sirf is baat ka hukm diya gaya tha ke woh Allah ki ibadat karein, apna deen sirf Usi ke liye khalis rakhein, aur har jhoot (shirk) se mun pher lein..."* 
*(Surah Al-Bayyinah, 98:5)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Qayamat ke din sabse pehle jin shakhs ka faisla hoga... woh ek shakhs hoga jise Allah ne sarmaya diya tha... Allah farmayega: 'Tune kya kiya?' Woh kahega: 'Maine koi aisa rasta nahi chhoda jahan Tu maal kharch karne ko pasand karta hai, magar maine Teri raza ke liye kharch kiya.' Allah farmayega: 'Tune jhoot bola. Tune sirf is liye kharch kiya taake log kahein ke woh bada sakhi hai. Aur yeh baat kahi ja chuki hai.' Phir usay chehre ke bal ghaseeta jayega aur aag mein phenk diya jayega."* 
*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Apni khidmat ki dehleez par thahro aur is haqiqat se kanpo. Jahannum ka pehla eendhan koi but-parast ya chor nahi, balki woh sakhawat karne wala shakhs hai jis ka dil na-pakeezah tha. *Nafs* fareb dene mein mahir hai. Yeh tumhein dopahar ki kadi dhoop mein paseena bahane par majboor karega, Trust ki khatir tumhari jawani ko thaka dega, magar khamoshi se tumhara sawab chura lega—sirf is liye ke tumhare dil mein logon ki wah-wah ki talab hogi.

Maidan mein utarne se pehle, apni rooh se sawal karo. Kya tum makhlooq ki khidmat is liye kar rahe ho taake Ulema wa Mashaikh ki majalis mein tumhara naam sar-aankhon par rakha jaye? Agar Trust ke jhande gira diye jayein aur logon ki zabanon se tumhara naam mita diya jaye, toh kya tumhare qadam is khidmat par tab bhi jame rahenge? Magar Shaitan ke is jaal se bacho: numayish ke darr se neki chhorna bhi ek bimari hai. Maidan mein raho, magar apne *Nafs* se laro. Dunya ke liye ki gayi pahaad bhar khairat raakh ho jati hai, magar sirf Allah ki raza ke liye ki gayi ek zarre barabar khidmat hamesha zinda rehti hai.

### Station 2: Amanah ka Khauf (Al-Amanah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Ay iman walo! Allah aur Uske Rasool ke sath khayanat na karo, aur na hi apni amanaton mein jaan boojh kar khayanat karo."* 
*(Surah Al-Anfal, 8:27)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Us shakhs mein koi iman nahi jis mein amanat-dari nahi, aur us mein koi deen nahi jo wada pura nahi karta."* 
*(Musnad Ahmad)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Jaan lo ke Trust ka sarmaya aur wasail tumhare liye koi saulat nahi, balki ek khaufnaak bojh hain. Tum Ummat ke *Bait-ul-Mal* (Awami Khazana) ke zimmedar ho. Zakat, Sadqa aur Lillah ka har woh rupaya jo tumhare hathon mein hai, agar usme khayanat hui toh woh Jahannum ka sulagta hua angara ban jayega.

Apni dosti aur itminan ko takabbur na banne dena. Agar tum Trust ki Amaanat ko, ya Trust ki kisi Ashya ko kisi zaati kaam ke liye istemal karte ho, agar tum zaati pasand-na-pasand ki bina par sarmaye ko kisi ese kaam ki taraf modte ho, ya yahan tak ke jab tumne Trust ke kaam ke liye apna waqt waqf kiya tha aur tum usey phone par zaya karte ho, toh tumne Allah ke ahad (covenant) ko tora hai. Trust se churaya hua waqt, ghareebon se churaya hua maal hai. Trust ke wasail se us khauf ke sath pesh ao jaise kisi sakht badshah ke khazane se pesh aate ho, kyunke tumhein har ek sikke, har ek qatre tel aur har ek minute ka hisab-kitab "Badshahon ke Badshah" ko dena hai.

### Station 3: Numayish ki Bimari (Riya)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Ay iman walo! Apni sadqat ko ehsan jata kar aur dukh de kar zaya na karo, us shakhs ki tarah jo apna maal sirf logon ko dikhane ke liye kharch karta hai..."* 
*(Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:264)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Mujhe tumhare liye sabse zyada darr 'shirk-e-asghar' (chhote shirk) ka hai."* Sahaba ne pucha, *"Ay Allah ke Rasool, woh kya hai?"* Aap ﷺ ne farmaya, *"Riya (dikhawa)."* 
*(Musnad Ahmad)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Is jadeed dour ki chhupi hui but-parasti se bacho. Jab tum toote hue aur mohtaj logon ke darmiyan khade hote ho, toh *Nafs* tumhare kaan mein phusphusata hai ke ek numayishi tasveer le lo, apne status par laga do, aur apni nekiyon ka ishtihar karo taake logon ke mobile ki screens tumhari parhezgari ki gawahi dein.

Mehboob-e-Kainaat ﷺ ki tanbeeh suno. Unhone isay *Shirk* kaha—Allah ke sath shareek karna. Jab tum amal toh Allah ke liye karte ho, magar umeed rakhte ho ke tumhare jaan-pehchan wale usey dekhein, toh tumne apne Rab ke mutlaq haq ko taqseem kar diya. *Riya* se aalooda amal us khoobsurat mahal ki tarah hai jo daldal par banaya gaya ho; woh lazmi doob jayega. Apni zaati nekiyon ko khamoshi ki zameen mein is tarah dafan kar do, jaise tum apne gunahon ko chhupate ho.

### Station 4: Hisab-Kitab ka Tawazun (Tawazun)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Agar tum khule aam sadqa karte ho toh yeh bahut achhi baat hai; aur agar ise chhupa kar ghareebon ko dete ho, toh yeh tumhare liye sabse behtar hai; aur Woh tumhare kuch gunah mita dega..."* 
*(Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:271)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Jisne Quran ko buland awaaz se padha woh uski tarah hai jisne khule aam sadqa kiya, aur jisne Quran ko khamoshi se padha woh uski tarah hai jisne chhupa kar sadqa kiya."*

Mulki qanoon aur sarmaya-daanon (donors) ka haq yeh hai ke rasmi tasveer, raseed aur records maujood hon. Yeh shfafiyat (transparency) ke liye zaruri hai. Magar dil ka qanoon mukammal poshidgi ka taqaza karta hai. Tum is kashmakash se kaise bachoge?

Us shakhs se seekho jo doosron ko sikhane ke liye Quran buland awaaz se padhta hai, magar uska dil tanhayi mein khauf se jhuka rehta hai. Jab Trust ke dastavezat (records) ke liye tumhe tasveer leni pade, toh apni haqiqat ko do hisson mein baant lo. Apne hath ko tasveer lene do taake sarmaya-daan ka haq pura ho, magar apne dil ko us tasveer se mukammal taur par alag kar lo. Jab camera ki flash chamke, toh tumhara dil *Istighfar* kare. Apne *Nafs* se kaho: *"Yeh record insanon ke hisab-kitab ke liye hai, magar meri rooh Allah ke Hisab se kanpti hai."* Idare ki intizamia (administration) mein zahir raho, magar apni zaati ibadat mein sakhti se poshida raho.

Sayyidah Ayesha (Radi Allahu Anha) jab mohtaj khandanon ko sadqa bhejti toh apne khadim se kehti ke woh un khandanon ki duayein lafz-ba-lafz yaad kar le. Aur agar tum is baat par gussa hote ho ke *Sa'il* na-shukra hai ya sakht lehje mein baat kar raha hai, toh yeh is baat ki daleel hai ke tum unki khidmat Allah ke liye nahi, balki apne *Nafs* ki tasalli ke liye kar rahe the. Sacha Khadim makhlooq ki tareefon se behra aur unki na-shukri se andha hota hai. Tum toh sirf ek dakiya (postman) ho jo *Ar-Razzaq* ka bheja hua rizq pahuncha raha hai. Kya dakiya us khat ki delivery par shukriya mangta hai jo uska apna tha hi nahi?

### Station 8: Sadqa Pehle Apne Ghar Walon ko (Huquq al-Ahl)

Ay Khadim! Yeh kitna ajeeb tazad (irony) hai ke ek shakhs galiyon mein toh masiha hai, magar apne ghar mein ek ajnabi. *Nafs* ko awami khidmat is liye pasand hai kyunke is se awami tareef milti hai. Magar maa ke tanhai mein baithne ka koi shohrat-nama nahi hota, thaki hui biwi ki madad karne ka koi tamgha nahi milta, aur bachon ko Quran sikhane ka koi stage nahi hota.

Ayat ki ilahi tarteeb ko dekho: maa-baap aur rishtedar, yateemon aur mohtajon se *pehle* aate hain. Agar tum Ummat ki khidmat mein aspatalon ke chakkar laga rahe ho, jabke tumhare apne maa-baap tumhare waqt ke liye taras rahe hain, toh tumhari khidmat ek sarab (mirage) hai. ### Station 9: Halal Rizq ka Adab (Rizq-e-Halal)

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne ek aise shakhs ka zikr kiya jo lambe safar se aaya, uske baal bikhre hue the aur woh dhool mein lipta hua tha, asman ki taraf hath uthaye keh raha tha, *'Ay Mere Rab, Ay Mere Rab!'* Magar uska khana haram tha, uska peena haram tha, uska libas haram tha, aur uski ghiza haram thi. *"Toh phir uski dua kaise qabool hogi?"* 
*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**

Agar tum bazaron mein dhoke-baazi, naap-tol mein kami, ya sood (Riba) ki gandagi se apni rozana ki roti kamate ho, aur phir shaam ko Trust mein aakar deeni wa samaji wa falahi khidmat anjam dete ho, toh tumne is khaym-e-khidmat mein ek rohani zeher ghola hai. Haram ghiza se parwarish pane wala jism un hathon ko nahi utha sakta jo asmanon ko cheer den. Ummat lahu-luhan hai, aur usey tumhari qabool hone wali duayon ki sakht zaroorat hai. Apne karobar ko pakiza karo, taake jab tum kisi musibat-zada ilaqe mein khade ho kar pukarona, *'Ay Mere Rab!'*, toh asman ke darwaze tumhare liye khul jayen.

### Station 10: Khamosh Khadim (Al-Khafi)

*(Surah Al-Kahf, 18:28)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**

*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim-e-Pichli-Saf! Yeh aakhri maqam tumhare liye hai. Tum woh ho jo mehfil khatam hone ke baad farsh saaf karte ho. Tum woh ho jo godam (warehouse) mein ration kit ke dabbo’n ko pack karte ho, jabke sarbarah stage par hote hain. Ho sakta hai ki *Intizamia* tumhara naam tak nahi janti, tumhein kabhi sar-meze laane ki dawat nahi milti, aur tumhari tasveer bhi social media mein kabhi nahi chapti.

Khush raho, kyunke tum Allah ke mehboob ho. Nabi ﷺ ko khud Allah ne hukm diya ke apni nazrein un logon par jame rakho jo tumhari tarah poshida hain—woh khamosh ibadat-guzaar jinhein samaj ki ameeri-aur-shohrat wale log nazar-andaz kar dete hain. Dunyawi pehchan ki kami se apne dil ko na torna. Is Hadith mein zikr ki gayi "be-niyazi" (Ghinah) bataue (wallet) ki amiri nahi hai; yeh us rooh ki amiri hai jo *Intizamia* se kisi bhi tawajjo ki umeed nahi rakhti. Arsh ke farishte tumhara naam mukammal taur par jante hain. Poshida raho, mukhlis raho, aur jaan lo ke Qayamat ke din, yeh khamosh khadim wohi rohani shohrat payega jo zameen ke badshahon ko bhi naseeb nahi hui.

### Station 11: Wali-e-Nemat aur Nazron ki Hifazat (Ghad al-Basar)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"...Tum unhein dekh kar samjhoge ke woh ghareeb hain kyunke woh sabr karte hain. Tum unhein unki nishani se pehchanoge, woh logon se zid kar ke nahi mangte..."* 
*(Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:273)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Mujhe apne kamzoron mein talash karo, kyunke tumhein rizq aur madad unhi kamzoron ki wajah se milti hai."* 
*(Sunan Abu Dawud)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay imdadi muhim ke Khadim! Rizq baantne se pehle apni nazar ko darust karo. Maidan-e-khidmat ka sabse bada fareb ghareeb ko "tarass" ki nazar se dekhna hai. Tarass woh jazba hai jo ek bada shakhs kisi chhote par karta hai. Khuda ki kasam, yahan tum chhote ho. Woh mohtaj khandan jo tumhare samne khada hai, woh tumhara *Wali-e-Nemat* (Ehsan karne wala) hai. Wohi woh wahid zariya hai jis ki wajah se Trust ko sarmaya milta hai, aur wohi woh pul hai jo tumhein Jahannum ki aag se bacha raha hai.

Jab tum basti ke kisi majboor baap ko rashan ka thaila thamao, toh apni nazrein neechi rakho. Uski aankhon mein mat dekho, kahin woh apni ghareebi ki zillat aur apni ghairat ke tootne ka zayqa na chakh le. Apne tarass ko "ehteram" mein badal do. Usey rizq is aajzi ke sath do jaise ek khadim apne aaqa ko unki amanat lautata hai. Agar tum bastiyon mein yeh soch kar jate ho ke tum "masiha" ho, toh tumne apna amal kho diya. Tum unhein bhook se nahi bacha rahe; balki woh tumhein Allah ke ghazab se bacha rahe hain.

### Station 12: Ehsan Jatane ki Mana'at (Al-Mann wal-Aza)

**Qurani Ayat:**

*(Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:262)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Teen log aise hain jinse Allah Qayamat ke din kalaam nahi karega, na unki taraf dekhega, aur na unhein pak karega... [unmein se ek] woh shakhs hai jo logon ko apne diye hue sadqe yaad dilata hai (Al-Mannan)."* 
*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Zaban ke is khauf se kanpo. Tum das saal tak ek khandan ki kafil raho, unke qarz utaro aur unke bachon ko khilao, magar ek takabbur bhara jumla tumhari das saal ki nekiyon ko ek pal mein raakh kar sakta hai.

Agar tumne kabhi kisi *Sa'il* se yeh kaha, *"Yaad hai pichle saal hamare Trust ne tumhari madad ki thi?"* ya tum yeh umeed rakhte ho ke woh tumhare aage jhuke kyunke tumne unhein imdad di hai, toh tumne *Al-Mann* (ehsan jatana) ka gunah kiya. Allah ne elaan farmaya hai ke woh Qayamat ke din aise shakhs ki taraf dekhega tak nahi. Woh sarmaya tumhara tha hi nahi; woh Allah ka tha, aur tum sirf ek dakiya the. Ek dakiya us tohfe par fakhr kaise kar sakta hai jo uska apna nahi tha? Apne honton ko band kar lo, ehsan ko usi lamhe bhool jao jab woh tumhare hath se nikle, aur Ummat ko kabhi apni khairat ka bhari aur dum ghutne wala bojh mehsoos na hone do.

### Station 13: Yateemon aur Bewaon ki Azmat (Kafalah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Pas yateem par sakhti na karo. Aur mangne wale ko jhidko mat."* 
*(Surah Ad-Duha, 93:9-10)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne apni shahadat wali ungli aur darmiyani ungli ko mila kar farmaya: *"Main aur yateem ki kafalat karne wala Jannat mein is tarah honge."* 
*(Sahih al-Bukhari)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Jab tum kisi ujre hue ghar mein yateem ki kafalat ka sarmaya dene jao, toh ise sirf ek daftari kaam na samjho. Tum Rasool-Allah ﷺ ke muqaddas mehmanon ke samne khade ho.

Yateem ki khidmat karna Nabi ﷺ ki mubarak ungliyon se ek seedha rohani talluq (*Nisbat*) paida karna hai. Us bachay ke paas mukammal haya aur dehshat ke sath jao. Magar yaad rakho: us gham-zada aur muhafiz bewa maa ko nazar-andaz kar ke bachay ko khilona na thamao. Bewa ki izzat utni hi muqaddas hai jitne yateem ke aansu. Unse aisi narmi se baat karo ke pahaad pighal jayein. Agar tumne us ghar mein sakhti se baat ki, ya unhein yeh ehsas dilaya ke woh Trust par ek bojh hain, toh tumne asmanon mein unke Asal Sarparast se jung ka elaan kar diya. Unki izzat ki hifazat karo, aur jaan lo ke unki khidmat Jannat mein Mehboob-e-Kainaat ﷺ ki sohbat ka sabse tez zariya hai.

### Station 14: Shifa ke Hath aur Muskurata Chehra (Shifa & Tabassum)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"...Aur jisne ek jaan bachayi, usne goya puri insaniyat ko bachaya."* 
*(Surah Al-Ma'idah, 5:32)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Allah Qayamat ke din farmayega: 'Ay bete Adam! Main beemar tha aur tune Meri ayadat nahi ki... Kya tujhe nahi maloom ke Mere is bande ki beemari mein Main uske sath tha?'"* 
*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay muft shifakhano, aspatalon aur khoon ki imdad ke Khadim! Is Hadith ko padho aur roo lo. Tum sirf dawaen nahi baant rahe ya bistar ka intezam nahi kar rahe; tum dar-asal Allah ki hazuri mein dakhil ho rahe ho. Ilahi Rahmat beemaron aur toote hue logon ke bistaron par nihayat shiddat se nazil hoti hai.

Jab tum ek muft shifakhane mein barah ghante apne paon par khade raho, jab tumhari kamar dukh rahi ho aur tumhara zehen mukammal thak chuka ho, toh *Nafs* tumhein hukm dega ke tum tewrao aur is shore-o-gul wale hujoom se sakhti se baat karo. Iska muqabla karo. Beemaron ke liye ek pur-noor aur narm-garm muskurahat paida karo. Tumhari dawa unke jism ko theek karti hai, magar tumhari narm muskurahat unke khauf ka bhi ilaaj karti hai. Jismani thakan ke bawajood mukammal *Adab* barkarar rakhna hi asal rohani jawari (*Futuwwa*) hai. Tum Ilahi Hazuri mein ho; yeh na hone dena ke farishte tumhein Allah ke mehmanon par tewraate hue likhein.

### Station 15: Zehno ki Roshni (Nur al-Ilm)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"...Allah tum mein se iman walon aur jinhein ilm diya gaya, unke darjaat buland farmayega..."* 
*(Surah Al-Mujadila, 58:11)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Yaqeenan, Allah, Uske farishte, asman aur zameen ke rehne wale, yahan tak ke apne bil ki chiyunti aur samandar ki machhli bhi us shakhs par durood bhejte hain jo logon ko bhalaayi sikhata hai."* 
*(Sunan at-Tirmidhi)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Taleem ke Khadim—chahe tum Madrasay mein chawk (chalk) pakde hue ho, ya Trust ke daftar mein baith kar wazaif / Scholarship ke kaghazat aur taleemi akhrajat ka hisab kar rahe ho—yeh na samjho ke tumhari khidmat kamtar hai kyunke tum khana nahi baant rahe. Pait bharna ek abehtareen sadqa hai, magar zehen ko Noor-e-Ilahi se bharna ek aisi virasat hai jo asmanon ko hila deti hai.

Jab tum kisi naujawan ko jahalat ki tareeki se bachane ke liye wazifa ka intezam karte ho, toh tum ek aisa azeem amal kar rahe hote ho ke samandar ki gehraiyon ki machhliyan aur zameen ki chiyuntiyan Allah se tumhari maghfirat ki dua karti hain. Ilm ka zariya banne wala shakhs wahi sawab pata hai jo ilm sikhane wale ko milta hai. Ek zehen ki tameer Mizan (scales) par deewar khadi karne se kahin zyada bhari hai. Woh har ek harf jo woh bacha padhega, aur har ek halal rupaya jo woh mustaqbil mein kamayega, tumhari qabr mein ek kabhi na khatam hone wali sawab ki nadi ban kar bahega.

### Station 16: Bachane wala aur Jodne wala (Ighathah & Islah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Momin toh aapas mein bhai bhai hain, pas apne bhaiyon ke darmiyan sulah (Islah) karao. Aur Allah se daro taake tum par raham kiya jaye."* 
*(Surah Al-Hujurat, 49:10)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Kya main tumhein kisi aisi cheez ki khabar na doon jo roze, namaz aur sadqe se behtar hai?"* Sahaba ne arz kiya: *"Ji haan, ay Allah ke Rasool!"* Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Do logon ke darmiyan sulah karana (Islah Dhat al-Bayn)..."* 
*(Sunan Abu Dawud)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Trust do samno (fronts) par kaam karta hai: ek woh musibaten jo zahir hoti hain, aur ek woh jo poshida (hidden) hoti hain. Jab sailaab aata hai, toh tum doobte hue logon ko pani se nikalne ke liye daurte ho. Yeh jismani nijat (*Ighathah*) ek azeem saadat hai. Magar un khamosh zalzalon ko nazar-andaz na karo jo muashray (society) ko andar se tod rahe hain.

Tute hue rishton ko jodne (*Islah*) mein ek aisi aala khairat hai jise aksar bhool diya jata hai. Jab tum kisi ghar ko tootne se bachane ke liye sulah-kar bante ho, jab tum bazaar mein kisi tijarati ikhtilaf ko khatam karte ho, ya jab tum do bhaiyon ko virasat ki ladaee se nikal kar ek karte ho, toh tum woh amal kar rahe hote ho jise Nabi ﷺ ne nafal roze aur namaz se zyada bhari qarar diya. Ek jism ko sailaab se bachana badi neki hai, magar ek khandan ya ek basti ko nafrat se bachana dar-asal Ummat ki hifazat hai. Us suee (needle) ki tarah bano jo muashray ke phate hue libas ko dobara se si (stitch) de.

### Station 17: Khud-Kifayati ka Tohfa (Tamkeen)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"...taake yeh maal tumhare amiron ke darmiyan hi na ghumta rahe..."* 
*(Surah Al-Hashr, 59:7)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Kisi ne us khane se behtar khana nahi khaya jo usne apne hathon ki mehnat se kamaya ho. Aur Allah ke Nabi Dawud (A.S) apne hathon ki kamayi se khate the."* 
*(Sahih al-Bukhari)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Rozgar ke Khadim! Tumhara farz hai ke tum "sirf zinda rakhne wali khairat" se nikal kar "izzat-e-nafs wali khairat" ki taraf jao. Jab tum ek bewa ko silai machine dete ho ya kisi majboor baap ko karobar ke liye chota sarmaya dete ho, toh tum unhein azadi aur khud-kifayati (*Tamkeen*) ka aala tohfa de rahe hote ho.

Magar "masiha hone ke waham" se bacho. Yeh umeed na rakho ke jis shakhs ko tumne qabil banaya, woh zindagi bhar Trust ka "numayishi chehra" bana rahega. Yeh umeed na rakho ke woh hamesha muashre mai khada ho kar tumhara aur sarmaya-daanon ke liye shukrane ke aansu bahayega. Jab tum unhein apne paon par khada kar do, toh unhein mukammal azad kar do. Unka tum par koi ehsan nahi; unki kamyabi sirf Allah ki hai. Trust ki asal jeet isme nahi hai ke hamari khairat lene walon ki tadad badhe, balki isme hai ke hum unhein is muqam tak le jayein ke woh hamari zaroorat se azad ho kar chale jayein.

### Station 18: Tehqiq aur Parda-poshi ka Fiqh (Sitr)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Beshak woh log jo yeh pasand karte hain ke iman walon ke darmiyan behayayi phailay, unke liye duniya aur Aakhirat mein dardnak azab hai..."* 
*(Surah An-Nur, 24:19)*

*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay maidani tehqiq (survey) ke Khadim! Tumhare kandhon par ek khaufnaak dohra bojh hai. Tumhein khandanon ki chhan-bin ke liye bheja jata hai taake Trust ki Zakat haq-daron tak pahunche. Magar kya hota hai jab tum kisi darwaze par dastak dete ho aur wahan koi chhupa hua aib ya sharam-naak raaz khul jata hai? Kya ho agar tumhein maloom ho ke baap jua-baz hai, nasha-parast hai, ya usne darkhwast (application) mein jhoot bola hai?

Yahan tumhein *Sitr* (parda-poshi) ke sakht Fiqh par amal karna hoga. Tumhein Trust ke sarmaye ko us nasha-parast baap se bachana hai, magar tumhein un masoom bachon ko nahi chhorna. Hikmat se kaam lo: baap ko bypass karo, rashan seedha maa ke hath mein do taake khandan bhookha na rahe, aur us baap ke raaz ko apne dil mein hamesha ke liye dafan kar do. Agar tum apne dosto ya rishtedaaro mai us khandan ke gunahon ki ghibat karte ho, toh tumne apne upar Allah ki laanat ko dawat di hai. Ummat ke aibon ko mukammal andhere mein lapet do. Sarmaye ki hifazat karo, bhoonko ko khilao, aur kisi khandan ki izzat ko kabhi nanga na karo.

### Station 19: Alam-geer Rahmat (Rahmatan lil-Alameen)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Aur Humne aapko (ay Nabi ﷺ) tamam jahanon ke liye rahmat bana kar bheja."* 
*(Surah Al-Anbiya, 21:107)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Ek shakhs ne kuen se apne jootay mein pani bhar kar ek pyase kutte ko pilaya. *"Allah ne uske is amal ki qadar ki aur usay maaf kar diya."* Sahaba ne pucha, *"Kya janwaron ki khidmat par bhi sawab hai?"* Farmaya, *"Har zinda jandar ki khidmat mein sawab hai."* 
*(Sahih al-Bukhari)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Tum ek aise muashray mein kaam kar rahe ho jahan mukhtalif aqidat ke log rehte hain. Agar tumhari raham-dili sirf un logon tak mahdood hai jo tumhare deen par hain, toh tumne apne Nabi ﷺ ki azmat ko nahi samjha. Aap ﷺ sirf Musalmanon ke liye nahi, balki *Al-Alameen* (tamam jahanon) ke liye rahmat bana kar bheje gaye the.

Tumhara insani farz ek azeem ibadat hai. Jab tum raste se koi takleef-deh cheez hatate ho, jab tum kisi awara janwar ko pani pilate ho, ya jab tum apni imdadi koshishon ko kisi gair-muslim padosi tak pahunchate ho, toh tumhari raham-dili **be-shart** honi chahiye. Unki khidmat kisi len-den ki niyat se na karo. Hum bhooke padosi ko is umeed mein nahi khilate ke woh badle mein kuch karega; hum usey sirf is liye khilate hain kyunke Rab-ul-Alameen ne humein insaniyat ki izzat karne ka hukm diya hai. Tumhara be-daagh kirdar *Ar-Rahman* ka aaina hona chahiye, bina kisi shart aur bina kisi lalach ke.

### Station 20: Aala Muqam (Ru'yatul Khaliq fil Khalq)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Aur woh kisi ko badle ki umeed mein ehsan nahi karta, balki sirf apne Rab-e-Ali ke chehre (raza) ki talash mein hota hai. Aur jald woh razi ho jayega."* 
*(Surah Al-Lail, 92:19-21)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**

*(Sunan at-Tirmidhi)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Jab tum maidan-e-khidmat ke aakhir mein khade ho, aur tumhara jism khidmat ki dhool se lipta ho, toh apne hathon ko dekho. Tum dar-asal kar kya rahe ho?

Rohani jawari (*Futuwwa*) ka aala muqam yeh ehsas hai ke makhlooq ke sath har ek muamla dar-asal Khaliq ke sath ek len-den hai. Jab tum ek yateem ke aansu ponchte ho, toh tum dar-asal Allah se iltija kar rahe hote ho ke Qayamat ke din Woh tumhare aansu ponch de. Jab tum bewa ko khilate ho, toh tum *Ar-Razzaq* se bheek mang rahe hote ho ke Woh tumhein Jannat ke phalon se khilaye. Tum makhlooq ki khidmat makhlooq ke liye nahi kar rahe; tum makhlooq ki khidmat apne Rab-e-Aali ki raza ke liye kar rahe ho. Dunya ko phika parne do. Makhlooq ki khidmat mein apne Khaliq ko talash karo, aur jald hi, jaisa ke Allah ne wada kiya hai, tumhara dil mukammal taur par mutmain aur razi ho jayega.

## STAGE 3: AL-TANZIM (Intizamia aur Bhaichara)
*Shakhon (branches) ki raqabat, qayadat ke gharoor aur idarati siyasat ki bimariyon ka ilaaj.*

### Station 21: Khidmat ka Bhaichara (Ukhuwwah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Aur Allah ki rassi ko sab mil kar mazbooti se thaam lo aur tafarruqa (division) mein na pado. Aur Allah ke us ehsan ko yaad karo jab tum dushman the aur Usne tumhare dilon ko mila diya..."* 
*(Surah Al-Imran, 3:103)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Momin apne aapsi raham, shafqat aur hamdardi mein ek jism ki tarah hain. Jab jism ke ek hisse mein takleef hoti hai, toh pura jism be-khwabi aur bukhar se jawab deta hai."* 
*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! "Shakh-parasti" (Branch Nationalism) ki bimari se bacho. Kya tumhara dil tang hota hai jab koi dusri shakh tumhare shehar ke kisi sarmaya-daan se imdad jama karti hai? Kya tum padosi shakhon ko dushman idaron ki tarah dekhte ho jo tumhara "hissa" chura rahe hain?

Yeh ilaqai takabbur *Nafs* ki ek bimari hai. Sarmaya-daan tumhare nahi hain, ghareeb tumhare nahi hain, aur yeh ilaqah tumhari shakh ki jageer nahi hai; yeh sab Rab-ul-Alameen ka hai. Trust ek wahid, saans leta hua rohani jism hai. Agar Shakh-A, Shakh-B ke shehar ke sarmaye se ek behtareen imdadi muhim chalati hai, toh Khadim ko chahiye ke woh sajde mein gir jaye aur is jama'i kamyabi par Allah ka shukr ada kare. "Hum aur Woh" wali zehniyat ko khatam karo, kyunke ilaqai taqseem pure Trust se Ilahi Barakah ko cheen leti hai.

### Station 22: Hasad ka Zeher (Hasad)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Ya phir woh logon se hasad karte hain us cheez par jo Allah ne unhein apne fazl se di hai?..."* 
*(Surah An-Nisa, 4:54)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Hasad se bacho, kyunke hasad nekiyon ko is tarah kha jata hai jaise aag lakdi ko kha jati hai."* 
*(Sunan Abu Dawud)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Tumhare dil mein kya hota hai jab kisi sathi khadim ko stage par bulaya jata hai aur uski tareef hoti hai, jabke tumhein nazar-andaz kiya jata hai? Kya hota hai jab kisi dusri shakh ko unke project ke liye bada sarmaya diya jata hai, jabke tumhari shakh ko intezar karne ko kaha jata hai?

Agar tumhare seene mein koi ranjish paida hoti hai, toh samajh lo ke tum *Hasad* ki bimari mein mubtala ho chuke ho. Hasad ek sulagti hui aag hai jo us shakhs ko nahi jalati jisse tum hasad karte ho, balki tumhein khud jalati hai. Tum das saal tak sailaabon ke pani mein utar sakte ho aur Ummat ke liye apni neendein qurban kar sakte ho, magar apne bhai ke liye dil mein paida hone wala ek zarra hasad tumhari un tamam raaton ki ibadat ko raakh kar dega. Sarmaya, izzat aur kamyabi baantne wala sirf Allah hai. Apne bhai se hasad karna dar-asal khamoshi se Allah ke taqseem karne ke faisle par aitraz karna hai. Apne dil ko pakiza karo. Apne bhai ki bulandi par khush ho, tabhi tumhare amaal mehfooz rahenge.

### Station 23: Ita'at aur Naseehah (Obedience & Advice)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Ay iman walo! Allah ki ita'at karo aur Rasool ki, aur unki jo tum mein se ikhtiyar (authority) wale hain..."* 
*(Surah An-Nisa, 4:59)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Deen khulus (Naseehah) hai... Allah ke liye, Uske Kitab ke liye, Uske Rasool ke liye, Musalmanon ke amiron ke liye, aur unke aam logon ke liye."* Aur Aap ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Jisne Ameer ki ita'at ki, usne meri ita'at ki."* 
*(Sahih Muslim / Sahih al-Bukhari)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay maidani Khadim! Tum naseehat karne ki himmat aur farmanbardari ki aajzi ke darmiyan ek bareek lakeer par chal rahe ho. Agar tum dekhte ho ke Ameer (Branch Head) koi badi tadbeeri galti kar raha hai, toh tumhare liye khamosh tamashai bane rehna jayez nahi. Tumhara deeni farz hai ke tum nihayat adab ke sath, tanhayi mein aur pur-khulus andaz mein *Naseehah* (sachi naseehat) pesh karo.

Magar jab mashwara khatam ho jaye aur Ameer apna aakhri faisla suna de, toh tumhara farz mukammal ittehad aur amal mein badal jata hai. Agar tumhein lagta hai ke tumhari hikmat-e-amli zyada behtar thi, tab bhi baghawat aur piche se baatein karna Trust ke rohani qile ko tabah kar deta hai. Tum Ameer ki ita'at is liye nahi karte ke woh masoom ya be-khata hai; balki tum ita'at is liye karte ho taake *Jama'ah* (collective body) ka ittehad barkarar rahe. Band darwazon ke piche sher ki tarah naseehat karo, magar aakhri hukm par ek farmanbardar bande ki tarah amal karo.

### Station 24: Mashwarat aur Amanah (Shura & Amanah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"...aur unse mashwara karo. Phir jab tum faisla kar lo, toh Allah par tawakkul karo. Beshak, Allah un logon se mohabbat karta hai jo Us par tawakkul karte hain."* 
*(Surah Al-Imran, 3:159)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Jis shakhs ne kisi ko ek giroh par muqarrar kiya, jabke un mein koi dusra shakhs Allah ki nazron mein zyada pasandida tha, usne Allah, Uske Rasool aur momineen ke sath khayanat ki."* 
*(Al-Hakim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Ameer! Ay shakh ke sarbarah! Qayadat koi sultani ya dictatorship nahi hai; balki yeh aapsi mashwarat (*Shura*) ka ek bhari bojh hai. Magar "nam-o-nishaan ki mashwarat" (Rubber-Stamp Shura) ke munafiqat se bacho—yani apne khadimon ko sirf is liye jama karna ke tum unki baat sunte hue nazar ao, jabke tumne faisla pehle hi apne chand khas logon ke sath mil kar kar liya ho. Shura koi numayishi dikhawa nahi hai; balki yeh ek muqaddas aajzi hai ke tum apne zehen ko ek aam ground worker ki hikmat se badalne ki ijazat do.

Mazeed yeh ke, muqarrari (appointments) ke Fiqh se kanpo. Agar tum apne kisi dost, apni biradari ke shakhs, ya apne rishtedar ko kisi committee mein bithate ho, jabke tumhare samne ek aisa Khadim maujood hai jo zyada parhezgar, qabil aur Allah ki nazron mein zyada pasandida hai, toh tumne idarati khayanat ki hai. Rishtedaari-pasandi (Nepotism) Allah aur Uske Rasool ﷺ ke sath dhoka hai. Trust Ummat ki amanat hai. Sirf qabiliyat aur taqwa ki bina par muqarrar karo, warna Qayamat ke din is khayanat ka jawab dene ke liye tayyar raho.

***

### Station 25: Ohde ki Talab ka Khatra (Talab al-Imarah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Aakhirat ka ghar Hum unke liye muqarrar karte hain jo zameen par gharoor aur fasad nahi chahte. Aur behtareen anjam nek logon ke liye hai."* 
*(Surah Al-Qasas, 28:83)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Ay Abdur-Rahman! Ohde ki talab na karo, kyunke agar tumhein tumhari talab par ohda mila, toh tum usay apne bharose par chalaoge (Allah ki madad nahi milegi). Magar agar tumne talab nahi ki aur tumhein diya gaya, toh Allah tumhari madad farmayega."* 
*(Sahih al-Bukhari)*

**Tafakkur:**

*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Ameer! Ay Khazanchi! Ay Khadim! Tum hisab ki sakhti se achi tayyari karte ho. Tum sijilat (ledgers) ko darust karte ho taake sarmaya-daan razi rahein. Magar us "Aakhri Hisab" ka kya?

Tasawwur karo ke tum Qayamat ke din pahunche ho, tumhare sath khairat ke pahad hain, imdadi kaam hain, aur madrason ki funding hai. Magar kyunke tumne WhatsApp group mein kisi sathi khadim ki ghibat ki thi, ya tumne kisi yateem par chillaya tha, ya kisi bewa ki izzat ko thais pahunchayi thi—Allah tumhari un tamam idarati nekiyon ko utha kar tumhare mazloomon ko de dega. Tumhare kaghazat toh saaf the, magar tumhara kirdar muflis tha. Insanon ke hisab o kitaab se pehle Asmanon ke hisab kitab ki tayyari karo. Apni zaban aur apne gusse ki hifazat karo, warna aisa na ho ke tumne apni khairat se Jannat mein mahal toh khada kar liya, magar apne takabbur ki wajah se woh mahal kisi aur ke naam kar diya.

### Station 39: Awliya ka Rasta (Sunnat al-Awliya / Nisbat)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Beshak Allah ke doston (Awliya) ko na koi khauf hoga aur na woh ghamgeen honge."* 
*(Surah Yunus, 10:62)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Allah ke nazdeek sabse mehboob insan woh hai jo logon ke liye sabse zyada nafah-bakhsh (beneficial) ho..."* 
*(Al-Mu'jam al-Awsat)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Yeh na samjho ke *Wilayat* ka rasta sirf unke liye hai jo ghaaron mein tanhai ikhtiyar karte hain ya puri zindagi masjid ke ek kone mein guzarte hain. *Ahle Sunnah* ki azeem riwayaton mein, sabse bade Awliya—Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti se lekar Data Ganj Bakhsh tak—sirf namaz nahi padhte the; unhone bhookon ko khilaya, yateemon ko panah di, aur toote hue dilon ke aansu ponche.

Trust mein tumhari khidmat rohani raste se koi hat-kar kaam nahi hai; balki yeh khud ek *Tariqah* (Suluk) hai. Jab tum kisi *Sa'il* ki galiyon ko bardasht karte ho, toh yeh ek rohani mashq hai. Jab tum muft shifakhane ka farsh saaf karte ho, toh yeh rooh ki pakeezgi hai. Apne dil ko un nek salaf ki rohani *Nisbat* se jodo. Tum un Buzurgon ke qadmon ke nishanon par chal rahe ho. Makhlooq ki khidmat nihayat *Adab* ke sath karo, aur Khaliq tumhein apne mehboob Awliya ke darjaat tak pahunchayega.

### Station 40: Aakhri Mulakat (Liqa' Allah)

**Qurani Ayat:**
*"Us din kuch chehre roshan honge, apne Rab ko dekhte hue."* 
*(Surah Al-Qiyamah, 75:22-23)*

**Hadith-e-Mubaraka:**
Rasool-Allah ﷺ ne farmaya: *"Jab Jannati Jannat mein dakhil ho jayenge, toh Allah farmayega, 'Kya tumhein kuch aur chahiye?' Woh kahenge, 'Kya Aapne hamare chehre roshan nahi kiye? Kya Aapne humein Jannat mein dakhil nahi kiya aur aag se nahi bachaya?' Phir Woh parda uthayega, aur unhein usse zyada pyari koi cheez nahi lagegi ke woh apne Rab ka deedar karein."* 
*(Sahih Muslim)*

**Tafakkur:**
Ay Khadim! Yahan yeh safar khatam hota hai. Shaikh Ul Islam Trust se aage dekho. In Khidmati wa Falahi wa Samaaji kaamoon se aage dekho. Is puri dunya se aage dekho. Tumne woh raaton ka jaagna kyun saha? Jab *Intizamia* ne tumhein ohde se hataya, toh tumne apna gharoor kyun nigla? Jab tumhara apna jism toot raha tha, tab tumne beemaron ke liye muskurahat kyun paida ki?

Yeh kabhi kisi idare (NGO) ke liye nahi tha. Yeh kabhi sirf Ummat ke liye bhi nahi tha. Yeh hamesha, sirf aur sirf, *Us* (Allah) ke liye tha.

Tumhari khidmat ka aala inaam logon ki tareef nahi, na hi sirf Jannat ki nehren hain. Tumhare paseene ke har qatre ka aakhri aur sabse haseen maqsad woh lamha hai jab "Parda" uthaya jayega. Us abadi baghon mein khade hokar, apna sar uthana, aur Rab-ul-Alameen ke Noorani Chehre ka deedar karna—yahi asal kamyabi hai. Dunya ko jalne do. Ohdon ko mitne do. Ay Khadim, apni nazrein us ufaq (horizon) par jame rakho, aur tab tak chalo jab tak tum apne Rab se na mil lo.
